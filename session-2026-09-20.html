traveller sota session Secrets of the Ancients

Session date: 2026-09-20 In-fiction date: roughly 212–218-1105 — mid-way through the third jump, Whanga → Alell (approximate; the table didn’t track days, only jumps)

GM’s framing: the three-week transit to Alell, one random event per jump — the three D100s rolled last session paid out one at a time. StClair’s player was absent, but the GM let the crew “use him once” (he was used three times). Ozy’s player joined from a car for the first half.

Starting position

Two or three days into the first jump, Regina → Forboldn, with five high passengers aboard and Talia asking for help with the Helix / Black Horizon row over a joint discovery. Kostas in the medbay recovering from surgery.

Live notes
  • Training. Eight weeks per skill; three jumps to Alell means three weeks banked if nothing interrupts. Cassius settled on Investigate after wavering towards Persuade. Ozy took Persuade — “more within the line of my character”. The GM barred Kostas from training in week one — “not while recovering in the medbay” — Kostas is training Streetwise. Using a skill at the table still doesn’t count
  • The Helix / Black Horizon dispute. Helix’s equipment and Black Horizon’s research both went into a breakthrough; each claims it. Not yet disturbing the passengers, “but it will eventually”. Talia at her wits’ end
    • Ozy read the contracts. Helix: genuinely honest, painfully bureaucratic, red tape everywhere; their terms give the ship a 2% royalty capped at Cr1,000,000. Black Horizon: secretive, encrypt everything, claim exclusive patent rights on any breakthrough and pay no royalties — and that’s in the contract the crew signed
    • Ozy’s carrier-clause idea from last session was a bluff (“I’m just saying, if I kept notes”). Cassius tried “this is above your pay grade, refer it to head office” — the reps are empowered to settle everything for their teams and threw that out. They wanted it sorted then and there
    • The plan became turn a qualitative argument into a quantitative one: pull the logs and data packets and work out each side’s percentage contribution. The GM asked whether they’d fake the numbers; Kostas (delirious in the medbay, and the only one with the spreadsheet) said don’t, the ship needs the research income. Ozy considered keeping a personal backup of the data; Black Horizon’s encryption would have raised the difficulty, so he dropped it. Difficulty stayed at 8
    • Ozy, Electronics (Computers) +7, rolled 5 → 12, Effect +4. The GM let him choose the outcome. He chose Black Horizon in the right — “if Black Horizon are the more dodgy, it’s easier if they’re in the right, because it’s easier to convince Helix to back down”. The records show the discovery was Black Horizon’s, “pretty much bang on”
    • Result: Black Horizon keep the discovery, exclusive patent, no royalties to anyone. Helix are pissed off but bound by the contract. Talia is happy — it’s off her plate. And Kay, Black Horizon’s rep, is very happy with Ozy: she thinks he looks like one of her exes
  • Ozy and Kay. Ozy spent part of the week chatting Kay up — “a very boring corporate-to-corporate argument for getting laid”, PowerPoint implied. The GM ruled it costs him a couple of days of training for the week. The roll succeeded; the scene went to fade to black and “went down well”. The GM promised an animation
  • Kostas’s recovery. Five days of care needed; by the end of the first jump he had a day or two spare. Coffees resumed
  • Arrival at Forboldn. Uneventful. Skimmed the gas giant — jump-2 means refuelling every leg
  • Jump 2, Forboldn → Whanga (two parsecs). Cassius ran the ship computer: first astrogation attempt was rolled at the wrong difficulty on Roll20 and redone — Effect +1 either way; jump drive roll +7. One week. Nobody remarked that Kostas, the ship’s astrogator, wasn’t there
  • Event: the smell. A few days in, a smell spread through the ship — different for everyone, always the best smell of your childhood: burning rubber, candy floss, fresh-cut grass. It didn’t fade. Everything, food and drink included, tasted of it. Both research companies: “not us”. Left alone it would have imposed Bane dice on everything done aboard
    • Kostas reconned the ventilation and found nothing
    • StClair, dragged from his lab “hair even crazier than normal, I can’t work like this”, flew a repair drone through the vents on Remote Ops (11, Effect +3) and found a blob of pulsating goo in the ducts, growing. A Medic check: it’s emitting a pheromone that overrides the sense of smell. “You’re all breathing in alien blob jizz”
    • The GM prompted a headcount of the passengers. Talia (Steward +3, rolled 8) went round the staterooms: one passed out drunk; one quietly keeping to their room since Regina; one rich noble twat haunting the ship like he owns it; one overly friendly, chatting to everyone and “seeing how things work”. The fifth door: a scream, and on the floor an empty skin suit
    • “How could it possibly be one of our passengers? We checked them so carefully”
    • Ozy rigged a sensor on Computers (“I’m basically trying to invent the scanner in Alien” — it goes beep): the goo’s DNA matches the inside of the skin suit. One of the five high passengers is the blob. The GM: this is what the D66 table rolled for the passengers — “it’s funny because one of them turned out to be an alien”
    • Killing it would forfeit its Cr60,000 passage. StClair on Science (his only science is Psionicology; a plain Science check, 9, Effect +1): the voyage coincided with the alien’s reproductive cycle and the pheromone is a mating call. It would last a few more days. Options: shut off ventilation around it or wait it out
    • The crew sealed the ventilation around it, no roll needed. Back in its skin suit it was sheepish, apologised (“won’t happen again” — it can talk in the suit, nobody knows how) and hid in its stateroom for the rest of the trip. No lasting effects; “everyone’s horny”
  • Arrival at Whanga. Skimmed the gas giant. Two weeks of training banked for Cassius and Ozy; Kostas’s first week (he’s a week behind). Kostas is back to full and out of the medbay
  • Jump 3, Whanga → Alell (one parsec). Cassius: astrogation Effect +2. Same jump duration as always
  • Event: containment breach. Peaceful, eventless — until alarms rang through the ship and a Black Horizon security detail turned up on the bridge. Specimens they’d brought aboard — under the contract clause that says the crew don’t get to look at what they bring — have escaped into the maintenance shafts. “They’re not tribbles this time.” Kostas: our maintenance shafts seem to be exposed to everything
    • The specimens are juvenile carthuses: tall, pale cave-dwellers from deep desert, four limbs, a spine so flexible they dislocate joints at will and worm through cracks; hide in the dark and hunt from it; normally suck the moisture out of fungus and moss, “but obviously it’s found different prey on this ship”. Three, maybe four, possibly more — Black Horizon’s count was vague (“a very slapdash operation”). Acid for blood was asked as a joke and got a “yeah” — treat as a joke
    • Black Horizon want them back alive, and “appreciate” means money. Stun them. The crew own no stunners; the Black Horizon security officer with them has one, StClair has a stun stick, Ozy has explosives (please not in the vents). Gunnerson is manning the guns
    • Marching order into the vents: security officer (stunner first), Ozy (“corporate bother boy, expendable, like in Aliens”), Kostas, Cassius at the rear. Glow sticks out; the GM’s lighting shows only the area around them
    • The first one came from behind, at Cassius. Initiative. Ozy shot it first — no armour, big damage, and the GM warned that another hit like that would kill it. It leapt on Cassius (attack 11, hit; bite through his Protection 10 armour for 3 END). Cassius looked for a reaction — dodge (DEX DM 0, Athletics 0), parry (Melee 1, total 1), his thruster pack — and the GM ruled nothing applied; movement isn’t a reaction. Cassius held, the security officer squeezed past and missed with the stunner. Kostas’s turn: “it looks like it’s on the verge of death, and so am I, and that fucking idiot’s just missed it. Shoot the fucker” — instead Kostas took StClair’s stun stick (2D6−1, no modifier), got in front of the Admiral and stunned it in one. “You have a snoozing alien in front of you.” The security officer was impressed: “more of that would be appreciated”
    • Black Horizon are sending a team to collect it. New marching order: Kostas at the rear. The GM needs to work out where the rest are — paused here, mid-hunt, one down
Decisions and discoveries
  • The Helix / Black Horizon row is settled in Black Horizon’s favour on Ozy’s Effect +4 Computers roll — his choice, made because a dodgy company in the right is easier to enforce than an honest one in the wrong. Black Horizon: exclusive patent, no royalties. Helix: bound, and sour. The crew gain nothing from the discovery; what it actually is was never asked
  • The two charter contracts are now known: Helix pays the ship a 2% royalty capped at Cr1,000,000 on breakthroughs; Black Horizon pays no royalties and claims exclusive patents; and Black Horizon’s contract forbids the crew inspecting their cargo — which is how live specimens came aboard unseen
  • Kay is Black Horizon’s rep aboard, and Ozy is involved with her. The Helix rep’s name was heard as “Rock Anlo” — spelling unconfirmed, no note yet
  • One of the five high passengers is a shapeshifting alien — a blob in a skin suit, of a species nobody named, in its mating season. Harmless, apologetic, still paying. The Five High Passengers are now mostly characterised: the drunk, the recluse, the noble, the friendly one, and the blob
  • Black Horizon brought live carthuses aboard and lost them into the shafts during the third jump. One stunned, two or three or more loose. Cassius bitten for 3 END
  • Training: Cassius Investigate, Ozy Persuade — two weeks each by Whanga (Ozy’s flirting may have cost him days of week one; the GM counted two anyway). Kostas Streetwise, one week
  • Rules settled: no training while in the medbay; movement/pressing a button isn’t a reaction; dodge uses DEX DM or Athletics, parry uses Melee, neither reduces damage — they penalise the attack roll; the security officer counts as a stat-block NPC the GM rolled up on the spot (Protection 6)
End position
  • In the third jump, Whanga → Alell, days from arrival, in the maintenance shafts hunting escaped carthuses with a Black Horizon security officer and one stun stick between them. One stunned and awaiting collection; the rest loose and roused
  • Cassius at END −3 from the bite. Kostas fully recovered from surgery, carrying StClair’s stun stick, at the rear
  • Marching order: security officer, Ozy, Cassius, Kostas
  • Cr300,000 in passage fees still due on arrival — all five passengers alive, one of them a blob
  • Helix nursing a grievance; Black Horizon in the crew’s debt twice over (the ruling, and the specimen)
  • Harlen, Exceedingly Discreet, Detective Odoy, Vlen’s body, Boughene, Juni on Beck’s World — all untouched this session
Priorities for next session
  • Finish the carthus hunt — stun, don’t kill; count them properly; nobody go alone
  • Land at Alell: collect Cr300,000, pay the month, and start on Vlen’s family and his burial
  • Ask Black Horizon what else is in their cargo — the contract says the crew can’t look, but two incidents in three weeks is grounds for a conversation
  • What was the discovery? Nobody asked. And what Black Horizon “appreciate” translates to in credits
  • Cassius’s END, StClair’s stun stick back, and buying stunners at the first port that sells them
  • Third week of training if the hunt doesn’t eat it
  • Still open, further away now: Harlen, Exceedingly Discreet, Darrian Jak in prison, Detective Odoy, The Device, Vlen’s body, Boughene, Juni
