*An animal, not a sophont.*

A winged snake with a spiked tail. Extremely effective predators: they attack by **hurling themselves down on prey from a great height**, embedding their tail spikes deep into the target's flesh, then thrashing wildly and snapping the victim's spine.

Cadgeree usually attack smaller creatures, but have been known to attack humans when hungry.
