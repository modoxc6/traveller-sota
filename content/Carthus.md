[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

Tall, pale creature that dwells in deep desert caves. Four limbs and an **extremely flexible spine — it dislocates its joints at will and worms through cracks and crevices**. Hides in dark surroundings and hunts from them. Normally sucks the moisture out of fungus and moss; will take other prey when it's available. Bites: one got through Protection 10 armour for 3 END.

**Juvenile carthuses were among the specimens [[Black Horizon]] brought aboard the [[Event Horizon]]** under a contract clause that stopped the crew inspecting their cargo. They escaped into the maintenance shafts during the jump from [[Whanga]] to [[Alell]] — "three, maybe four, possibly more". Black Horizon want them back alive and will pay for it; stunners recommended. No armour of their own; a single rifle hit from [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] nearly killed one, and [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] put it down with a stun stick.

**What they were** (per [[Kay]]): cryogenically frozen embryos that came into contact with [[Black Horizon's Growth Serum]] by accident and **grew instantly**, which is why they were juveniles and weaker than adults. The rest of the embryo samples have been destroyed.

**Hunt over, [[Session 2026-09-27]].** Seven in all: four killed, three stunned and taken into Black Horizon's containment. Two broke into the control room and tore into the ship's systems. At full strength they bite hard: 16, 13, 10 and 19 were rolled against the crew. One put [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] unconscious through armour 5, and another took a chunk out of [[Pedro]]'s shoulder.

"Acid for blood" was a joke at the table that got a "yeah" — not treated as fact.

Name as heard; spelling unconfirmed (GM's stat block not seen).
