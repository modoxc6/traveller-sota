**[[Black Horizon]]'s representative aboard the [[Event Horizon]]** — empowered to settle any matter concerning their research team, which is why "refer it to head office" didn't work on her. The GM plays her with a cat picture.

Came out on top of the [[Helix]] / Black Horizon ownership row in the first jump out of [[Regina]] when [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]]'s analysis of the logs put the discovery squarely with Black Horizon. She thinks Ozy looks like one of her exes; he spent part of the week chatting her up, the roll succeeded, and it went to fade to black.

Name heard as Kay / K; spelling unconfirmed.
