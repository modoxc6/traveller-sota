**Zed Cole**, rolled from the GM's name tables in [[Session 2026-10-18]]. Heard on the recording as "Zed Cole"; spelling per Rich.

He was **the friendly one** of the [[Five High Passengers]]: overly friendly, chatting to everyone, "seeing how things work". He took the beam laser for one salvo against [[Delta 2]], untrained. **Now crew of the [[Event Horizon]]**, voted in while [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] was away.

###### As rolled (Core Rulebook p. 92, Quick Characters)
- **Experienced Combatant**: the GM gave him **+2 on gun skills**, and **+1 piloting small craft**, not the lab ship. The book's Experienced Combatant gets Drive/Flyer, Gun Combat, Heavy Weapons, Melee and Recon at around level 2, and characteristic bonuses of +1, +2
- **Quirk: "has secret allies"**. The GM first read "in debt to criminals", then corrected it after a re-roll. He said the quirk is out-of-game knowledge, and that it's "interesting" but he has no time to build the subplot yet

###### Pay
The Admiral floated a six-year unpaid "trial period", to keep the ship's cash problems quiet. The table settled on "he gets a salary". Amount not set.
