The **five high passengers** the [[Event Horizon]] took on at [[Regina]] on 196-1105 for [[Alell]], Cr60,000 each on safe arrival. Vetted by [[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]] on Streetwise and declared "so on the level you don't even need a spirit level"; the GM then rolled each of them on a D66 table, privately. [[Gunner Gunnerson]] looked them over and called the crew stupid.

What [[Talia]]'s headcount in the second jump turned up, none of them named yet:

- **The drunk** — passed out when checked
- **The recluse** — has quietly kept to their stateroom since leaving Regina
- **The noble** — "rich noble twat", haunting the ship like he owns it
- **The friendly one** — overly friendly, chatting to everyone, "seeing how things work", wanting to be part of everything. *Unremarked on at the table, but worth watching*
- **The blob** — a shapeshifting alien of an unnamed species that lives in a **skin suit**. Its reproductive cycle hit mid-voyage; it left the suit, set up in the ventilation as a mass of pulsating goo and filled the ship with a mating pheromone that made everything smell and taste of childhood. Identified by DNA match between goo and suit. Back in its suit it was sheepish, apologised, and hid in its stateroom for the rest of the trip. Can speak while suited. **Still paying**

They can roam the ship but not the research labs. Each uses a ton of cargo space.
