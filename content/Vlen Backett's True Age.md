The coroner's report on [[Vlen Backett]] states:

- **Physiological age:** 52
- **Historical records indicate true age at least 180**
- **No sign of anagathics in his blood system**
- No augmentations

He was at least 180 years old, had the body of a 52-year-old, and there is no chemical explanation for it.

Every relative who knew him as "every child's favourite uncle" knew a man who had been doing that for generations. [[Kostas Backett]] is 38.
