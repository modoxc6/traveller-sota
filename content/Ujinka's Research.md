[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

[[Iaros Ujinka]] was investigating **links between [[G-Band Transmissions]] and sites of [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]]**. That's what took her to [[Beck's World]], and it is what she and [[Vlen Backett]] were going to discuss.

###### Vlen's involvement
**Vlen was going to provide funding and a ship** to support the research. That is the "topics of mutual interest" from his letter — and it means a drifter with no visible income was in a position to bankroll a university research programme and put a hull under it.

He was murdered before the meeting happened.

###### The proposal to the party
Before her death, Ujinka asked whether the party could use the [[Event Horizon]] to support the research instead. **No final answer given yet.**

The Event Horizon is a Lab Ship. She is, functionally, exactly the vessel this research needs — and [[Graxius StClair]], who owns her, is Ujinka's former student.

Worth knowing: [[Kaldi Research]] runs lab ships for hire throughout the Marches. Ujinka could have chartered a professional research vessel. She asked a dead man's nephew's crew instead.

###### Why this matters
Vlen agreed to fund and crew an expedition to Ancient sites, and was shot before he could. **Ujinka was then shot dead** in her own house, minutes after explaining the research to a second set of potential backers.

Two people, one project, both murdered inside four days. Someone is killing to stop this research — and they knew where and when to find her.

###### Who would care
Ancients sites cluster in the [[Spinward Marches]], and their technology runs beyond TL25. A reliable method of *finding* those sites — which is what [[G-Band Transmissions]] would be — is worth killing for. Two movements on record already stake their theology on Ancients inheritance:

- [[Church of the Chosen Ones]] — [[Vargr]] are the Ancients' rightful heirs
- [[Brotherhood of Humaniti]] — the Ancients broke humanity, and opposing institutions must be destroyed

Nobody has been placed at either murder.

###### What survives her
- The party, who heard it from her directly
- Data recovered by Ozy from her house at [[Dancado]], concerning the Ancients research, Vlen and the g-band work. The GM has not yet specified its contents
- [[Beck's World]], where the fieldwork was, and where [[Graxius StClair]]'s ex-wife still is
- The [[University of Regina]], her department
- The [[Event Horizon]], still the right ship for it, and the decision now nobody's to accept

###### Notes
- The crew have not yet decided whether to commit the Event Horizon to continuing the research.
