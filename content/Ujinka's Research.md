#traveller #sota #clue
[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

[[Iaros Ujinka]] was investigating **links between [[G-Band Transmissions]] and sites of [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]]**. That's what took her to [[Beck's World]], and it is what she and [[Vlen Backett]] were going to discuss.

###### Vlen's involvement
**Vlen was going to provide funding and a ship** to support the research. That is the "topics of mutual interest" from his letter — and it means a drifter with no visible income was in a position to bankroll a university research programme and put a hull under it.

He was murdered before the meeting happened.

###### The proposal to the party
Before her death, Ujinka asked whether the party could use the [[Event Horizon]] to support the research instead. **No final answer given yet.**

The Event Horizon is a Lab Ship. She is, functionally, exactly the vessel this research needs — and [[Graxius StClair]], who owns her, is Ujinka's former student.

Worth knowing: [[Kaldi Research]] runs lab ships for hire throughout the Marches. Ujinka could have chartered a professional research vessel. She asked a dead man's nephew's crew instead.

###### Why this matters
Vlen agreed to fund and crew an expedition to Ancient sites, and was shot before he could. **Ujinka was then shot dead** in her own house, minutes after explaining the research to a second set of potential backers.

Two people, one project, both murdered inside four days. Someone is killing to stop this research — and they knew where and when to find her.

###### Who would care
Ancients sites cluster in the [[Spinward Marches]], and their technology runs beyond TL25. A reliable method of *finding* those sites — which is what [[G-Band Transmissions]] would be — is worth killing for. Two movements on record already stake their theology on Ancients inheritance:

- [[Church of the Chosen Ones]] — [[Vargr]] are the Ancients' rightful heirs
- [[Brotherhood of Humaniti]] — the Ancients broke humanity, and opposing institutions must be destroyed

Nobody has been placed at either murder.

- [[Beck's World]], where the fieldwork was, and where [[Graxius StClair]]'s ex-wife still is
- The [[University of Regina]], her department
- The [[Event Horizon]], still the right ship for it, and the decision now nobody's to accept

###### The research notes — recovered 196-1105
Three notes [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] pulled off Ujinka's computer at [[Dancado]], shared by the GM aboard the [[Event Horizon]] on 196-1105. Text as given.

**Research Note 1**
> Subject: Anomalous Attenuation of G-Band Radio Frequencies in Proximity to Purported Precursor Sites.
>
> ...While conventional Imperial archaeology treats the [[Boughene]] and [[Regina]] precursor ruins as chemically inert, localized sensor logs paint a radically different picture. During the 1102 survey, standard subspace telemetry in the G-band suffered a sharp, recursive drop-off. This is not natural solar radiation or iron-ore interference. It mimics a low-frequency, multi-phasic dampening field — a passive 'footprint' left by active Monadium structures buried deep beneath the mantle. If my calculations are correct, we can map hidden sites not by looking for ruins, but by looking for the communication 'dead zones' they cast across a system.

A later added note states:
> [[Juni]] and her team will be investigating these G-band signals further at the [[Beck's World]] ancients site. I wonder what secrets she will uncover.

**Research Note 2**
> - **Log Entry 884-A**: Orbiting Gas Giant (Boughene IV). High metal-rich asteroid density.
> - **Telemetry**: Ship sensor packet degradation detected at 4.2 GHz (G-band). Automated navigation computers experienced a 0.03-second clock-drift.
> - **Ujinka's Scribbled Margin Note**: "The trader wasn't lying. The statue he pulled from the asteroid belt is a fragment of a larger anchor array. The signal dampening here perfectly matches the footprint I isolated in the Regina sub-sector. Could something else be hidden away in this system? [[Vlen Backett]] thinks the system is worth studying, he has asked to borrow the statue apparently for analysis by one of his contacts."

**Research Note 3**
> Notes from the Deneb Archeological Conference: The Vilani records from thousands of years ago are fundamentally flawed. They logged the site at [[Kan]] as just another 'precursor society'. They lacked the conceptual leap. They found shimmering glass harder than diamond and containers made of unblemished, un-corroded cobalt, yet they classified it as dead rubbish. It isn't a collection of disparate alien species. It is a single, unified entity that wielded localised physics like paintbrushes. They didn't just build structures; they engineered the very matter to resist time.

###### What the notes establish
- **The method**: Ancient sites cast g-band "dead zones" across a system. Find the dead zone, find the site — no ruins needed. Ujinka attributes the dampening to buried "Monadium structures" (her term; not otherwise explained)
- **Two confirmed footprints**: the [[Regina]] sub-sector (the 1102 survey) and **[[Boughene]]**, at the asteroid belt around Boughene IV. Boughene's official description says there is nothing there of interest
- **The statue is from Boughene.** A trader pulled it from the belt; Ujinka judged it a **fragment of a larger anchor array**; Vlen borrowed it. It is now [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals|the statuette on the Event Horizon]]. The ship's scans call it a work of art, not a device, and of Ancients origin
- **Vlen's disks** corroborate all of this and add **a note from Vlen reminding himself to acquire the Professor's g-band sensor system and use it to scan the Boughene system**. Vlen had a target, not just an interest
- **[[Juni]]** — [[Graxius StClair]]'s ex-wife — has taken the g-band work to the Ancients site on [[Beck's World]] and is there now
- **Note 3 is a thesis about [[The Ancients]]** rather than about the signals: one unified entity, not many species, that "wielded localised physics like paintbrushes" and built matter to resist time. The Vilani saw the site at [[Kan]] and misfiled it as rubbish
