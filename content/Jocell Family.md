A branch of the [[Backett Family]] on [[Alell]], descended from a cousin who left [[Vaca Goya]] to find work in the starport.

They consider themselves cultured and cosmopolitan and look down on their "country bumpkin cousins", the Backetts. Kostas's parents had little to do with them.

The Jocell delegation is led by [[Marisa Jocell]], with her son [[Bard Jocell]].

*Source: Tel's pre-session handout on [[Alell]], mostly what [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]'s parents have told him.*
