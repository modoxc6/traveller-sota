[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

**A former pirate base.** A volcanic wilderness with a minimal biosphere and few natural resources.

Where [[Iaros Ujinka]] was working on [[Ujinka's Research]] — links between [[G-Band Transmissions]] and sites of [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]]. She had just returned from here when she wrote to [[Vlen Backett]] offering the meeting he never made.

**[[Graxius StClair]]'s ex-wife is still there.**

###### Worth noting
A volcanic rock with no biosphere, no resources and a pirate history is not somewhere a university historian goes for the archives. Whatever drew Ujinka there, it was in the ground or in the sky — and she came back from it ready to ask for funding and a ship.
