[[Travellers' Aid Society]] security camera footage from the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] — obtained and viewed by the party — shows **three men going to [[Vlen Backett]]'s room**.

- Two are on camera. Heavy-set men, dark jackets. Neither identified.
- **The third's face wasn't seen**, but he had **red hair**.
- The third man was carrying a device — see [[The Device]].

The two on the board are shown with question marks: the party has faces, not names.

###### Two identified at Dancado
At [[Dancado]], **two men came in through the front door and upstairs; one was killed and the other knocked unconscious and later taken into POC custody** — clearly the same two men from the footage.

That confirms the two attacks are the work of the same crew. Before the authorities arrived, the surviving intruder mentioned [[Ven Yasha]] but gave little else.

**The third man is still unaccounted for.** A shooter stayed outside throughout and did not come in. Whether that's the red-haired man with [[The Device]] is unestablished — but the attack opened the same way as the hostel job, with power and security knocked out first.

[[Pallod Norrim]] later said the POC had arrested [[Darrian Jak]] for Vlen's murder and confirmed that Jak is short and red-haired. That matches the third man's broad description, but it is not an independent identification.

###### Open questions
- Who the two Dancado attackers were, and who sent them
- Whether all three went into Vlen's room, and how many came out
- Whether the red-haired man deliberately kept off camera — see [[The Device]]
- Whether the shooter who stayed outside at Dancado is the third man
- Whether Darrian Jak is the red-haired third man or a convenient suspect
