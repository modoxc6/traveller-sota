**Round 5** — within **long range** now; Delta 2 went first
- Delta 2's first salvo arrived. At this range the crew could see it: **four missiles**. Cassius spent a Thrust on **Evasive Action** (DM−2). Gunnerson tried **point defence** with the pulse laser (Gunner +2): **missed**. Atticus had meant to move to the beam laser, but nobody had said so, so it stayed empty

- Attack: **Atticus fired the last 3 missiles**. **Missiles 0 of 12.** Firing used his turn, so he couldn't change turrets

- Cassius: **Leadership, Effect +5**

**Round 6** — the Event Horizon at 12, Delta 2 at −4: **the Event Horizon went first**

The GM rolled seven lots of 2D6 for the damage chain. **Delta 2 exploded**: secondary explosions ripped through it, then the second salvo "obliterates the hulk", fuel lines and gas venting on fire. **Delta 2 destroyed**

Roll **11, Effect +3**. **18 × 3 = 54 damage.** Three Severity 1 criticals (2D6 rolls of 3, 12 and 10): **Power Plant** (Power −10%), **Bridge** (a random station disabled: the GM made it **comms**), **Jump Drive** (DM−2 to all jump drive checks)
	- The second: Cassius evaded with his last Thrust. Roll **8, Effect 0**: it hits, but missile damage is multiplied by the Effect, so **no damage**
- **Hull 47 of 124**, "about half". The GM: if Delta 2 had gone first it would have been in range with its beam laser and could have shot down some of the crew's missiles: "good captaining there, Admiral"

###### Decisions and discoveries
- **[[Delta 2]] destroyed** in round 6: a **Far Trader** (missile rack, beam laser) posing as an Alell patrol ship. 58 damage from the Event Horizon's missiles, then the hulk obliterated. Who was aboard, and why, is unknown
- **All 12 missiles spent.** Restock Cr250,000
- **The Event Horizon took 77 damage: Hull 47 of 124.** She has **no armour** (the GM confirmed). Criticals: Hull (1D, done), **Power Plant (Power −10%)**, **Bridge comms (fixed by Ozy)**, **Jump Drive (DM−2 to jump checks, still broken)**
- **Cr300,000 in fares collected**
- **The friendly passenger** helped in the beam laser and may join the crew, to be rolled up
- **Black Horizon incinerated the stunned carthuses**, per the GM: none were kept alive after all
- **Alell is Kostas's birthplace**, and some of chapter 2's NPCs are his family
- **Rules settled at the table:** missile flight time depends only on range at launch, not on movement afterwards. Electronic Warfare against missiles is Difficult (10+) Electronics (sensors). Sensor Lock is contested and helps turret fire, not missiles. Evasive Action works against missiles, using unspent Thrust. Each salvo attacks separately, +1 per missile. Missile damage is multiplied by the attack's Effect, so **Effect 0 does no damage**. Every 10% of starting Hull lost is a Severity 1 critical. Overloading the drive again takes a cumulative DM−2. Hull damage can't be quick-repaired in combat. No momentum: cutting thrust stops the ship
