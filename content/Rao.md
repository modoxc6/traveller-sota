[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

Air-shuttle pilot working the [[Alell Starport]]. Name read off her shuttle window: **R-A-O, pronounced "Rayo"**. Flew the party and [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]]'s cryotube to [[Vaca Goya]] in [[Session 2026-10-18]].

![[rao.png|250]]

- Spotted the Backett family resemblance in Kostas. **Has ferried Vlen around a few times over the years**, and thought him "a bit secretive"
- Gave the tour: Alell was a hunting ground for nobles before the [[First Imperium]] fell, and is now a mix of science and conservation. Most settlements have high walls, smaller ones a sensor grid. Never go into the jungle alone; take a guide. "This place can eat you alive if you let it"
- Played along with Kostas's made-up facts so as not to embarrass him

###### Vlen's charter
**Some while ago Vlen chartered her to fly him out into the middle of nowhere**, no questions asked. She took the credits and left him. **She picked him up a week later**, unharmed. "He was mad for doing that." For **Cr100** from Kostas she marked the spot roughly on a moth-eaten map: [[Rao's Map]].

**Kostas has her number** for another ride.
