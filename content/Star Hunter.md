**[[Vlen Backett]]'s Far Trader.** Claimed by [[Marisa Jocell]] at Vlen's homecoming ([[Session 2026-10-25]]): "He owes me his spaceship."

###### Marisa's story
- **Ten years ago** Vlen arrived at the [[Alell Starport]], where Marisa works as a clerk, and **contacted her secretly: he was being followed and needed to hide his ship**
- She **altered the starport records and hid it in a disused hangar**
- For that favour, Vlen said he'd **leave her the ship when he died**
- **A few hours later an Imperial Scout ship came in**, piloted by [[Gand Holsiss]], who questioned Vlen about where he'd been and where his ship was. Vlen denied everything
- **The ship later went missing.** Marisa doesn't know where it is
- **She has the only copy of the original documentation** (per the GM). She wouldn't give [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] ID numbers or drive signatures to trace it

All of this is Marisa's word, except that the documentation exists. Her claim rests on a verbal promise, and on records she admits falsifying. See [[Claims on Vlen's Estate]].

*Possibly the ship Vlen was going to provide for [[Ujinka's Research]]. Connection drawn here, not at the table.*
