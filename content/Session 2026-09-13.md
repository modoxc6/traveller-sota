###### Live notes
- **Split up at the landing site.** The crew were still carrying weapons they're not allowed to hold on Law Level 9 Regina, and Kostas was bleeding out. [[Gunner Gunnerson|Gunnerson]] took [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] straight back up to the [[Event Horizon]] along with the statuette and the data disks; [[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]] and [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] went back into [[Credo City|Credo]] to tie up loose ends. Cover story if asked: they'd gone exploring outside the city and been ambushed. Cassius offered up his own savings to cover whatever the trip needed
- Kostas suggested from his sickbed that the others find [[Detective Odoy]] and give him an update — "let him keep causing more trouble for [[Ven Yasha]]". **It didn't happen.** GM: "Detective Odoy will never know what happened. He probably wouldn't believe you anyway"
- **[[Winter Shipping]]'s Credo office — a dead end.** Cassius and Ozy went back in. [[Exceedingly Discreet]] was gone — "the daffodil guy" is no longer in its sealed chamber, and nothing in the building indicated where it went. Ozy's one good Recon roll of the campaign turned up **a receipt for the trade the crew did with Winter Shipping earlier** — something they already knew about — and nothing else; the computers gave nothing. They were making the neighbours suspicious, so they left on a cover story about a package in transit being with a different courier. Ideas about checking shipping/taxi companies or finding where [[Llellewyoly|Dandelions]] congregate were floated and dropped: "this feels like a dead end". Back up to the [[Regina Highport|Highport]] on the hourly shuttle
- **Destination: [[Alell]]** — Vlen's homeworld and the Backetts', and where his body is to be buried. The GM noted Kostas is from [[Roup]] and that Ozy's homeworld is [[Uakye]], "right next door to Alell"
- **Route.** The Event Horizon is Jump-2, so Alell is **three jumps minimum, about three weeks**: [[Regina]] → [[Forboldn]] (two parsecs) → [[Whanga]] → Alell. Passengers could be sought for three destinations at two, four and five parsecs. Cassius asked for a pit stop at Barbag; the GM confirmed these are the real map names, not made up to sound rude
- **Passengers.** The ship's steward, [[Talia]] (Steward 3), rolled for high passage to Alell: Steward +3, high passage −4, starport A +5 (*heard as*), population/destination modifiers, came out at **11 people wanting high passage to Alell**. Cassius's idea of auctioning tickets to the highest bidder was withdrawn when the GM said a bad Streetwise roll would lose passengers. GM first ruled double occupancy for ten, then re-read the rule: **high passage is one per stateroom, so five**. Middle passage the same, basic passage two to a room. **Five high passengers to Alell at Cr60,000 each = Cr300,000** if they arrive safely
	- **Vetting.** Nobody has Investigate. Cassius rolled Streetwise and the table declared the passengers "fantastic people… so on the level you don't even need a spirit level" while describing them as Jeffrey Dahmer and Fred West lookalikes. The GM then rolled on a D66 table for each of the five, privately: **"Now I know who your high passengers are. And you guys don't."** Gunnerson looked them over and called the crew stupid. Kostas was in the medbay and saw none of it
	- Passengers can roam the ship but not the research labs or delicate areas
	- Each high passenger uses a ton of cargo space; the ship has 12 tons. **Low passage** was considered but the Event Horizon **has no low berths**
- **Cargo.** Ozy (Broker 1) rolled for incidental cargo: 15 lots available, two rolled at **6 tons and 4 tons**. The 6-ton lot was **advanced electronics**, base Cr100,000; the supplier wanted **110%** and Ozy's broker roll couldn't beat it. **Declined** — and by refusing, the crew have **burned their bridges with Regina's legal suppliers for a month**. GM hinted at other, less legal suppliers; the crew voted **passengers over cargo** instead
- **Finances.** Kostas's spreadsheet first said Cr236,000 a month in costs and Cr148,000 short; he then found a subtotal error — the ship was actually only **Cr33,500 short for the month** before this trip, having made Cr70,000 from passengers to Regina and Cr18,000 on the Regina cargo. The month falls due after two jumps; with Cr300,000 in passage fees the ship is in the black. GM: "You can't pay late. Well, you can — you'll just have people after you"
- **Fuel.** The lab ship sat skimming [[Assiniboia]] while the crew were on Regina. No roll is needed to skim, only to do it faster; Forboldn and Whanga both have gas giants, so the route can be flown on free fuel
- **Crew pay.** A Perception check (an easy 6) showed Talia was **pissed off** at five passengers landing on her while paid Cr2,000 a month — "Talia the underpaid". Ozy knew the figure. The crew **doubled her to Cr4,000**; she went "from not trusting you to mildly suspicious". Gunnerson is on Cr4,000 and the crew talked about a performance bonus for keeping them alive — undecided. The second gunner, [[Atticus Brown]], is cheap: he just wanted off-planet. Crew wages now Cr9,000 a month from Cr7,000
- **Kostas's surgery.** Done in the Event Horizon's medbay (DM+1, treats up to five patients) by [[Graxius StClair|StClair]], Medic 2, total DM+3, "up to his ass in research" and still mourning Ujinka. **Succeeded** — END up from 1 to **5**. Kostas plans the daily medical care from there: END to 7 and DEX up on day one, DEX to full on day two, STR sorted two days after. Two days of healing already banked by session end
- **The lore drop.** The GM shared three of [[Ujinka's Research|Ujinka's research notes]], recovered by Ozy from her computer at [[Dancado]] — see that note for the text — and described the statuette and disks:
	- **The statuette is a winged pillar of an ivory-like substance.** Scanned with the ship's sensors: a work of art, not a technological device. All evidence points to it being of [[The Ancients|Ancients]] origin
	- **It is the statue from Research Note 2** — the one a trader pulled from the asteroid belt in the [[Boughene]] system, which Ujinka's margin note says [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]] asked to borrow "for analysis by one of his contacts". Vlen somehow got hold of it; the disks corroborate the match
	- **The disks** cover the Boughene asteroid belt and the g-band fluctuations described in Note 2, and include **a note from Vlen reminding himself to acquire the Professor's g-band sensor system and use it to scan the Boughene system**
	- Note 1 is about the g-band "dead zone" method, and names **[[Juni]]** — StClair's ex-wife — as taking the work to the Ancients site on [[Beck's World]]. StClair reacted to her name
	- GM's plain-English gloss: unnatural interference on a band nobody at the table understands, which Ujinka — and Vlen — thought was a way to track down remaining Ancient sites, "and maybe the secrets of the Ancients"
- The Event Horizon is in one piece: the two research companies aboard, [[Helix]] and [[Black Horizon]], "haven't killed each other", and Talia reported a quiet few days. Gunnerson is regaling anyone who'll listen about his deeds on the base — his shin is still sore from where the Admiral kicked him, though it did shake an extra clip of ammo out of his pocket
- **Jump to Forboldn.** Cassius ran the ship computer: astrogation succeeded with Effect +1, jump drive roll came up **+6**. "Off to hell!" — **7 days 5 hours** in jump. D100 for random encounters came up clear
- **Downtime.** Cassius is **training** — Investigate was the joke, "some fucking thing we haven't got" — and needs eight weeks of it. StClair is researching; the GM hinted something might come of it. Using a skill at the table does not count towards training it
- **Cliffhanger, second or third morning in jump.** Talia came onto the bridge with a stern face: "I'm going to need your help on something." A **discovery has been made combining Helix equipment with Black Horizon research**, and the two teams are in a **blazing row over who owns it** — patents, royalties, ownership. The passengers haven't caught wind yet. **Ozy** intends to settle it with Advocate — he claims the charter contract has a clause defaulting disputed joint discoveries **to the carrier**, i.e. the ship; GM: "good try, we'll see". A well-thought-out approach may earn a Boon. **Parked for next session**

###### Decisions and discoveries
- **The ship is bound for [[Alell]]**, three jumps out, carrying five high passengers of unknown character. Vlen's home, his family, and his burial. Route Forboldn → [[Whanga]] → Alell; first leg under way
- **[[Exceedingly Discreet]] has gone.** No trace at the Credo office. It knows its base is gone or it doesn't; either way it's no longer where the crew can reach it. The [[Winter Shipping]] thread on Regina is closed for now
- **[[Detective Odoy]] was not told anything.** The crew left Regina without reporting back to anyone
- **The statuette is Ancient, is art, and came from [[Boughene]]'s asteroid belt** — the trader's find in Ujinka's Note 2, which Vlen "borrowed". It's a fragment of what Ujinka called a **larger anchor array**. This is the first physical link between Vlen, Ujinka's g-band work and a specific system
- **Vlen intended to scan Boughene** with Ujinka's g-band sensor system — the disks say so in his own words. His funding-and-a-ship offer had a target
- **[[Ujinka's Research]] now has text.** Three notes: the g-band dead-zone method (Note 1), the Boughene log and the statue (Note 2), and the Deneb conference claim that the Ancients were a single unified entity that "engineered the very matter to resist time" (Note 3)
- **StClair's ex-wife is [[Juni]]**, and she is working the Ancients site on [[Beck's World]] right now
- **Regina's legal cargo suppliers are closed to the crew for a month**
- **Kostas is out of danger** — surgery succeeded, END 5 and climbing
- **Rules settled at the table:** high/middle passage is one passenger per stateroom; each high passenger consumes a ton of cargo space; skimming needs no roll; a failed surgery costs 3 + Effect (confirmed from p.82 but not tested); skill use doesn't count as training
