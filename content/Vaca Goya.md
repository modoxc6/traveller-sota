Home of the [[Backett Family]] on [[Alell]]. Kostas's parents call it a village.

- One of its most important dignitaries is the **village porter**, currently [[Porter Sajan]]
- The [[Jocell Family]] are descended from a Backett cousin who **left Vaca Goya** for work in the starport

*Not a walled lodge-city: a small hilltop village inside a sensor grid ([[Session 2026-10-18]]).*

*Source: Tel's pre-session handout on [[Alell]], mostly what [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]'s parents have told him.*

See the [Alell Family Tree](https://modoxc6.github.io/personal/alell-family-tree/) for the whole family and everyone else at the homecoming, on one page.

###### Tel's map
![[Attachments/Vaca Goya.webp]]

- A **cleared patch of bare ground** cut out of the red and orange jungle
- The **village** is in the middle: about a hundred small buildings on winding lanes. A **round building** stands near the centre, and a few buildings are picked out in red
- **Five large green fields** fill the space between the village and the edge of the clearing, two of them on the east side
- A **river** comes in from the west, widens into a pool at the south-west corner, and leaves south through the boundary
- A lane leads from the village to a **round, bare patch** by the pool
- A few **outbuildings** stand alone out near the edges
- The whole clearing is ringed by a **line of evenly spaced green dots**, with jungle beyond

*Inferences, not stated: the dots look like a perimeter of posts, lights or defences rather than a wall. The round patch by the pool could be a landing pad. On this map Vaca Goya looks like a farming village, not one of the walled lodge-cities.* **Confirmed in [[Session 2026-10-18]]: the dots are mist-sprayer poles, and it is a farming village. The landing pad is still a guess.**

###### Notes
-
- [[Session 2026-10-18]]: visited for Vlen's homecoming
	- **A small village on a hilltop**, "not one of the big ones". **Founded on a nearby uranium deposit**; the mines are largely exhausted and it now lives on **forestry and farming**. **Population about a thousand**
	- **The green dots on the map are a ring of metal poles topped with motion-sensitive mist sprayers**, the village's sensor grid. **Green is good; "if they ever go red, watch out."** That settles the inference above: a sensor grid, not a wall, as smaller settlements use
	- Shuttles land on the outskirts. Four were already there when the party arrived, more came later
	- The **village hall** has terminals. [[Porter Sajan]] is magistrate and spiritual leader
	- Kostas's old school and old house are here. It looks smaller than he remembers
	- The GM said the map is AI-generated; the book has none
