One of **two research companies chartering lab space aboard the [[Event Horizon]]**, the other being [[Black Horizon]]. Their labs are off limits to passengers.

Two or three days into the jump from [[Regina]] to [[Forboldn]], a **discovery was made combining Helix equipment with Black Horizon research**, and the two teams fell into a blazing row over ownership, patents and royalties. [[Talia]] asked the crew to intervene; [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] means to argue a carrier clause in the charter contract. Unresolved — parked for next session.

Nothing else known about the company or what it researches.
