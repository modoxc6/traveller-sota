One of **two research companies chartering lab space aboard the [[Event Horizon]]**, the other being [[Black Horizon]]. Their labs are off limits to passengers.

Two or three days into the jump from [[Regina]] to [[Forboldn]], a **discovery was made combining Helix equipment with Black Horizon research**, and the two teams fell into a blazing row over ownership, patents and royalties. [[Talia]] asked the crew to intervene. **Settled against them**: Ozy's analysis of the logs (Computers, Effect +4 — he chose the outcome) showed the discovery was Black Horizon's, and Black Horizon's contract gives them exclusive patent rights with no royalties. Helix are pissed off but bound.

**Their charter terms** (per Ozy's read): genuinely honest, painfully bureaucratic, red tape everywhere; the ship gets a **2% royalty on breakthroughs, capped at Cr1,000,000**.

Their rep aboard is played with a hyena picture; name heard as "Rock Anlo", spelling unconfirmed.

Nothing else known about the company or what it researches.
