[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

[[The Ancients]] vanished abruptly some 300,000 years ago. The sudden extinction is a mystery to archaeologists, who have produced several competing theories. Mainstream thinking divides along **two axes**.

###### Homogenous vs Heterogenous
- **Homogenous** — there was a single Ancients culture that expanded and then vanished. Explains the similarities between sites: commonalities in architecture, the use of **[[Droyne]] as pets or servants**, and so on.
- **Heterogenous** — there were multiple competing cultures, a series of empires, or even multiple races that reached high technology in the Ancients period. Explains the wild variation in Ancients technology and architecture, the lack of clear structure to their territory, and the sudden end of their civilisation.

###### Catastrophist vs Gradual Decline
- **Catastrophist** — a single terrible event ended the Ancients hegemony. Homogenous Catastrophists point to disease, a hostile alien race, or a strange accident. Heterogenous Catastrophists suggest **civil war**.
- **Gradual Declinist** — a super-civilisation would be more robust than that; the Ancients must have slowly declined in vitality and viability over millennia.

###### Why it's in the file
Most Ancients sites show damage consistent with orbital bombardment, and some systems show anomalies explicable only by weapons that destroyed whole planets. Whatever happened, it was violent, and [[Ujinka's Research]] was a method for finding what's left of it.
