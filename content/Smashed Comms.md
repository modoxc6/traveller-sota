A smashed comms unit recovered from [[Vlen Backett]]'s room at the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] — the room the [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]] walked into.

Whether it was smashed by hand or knocked out by [[The Device]] is unestablished. Either way, someone did not want its contents read.

Ozy made several repair attempts. Immediately before the unit became fully damaged and unusable, he recovered **the last number called**: it belongs to [[Winter Shipping]], a logistics and cargo-buying company operating in Credo City and Star Town.
