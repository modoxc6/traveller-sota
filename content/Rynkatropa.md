[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A minor race of sophonts resembling ambulatory sea slugs.

- Three pseudopods, used to manipulate objects and to move on land
- Communicate by whistling
- Four sexes: male, female, neuter and incubator
- Had just reached **TL1** when they were **enslaved by the [[Vargr]]**
