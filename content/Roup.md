Homeworld of [[Kostas Backett]].

https://travellermap.com/?p=-94.679%2173.119%218.15

**A water world.** The only stable 'land' consists of synthetic islands grown from webs of micro-organisms and seaweed — huge rafts supporting vast cities, factories, farmland and even the starport.

Roup's citizens practise **aquaculture on a planetary scale**, with whole oceans sectioned off using bubble-nets to maximise productivity.

###### Worth noting
[[Tusal]] is **derived from a seaweed**. Kostas grew up on a world whose entire economy is farmed seaweed and micro-organism rafts. Probably nothing — Tusal is grown along the [[Corridor]] sector and has been in Imperial space a century — but the man whose uncle was killed with a seaweed narcotic comes from a planet of seaweed farms.
