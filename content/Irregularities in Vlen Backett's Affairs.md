From *Handout 2*, [[Pallod Norrim]]'s death notification:

> Given certain irregularities with Vlen Backett's affairs, it is vitally important that you contact the Public Order Commission upon arrival.

Unspecified. The [[Regina Public Order Commission]] won't say what they are in writing, which is itself worth noting.

###### What's unexplained
- What the irregularities actually are
- Why he pre-registered [[Kostas Backett]] as next of kin, given how little contact the family had with his real life
- Why a drifter with no visible income had money on deposit at the [[First Ducal Bank]] and heard from nobility, naval officers and [[Vargr]]

###### Since answered
- **How he died** — the letter never said. The coroner's report does: [[Vlen Backett's Murder]]. Norrim wrote to summon his next of kin without once mentioning he'd been shot, which may be the whole of the "irregularities" or may be a separate problem.
- The report also turned up something the letter didn't hint at: [[Vlen Backett's True Age]].

###### Notes
- Pallod later claimed [[Darrian Jak]] murdered Vlen and treated the case as closed, without explaining the irregularities that originally made Kostas's presence "vitally important".
