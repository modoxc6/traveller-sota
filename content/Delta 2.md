Ship that hailed the [[Event Horizon]] as she came out of jump at [[Alell]], c. 219-1105: **"This is Alell patrol ship Delta 2. Prepare for inspection. Please confirm."** Later "prepare for docking procedures".

**Destroyed by the Event Horizon's missiles, [[Session 2026-10-04]].**

**It wasn't what it said it was.**
- Ozy's Computers check: **Alell doesn't normally have patrol ships assigned**
- When Ozy asked for its security handshake and the proper ship-to-ship protocol, it **cut comms and armoured up**
- **Faster than the Event Horizon** (thrust above her 2), and it came straight at her, thrusting flat out
- **Per the GM after the fight: a Far Trader** with two weapons, a **missile rack** and a **beam laser**

The table guessed "space pirate". The GM's framing during the fight: it was going for a "shitty lab ship ripe for the plucking" that it probably didn't expect to be armed. Who crewed it and who sent it is unknown. Nobody was identified and nothing was recovered.

###### The fight
- **Fired at least three salvos**: one in round 1, two more in round 2 (the second launch was detected in round 3 and taken for a new one). The first, **four missiles**, hit in round 5 for 20 plus a 3-point Hull critical. The next two hit in round 6 after Delta 2 was already destroyed: **54 damage** with three criticals, then Effect 0 for none
- Its point defence knocked one missile out of the Event Horizon's first salvo
- It had the initiative in rounds 4 and 5. If it had gone first in round 6 it would have been in beam laser range, able to fire on the Event Horizon or her missiles
- **Took 30 damage in round 5 (Effect +2) and 28 in round 6 (Effect +3)**, then the damage chain and a second salvo blew it apart: "secondary, tertiary explosions… fuel lines exploding". The hulk was obliterated
