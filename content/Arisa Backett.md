**Claims to be [[Vlen Backett]]'s wife.** Arrived at his homecoming in [[Vaca Goya]] in [[Session 2026-10-18]], shyly, after the [[Brotherhood of Humaniti]] monks: fully clothed, with hair, and a **Southern accent** ("my little chickadee"). *Name heard several ways in the recording. Written up as "Arisa" until Rich confirmed **Arisa** after [[Session 2026-10-25]].*

- "My name is Arisa Backett. Vlen was my husband"
- To [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]: "I'm your aunt. Come here, my little nephew." Big hug and a kiss on the cheek. She says she's never met the family, and asked to be introduced
- **She has a wedding certificate**, and shows it to anyone who'll listen
- [[Tomas Backett]]: "Her husband, Vlen? Rubbish." He got flustered and kept glancing at Kostas
- [[Tyrus Cassius|The Admiral]] doubts the certificate is worth the paper it's written on

Nobody in the family knew Vlen had a wife. Unverified. See [[Claims on Vlen's Estate]].
