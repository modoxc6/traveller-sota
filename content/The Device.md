[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

The third of the [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]] — the red-haired one whose face was never caught — carried a device that appears to have **caused electrical problems**.

Recorded on the board with a question mark: the connection between the device and the interference is the party's reading of the footage, not an established fact.

"What is this device that was used?" is one of the party's active questions. It may also explain the [[Smashed Comms]].

###### Possible second sighting
At [[Dancado]], during the attack on [[Iaros Ujinka]], **the power was cut and a security drone taken out**. Electrical systems failing at the exact moment of an attack — the same signature as the interference on the hostel footage.

If that's the same device, the same crew is running both hits, and knocking out power and security is the opening move rather than a side effect.

[[Kostas Backett]] asked [[Pallod Norrim]] about the attackers' apparent ability to disable systems in a localised area. Pallod dismissed it, the grav car and any connection between the murders as coincidence and nothing to worry about.
