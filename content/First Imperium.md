The earlier, Vilani-era Imperium that preceded the [[Imperium|Third Imperium]].

- [[Alell]] was **settled in its dying days**, when nobles built hunting lodges there, staffed by technicians, groundskeepers and servants
- **It collapsed**, leaving Alell cut off; explorers from "the resurgent Imperium" re-contacted it several hundred years later
- Alell's starport dates back to it, and is considered an excellent example of fin-de-siècle Vilani design

*Source: Tel's pre-session handout, quoting* Checking In Indefinitely: A History of Alell *(Tharsis Djar, Alell Free Press, 1101).*
