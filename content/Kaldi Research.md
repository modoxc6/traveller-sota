[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A private corporation with two main branches:

- The bulk of its assets are in **fixed installations** doing research on contract for corporate bodies and planetary governments
- A much smaller proportion goes into operating **a number of laboratory ships** that travel throughout the Marches and beyond, either hiring out their services to whoever needs a general research team, or engaging in ongoing studies

###### Why this is in the file
The [[Event Horizon]] is a **Lab Ship**. Kaldi runs lab ships around the Marches for hire. That makes them the obvious commercial answer to the question [[Ujinka's Research]] posed — and a party with a lab ship looking to do Ancients fieldwork is either Kaldi's competition, Kaldi's cover, or Kaldi's next contract.

Nothing established connects Kaldi to either murder, or to Ujinka. Noted because the shape matches.
