The crew's contact at [[Winter Shipping]], arranged on 195-1105.

Olso is **collecting the crew's cargo** — the 3 tons of crystals and gems brought from [[Yori]], bought for Cr54,000 and sold to Winter Shipping for Cr72,000. The sale was cover for investigating the company, and the collection gives the crew a legitimate reason to watch Winter Shipping personnel at work.

Nothing else established: role, seniority, or any connection to [[Harlen]] or [[Ven Yasha]].

As with [[Marid]], Olso is on the legitimate side of the business and appears to know nothing of [[Exceedingly Discreet]] or the crew's arrangement with it.
