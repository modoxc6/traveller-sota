[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

The setting's dominant polity — formally the **Third Imperium**. Official documents are dated by its regnal year: *Handout 2* is sealed "190 Standard, in the 1105th year of the Third Imperium".

The **Imperial Calendar** numbers days within the year — 190-1105, 193-1105 — and names weekdays; 193-1105 is a Thirday. Campaign present is 193-1105. [[Vargr]] from *outside* the Imperium once travelled hundreds of parsecs to consult [[Vlen Backett]] — so there's a meaningful outside, and Vlen has business in it.

Presumably the same navy [[Kostas Backett]] was drafted into and [[Tyrus Cassius]] and [[Aylin Boro]] served in, though nothing has said so outright yet.
