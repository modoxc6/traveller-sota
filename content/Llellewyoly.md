[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A minor race native to Junidy in the Aramis subsector of the [[Spinward Marches]]. Nicknamed **'Dandelions'** or **'Dandies'**.

- Resemble a two-metre-tall version of a Terran plant, with **five stalk-like limbs** used for manipulation, movement and sensing
- Highly intelligent, with excellent memory recall and organisational skills — possibly a result of their incredibly complex social structure
- A Llellewyoly holds **dozens of formal titles simultaneously**, and addressing one by the wrong title is a grievous insult
- Rarely encountered outside their homeworld
