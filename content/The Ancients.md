[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

Colloquial name for a species that lived **300,000 years ago**. Comparatively little is known about them.

###### What they did
- Believed responsible for **transplanting Humaniti** from its original homeworld of Terra (the Solomani Hypothesis) and scattering the species across hundreds of planets
- Believed to have **uplifted the [[Vargr]] from Terran canines**
- Exceedingly high technology — many artefacts are millennia beyond cutting-edge Imperial science. Estimates of high-end Ancients tech place them **beyond TL25**

###### Their sites
Several have been discovered across Charted Space, **especially in the [[Spinward Marches]]** — where the party currently are.

- The technology at these sites **varies considerably**, suggesting different Ancients groups pursued radically different paths of development
- Most sites show **damage consistent with orbital bombardment or other violence**
- There are anomalies in other systems explicable only by weapons that destroyed entire planets
- That, plus their sudden disappearance from known space, has produced the [[Ancients Extinction Theories]]
- Ancients sites also show common use of **[[Droyne]] as pets or servants**

###### Why this matters here
[[Iaros Ujinka]] was tracing links between [[G-Band Transmissions]] and Ancients sites — see [[Ujinka's Research]]. [[Vlen Backett]] was going to fund and crew the expedition. **Both are now dead.**

Threads worth holding together, none of them established:
- Vlen was a man that [[Vargr]] crossed hundreds of parsecs to consult — and the Vargr are an Ancients-uplifted species
- The [[Church of the Chosen Ones]] holds that Vargr are the rightful heirs of the Ancients
- The [[Brotherhood of Humaniti]] holds that the Ancients *broke* humanity, and that institutions opposing its reunification must be destroyed
- [[Vlen Backett's True Age]]: at least 180 years old, physiologically 52, no anagathics. Nobody has explained that
