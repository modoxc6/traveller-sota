In-fiction date: 195-1105, evening, running into the early hours of 196-1105

###### Starting position
The crew are at [[The Flotsam]] late on 195-1105, having just heard out [[Detective Odoy]].

- Two irreconcilable accounts of [[Vlen Backett's Murder]] are on the table: the POC's ([[Darrian Jak]] did it, closed) and Odoy's ([[Ven Yasha]] did it, [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]] was his courier, the [[Imperium|Imperials]] are here for Yasha). Neither verified
- [[Pallod Norrim]] is unreachable; both [[Dancado]] attackers are in Imperial hands
- [[Winter Shipping]] is the strongest lead: Vlen's last call, [[Harlen]]'s employer, two buildings straddling the [[Credo City]]/[[Startown|Star Town]] fence, soft alarms, a near-empty network and launch runs to [[Regina Highport]] that take hours too long
- The crew are Cr18,000 up on the [[Yori]] cargo, sold to Winter Shipping as cover; **[[Olso]]** is collecting it
- [[Kostas Backett]] remains injured at STR 3, DEX 9, END 1

###### Live notes
- Planning discussion first. Options weighed: break into [[Winter Shipping]], visit [[Darrian Jak]] in prison, investigate the [[Alahir]]. The break-in won
- The [[Event Horizon]], in orbit around [[Assiniboia]], **can track Winter Shipping's launch** once it lifts. There is only **one launch**, parked by the [[Startown|Star Town]] office. Tracking alone is impractical without knowing which run is the anomalous one — hence the need for the flight schedule
- Rejected as unworkable: astrogation to narrow down destinations from flight durations, faking shipping documents (nobody has Streetwise), buying or building tracking drones, and shipping [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] up in a crate
- The crew have **no weapons** — [[Regina]] is Law Level 9. [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] has a plastic knife
- They approached the closed **Credo-side** office after dark. **The group stealth check failed** — the party were seen loitering and their presence noted as suspicious. They withdrew and tried again
- Long tangent on distractions (faking a heart attack for [[Tyrus Cassius|the Admiral]], StClair stabbing him in the leg, a bomb in a bin). None used
- **[[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] went in alone and aced it.** He reached the building unseen, disabled the alarms on an electronics/sensors roll, slipped inside and opened a side access for the others
- Inside the Credo-side building: dark, mostly empty crate tables — and one room in the far corner with a door ajar, light, muttering and the tapping of data pads
- Through the door: a **hermetically sealed environmental chamber** with a **[[Llellewyoly]]** — a "Dandelion" — inside, working an enormous volume of data across multiple pads and monitors on a **closed system**. This is why the hacked server held nothing
- Below the floor the crew found a **ramp down into a tunnel** running towards the Star Town building. Not concealed. Down the tunnel: a **newer pipe with vents at equal intervals** (unexplained), and a storeroom holding empty gin bottles and crates of **non-human food**. [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] ate some and vomited. The far end has a ramp up on the Star Town side, **closed, with no mechanism visible from the tunnel**
- Long argument over killing it, kidnapping it, poisoning its food or hacking it. An electronics check confirmed **no external route into its system** — kidnap or conversation only. The crew chose to talk
- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] and [[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]] knocked on the glass. It answered on a comm unit: *"I am exceedingly discreet. What is your title at this time, strangers?"* — recorded as **[[Exceedingly Discreet]]**. It addresses everyone by invented titles: "overly dressed pompous man" (Cassius), "pale-skinned smartly dressed man" (Ozy), "downtrodden depressed man" (Kostas), "unruly-haired rude scientist man" (StClair, after he greeted it as a "ballsack with chicken legs")
- Cassius' cover story — an external security audit — was dismissed as illogical, as was a follow-up about missing cargo. **The crew then told it the truth**: Vlen's murder, [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s murder, the [[Three Men at Vlen's Room|TAS footage]], the man present at both killings, the POC-liveried grav car, [[The Device]], and that they are looking for [[Harlen]] and for [[Ven Yasha]]
- It took the truth well. **It is the brains of the operation**, and says **[[Harlen]] runs the company** — "short-sighted and a poor tactician", but loyal. **[[Ven Yasha]] hired them all.** It thinks Harlen is not the right person to be running Winter Shipping long-term, and that the crew's evidence marks him as a failure
- **The deal, agreed:** it puts the crew on Winter Shipping's launch and up to **the company's station in orbit**, where [[Harlen]] is. In return the crew **guarantee Harlen does not survive**, and never report the conversation. Its own ambition — taking over the company — is the crew's guarantee it won't turn them in
- It can control the launch's flight path from its terminal. **[[Marid]] does not need to know.** It suggests the launch **rendezvous with the [[Event Horizon]] in orbit** so the crew can arm themselves — they will have to deal with the station's staff and Harlen, and Credo weapons law makes arming on the ground impossible
- What it gave up, all its own account and unverified:
	- **[[Ven Yasha]] ordered [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s death.** It does not understand why, and says so plainly — greater understanding of Ancient technology would only raise the value of Ancient artefacts
	- **[[Vlen Backett]] was a messenger for Ven Yasha** — "he must have brought bad news, hence his demise"
	- It was aware of both murders
	- On what Yasha wants from [[The Ancients|Ancient]] technology: nobody understands the secrets of the Ancients, but the potential runs to immortality, taking systems, taking the [[Imperium]], destroying armies. It knows of no specific plan
	- **[[Harlen]] holds some of [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]]'s possessions** — a **statuette**, and **data crystals**. Volunteered unprompted, because Kostas is his nephew
	- **Imperial Intelligence** were on [[Regina]] in numbers. They arrived on the [[Alahir]], **which has since departed** — whatever their business was, it is concluded. The captain's name was heard as "Gand Hulses" (spelling unconfirmed) and he is said to hold some special portfolio in the intelligence services. It does not know whether they came for Yasha or for something else
	- It refuses to discuss [[Ven Yasha]] himself at all — "for my own survival" — while offering information on Harlen, on the hired thugs, and on Imperial Intelligence
	- On Harlen's weaknesses: none as such. Loyal to Yasha and not for turning. "He is just a man, though"
- Session ended in the small hours of 196-1105, in the Credo-side office, deal struck

###### Decisions and discoveries
- **Winter Shipping's real records live in a [[Llellewyoly]]'s head and on a closed system in the Credo-side office.** The empty server is explained
- **[[Exceedingly Discreet]]** — the Llellewyoly — is now an ally of convenience with its own agenda: it wants [[Harlen]] dead and the company for itself
- **[[Harlen]] runs [[Winter Shipping]]**, and **[[Ven Yasha]] hired all of them.** First confirmation from inside the company of the Yasha link that [[Detective Odoy]] alleged
- **[[Ven Yasha]] ordered [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s murder** (per Exceedingly Discreet). The motive is unexplained even to the company's own brain
- **[[Vlen Backett]] was Yasha's messenger** — a second, independent version of Odoy's courier claim
- **[[Harlen]] holds Vlen's [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals|statuette and data crystals]]** — the first concrete object of Vlen's known to be in the killers' hands
- **There is a [[Winter Shipping Station|Winter Shipping station in orbit]]** — the destination of the overlong launch runs, and where Harlen is
- **The [[Alahir]] has left [[Regina]]**, its Imperial Intelligence contingent's business concluded. Its captain reportedly holds a special intelligence portfolio; name heard as "Gand Hulses", **spelling and identity unconfirmed**
- The **tunnel** between the two buildings is real and unconcealed, with a closed ramp at the Star Town end and an unexplained newer, vented pipe running its length
- The party still have **no weapons** and no Streetwise; almost every plan this session failed on a missing skill
- The crew have promised to **kill [[Harlen]]**. Nobody has yet raised what happens if that turns out to be a lie told to them by a plant

###### End position
- Early hours of 196-1105, inside the closed Credo-side [[Winter Shipping]] office, with the deal struck and [[Exceedingly Discreet]] still at its console
- The route up is arranged: Winter Shipping's launch, its flight path set by Exceedingly Discreet, with a rendezvous with the [[Event Horizon]] in orbit to collect weapons before boarding the [[Winter Shipping Station|station]]
- [[Marid]], [[Olso]] and the rest of the company are unaware of any of it
- [[Kostas Backett]] is still at STR 3, DEX 9, END 1. [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] has been sick and has a plastic knife
- [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s request that the [[Event Horizon]] back [[Ujinka's Research]] is still unanswered
