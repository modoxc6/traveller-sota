Player character. **Owns the [[Event Horizon]]** — though the other characters' Ship Shares contributed a little towards her.

###### Connections
- **Mentor** — [[Iaros Ujinka]], who was very supportive of him. She later went off to work with his ex-wife.
- **Ex-wife** — unnamed so far. Ujinka went to work with her, and she is **still on [[Beck's World]]** — where [[Ujinka's Research]] is centred.

###### Appearance
![[stclair.png|250]]

Older man, wild grey hair, wire-rimmed glasses, unshaven. Battered pale canvas coat over layered work clothes, festooned with straps, pouches, tools and a heavy satchel. Scuffed boots. Holding a book or slate. Looks like someone who works with his hands and hasn't slept indoors much lately.
