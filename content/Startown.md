#traveller #sota #location
[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

The region around the old [[Regina]] starport, now the primary downport. A strip of bars, cheap hostelries, raucous entertainments and less savoury businesses — **notoriously violent, corrupt and profitable**.

Technically divided between the nearby cities of [[Credo City|Credo]] and Atora; both blame the other for the state of it.

It is a thin strip between Credo and [[Atora]]. [[Winter Shipping]] operates here as well as in Credo City.

[[Regina Highport]] sits in geostationary orbit above Startown.

*If [[The Flotsam]] is anywhere, this is the sort of district it would be in — unconfirmed.*
