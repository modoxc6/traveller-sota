[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

**Rival.** A mysterious crime lord, wanted by numerous worlds around [[Regina]]. Location unknown.

No-one has ever seen them, so the wanted bulletins are extremely vague. Gender, species, whether "Ven Yasha" is even one person — all unknown.

###### Connection to Kostas
[[Kostas Backett]] has had plenty of issues throughout his career. Just the once, he put out feelers on who was behind one of them, and only one name came back.

Whispers from the shadows, a piece of paper found under a door, hushed exchanges with a contact — whatever went wrong and whenever he went looking for who was behind it, it was just a name and nothing more.

###### Connection to Vlen
His name **kept cropping up in connection with [[Vlen Backett]]** as well. [[Iaros Ujinka]] doesn't know him personally, only by reputation — and says he is very unpleasant.

So the same name attaches to both Kostas' career troubles and his murdered uncle's affairs. That is either the single most important fact in the investigation or a coincidence of a very common bogeyman.

The surviving intruder from the attack at [[Dancado]] mentioned Ven Yasha during the crew's brief questioning before the POC arrived. He gave little else, so the nature of the connection remains unknown.

###### Odoy's account — 195-1105
[[Detective Odoy]] is **investigating Ven Yasha**, and told the crew that **Yasha killed [[Vlen Backett]]** and that **Vlen was working as a courier for Yasha**. He also says the [[Imperium|Imperial]] force that arrived aboard the [[Alahir]] is on [[Regina]] **hunting Yasha**, with the [[Zhodani]]-spy story serving as cover for their takeover of the inquiry.

None of this is independently verified — it is Odoy's word, and his own agency and motives are unknown. But it is now the **second independent source** naming Yasha: the surviving [[Dancado]] attacker did so too, before the Imperials removed him from POC custody.

If Odoy is right, the courier theory would explain Vlen's constant travel and his improbable address book.

###### Open questions
- Who is Ven Yasha?
- If Vlen was his courier, what was Vlen carrying — and does it connect to [[Ujinka's Research]]?
- Why an Imperial force would cross to Regina to hunt him
- How many of Kostas' career problems are actually theirs? Only one was ever traced.
- What the connection to Vlen actually is — creditor, rival, employer, killer?
- Whether "he" is right: Ujinka uses it, but nobody has ever seen the man
