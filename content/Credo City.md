**Capital city of [[Regina]]**, population 87 million, on the eastern edge of the continent of Noncredo. Called **Credo** in the library entries; "Credo City" is how the Commission's paperwork refers to it.

Where [[Vlen Backett]] was murdered.

###### The city
Credo prides itself on its cosmopolitanism — a high transient population including many visitors from the Core worlds. Fashion and culture here are heavily influenced by those visitors, which causes friction with the more traditional cities on Regina. It is sometimes called **'little Capital'**, a mocking reference to its mimicry of Imperial high culture.

Despite the cultural pretensions, Regina's curious and strict legal system is fully in effect: **laws must be voted on by the entire population**, and the justice system is as bureaucratic as it is enthusiastic, without being efficient at either. See [[Regina Public Order Commission]] and [[Assumption of Custody]].

A city with a large transient population, and a legal regime that sweeps transients — see how those two facts sit together.

His body is held in a cryogenic facility here, pending transfer to [[Kostas Backett]] for transport home to [[Alell]]. His outstanding affairs and belongings are here too.

He was staying at the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] here when he was killed. The dealer [[Darrian Jak]] works back alleys around the commercial district. The estate of [[Dancado]] lies just outside the city, and [[The Flotsam]] is a bar here.

[[Winter Shipping]], the company behind the last number recovered from Vlen's comms, operates here and in [[Startown|Star Town]].
