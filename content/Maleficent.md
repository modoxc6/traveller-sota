*An animal.* Infamous and mysterious predators of the [[Alell]] jungles — fast and brutal.

- **Jet-black**, though markings can be seen under ultraviolet light
- Travel through the trees by means of **tentacles**, snaking along the canopy in search of prey
- Little is known about their biology

**Some scientists believe they are not originally native to Alell.**

That last line is why this note is tagged as a clue. A predator nobody can classify, with markings visible only outside the human visual range, on a world nobody can explain the wildlife of — in a campaign about a species that seeded life across hundreds of worlds. Noted, not concluded.
