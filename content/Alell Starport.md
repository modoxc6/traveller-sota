The **Class B surface starport** of [[Alell]]. Alell has no port in orbit, so this is the only place to land. A pinnace like the [[Implacable]] can use it; the [[Event Horizon]] can't.

![[Attachments/Alell Starport.webp]]

###### What's known
- Its original structure dates back to the [[First Imperium]], and is considered an excellent example of fin-de-siècle Vilani design
- Refined fuel, maintenance and spacecraft construction facilities (Starport B, from the UWP)
- The [[Jocell Family]] are descended from a [[Backett Family|Backett]] cousin who left [[Vaca Goya]] for work at the starport

###### From Tel's picture and map
- The main building is a huge **stepped pyramid** of grey stone or concrete, with a tall entrance arch on one face. Small craft are parked on its flat top, and another flies overhead
- A tall round **tower** stands beside it, and there's a second tower further off
- The pyramid sits on a cleared square in **red, orange and purple jungle**, with mountains behind and a **river** running past
- A road leads from the pyramid to a **round gatehouse** set in the wall of a **walled town** with a street grid. A second round structure stands near the river, and there's a small cluster of buildings beside the pyramid

*Inferences, not stated: the round structures on the map are probably the towers in the picture. The walled town fits the history's description of the old lodges, which are now walled cities. Neither is confirmed, and the town isn't named.*
