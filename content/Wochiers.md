[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

Atmospheric pressure at sea level is **more than four bars**, so the population lives in the mountains or in underground complexes.

That high pressure makes Wochiers ideal for certain engineering projects, especially **crystal growing**.

By tradition, all visitors must bring an offering of a living plant to the hydroponics caves. Suitable plants are available in the starport gift shop.
