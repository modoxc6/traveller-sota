Chief Inspector of the [[Regina Public Order Commission]].

Sent [[Kostas Backett]] the notification of [[Vlen Backett]]'s death (*Handout 2*), naming him next of kin and summoning him to [[Regina]] with all due haste. Wants Kostas to contact the Commission on arrival, citing irregularities in Vlen's affairs — see [[Irregularities in Vlen Backett's Affairs]].

Opens the letter as **Pallod Norrim** and signs it **Pallod Norum**. Probably a typo. Probably.

###### Notes
- [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] reported the attack and its aftermath at [[Dancado]] to him.
- Pallod later called [[Kostas Backett]] to say the POC had arrested short, red-haired [[Darrian Jak]] for Vlen's murder.
- Kostas asked about the apparent POC grav car at Dancado, the connection between the two murders and the attackers' apparent ability to disable local systems. Pallod called it all coincidence and nothing to worry about, then ended the call quickly.
- Kostas left a message with Pallod's secretary thanking him for closing the investigation and asking for an opportunity to face his uncle's alleged killer.
- Admiral Cassius's network reports that the captain of the [[Alahir]] has held numerous meetings with Pallod. This is not independently verified.
- On 195-1105 [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] called him to ask what had become of the surviving [[Dancado]] prisoner. **Pallod is no longer taking the crew's calls at all**, and nothing was learned.
- [[Detective Odoy]] contradicts Pallod's entire case the same evening: he says [[Ven Yasha]] killed Vlen, that the red-haired man is a fixer called [[Harlen]], and that the [[Zhodani]]-spy story is a cover for an [[Imperium|Imperial]] takeover of the inquiry. Whether Pallod is bought, coerced or merely sidelined is unknown.
