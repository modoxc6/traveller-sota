**Ally. Murdered.** "Uncle Vlen" — [[Kostas Backett]]'s extended family. Homeworld [[Alell]].

Human. Physiologically 52 at death; historical records put his true age at **at least 180**, with no anagathics in his blood — see [[Vlen Backett's True Age]]. Killed by a close-range gunshot to the heart while drugged senseless — see [[Vlen Backett's Murder]].

Took Kostas under his wing plenty of times throughout his troubled career — without Vlen's "loans" he may never have made it. The scare quotes are the character sheet's own; on what terms is unstated.

###### Character
Only at home when travelling. He'd visit Alell for a few weeks or a month or two, delighting friends and family with tales of distant planets and exotic cultures, then the wanderlust would take him off again. A charming old rogue — the more staid relatives disapproved of his questionable, unreliable ways, but he was every child's favourite uncle.

###### What he actually does
No-one was ever sure, or whether he was just a drifter amid the stars.
- Worked tramp traders, signed on as a deckhand on freighters
- Dabbled in archaeology and journalism
- Never liked to talk about it, but had quite a collection of firearms and could handle himself in a fight

###### His strange friends
Mostly other space voyagers — but he received irregular communications from scientists, naval officers, corporate oligarchs, even high nobility. On at least one occasion, [[Vargr]] from outside the [[Imperium]] travelled hundreds of parsecs to consult with him.

That detail reads differently now: the Vargr were **uplifted by [[The Ancients]]**, Vlen was funding a hunt for Ancients sites, and he was killed with a narcotic that came out of the Vargr extents.

None of these contacts are named. The archaeology plus the reach of that address book is a lot of smoke for a man with no visible job — worth pulling on.

###### What he was doing on Regina
He came to meet [[Iaros Ujinka]] and agree terms on [[Ujinka's Research]] — **he was going to provide the funding and a ship** for an expedition to sites of [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]]. He was killed three days after arriving, before the meeting happened.

[[Ven Yasha]]'s name kept cropping up in connection with him.

The last recoverable call from his [[Smashed Comms|smashed comms]] was to [[Winter Shipping]], a logistics and cargo-buying company operating in Credo City and Star Town.

###### Death
Murdered in [[Credo City]] on [[Regina]], late of Regina/[[Alell]]. The coroner's report gives the details ([[Vlen Backett's Murder]], [[Vlen Backett's True Age]]); the Commission's letter, below, never mentions that he was killed at all. Notified to [[Kostas Backett]] by [[Pallod Norrim]] of the [[Regina Public Order Commission]] — *Handout 2, "Message from Regina"*, sealed 190 Standard, 1105 [[Imperium|Third Imperium]].

- Before his death, Vlen left instructions on file with the Public Order Commission naming **Kostas as next of kin** — to resolve his outstanding affairs on Regina, take possession of his belongings, and transport his remains back to Alell.
- A sum of money is deposited with the [[First Ducal Bank]] to cover expenses.
- His body is in a cryogenic facility in Credo City, to be transferred to Kostas as soon as possible. *A TL14+ autodoc can reanimate a corpse kept in a low berth or cryoberth for up to 1D days after death — Vlen died on or before 190-1105 and it is now 193. Probably outside the window, and no one has raised it, but the body is in cryo and Kostas is the legal next of kin who takes possession of it.*
- **The body was transferred to the [[Event Horizon]]** before the crew left Regina, and is now with them at [[Alell]]. When and how the handover happened isn't recorded.
- Kostas is required, in the name of the Duke and under the Public Order Act of 527 (Amended), to travel to Regina with all due haste — or reply so alternate arrangements can be made.
- **"Given certain irregularities with Vlen Backett's affairs, it is vitally important that you contact the Public Order Commission upon arrival."** See [[Irregularities in Vlen Backett's Affairs]].

Two oddities in the letter itself: it's addressed to the "chattels and corpus of Vlen Backett (deceased)" rather than to Kostas by name, and the Chief Inspector signs off as **Pallod Norum** having opened as **Pallod Norrim**.

Pallod now claims that short, red-haired [[Darrian Jak]] murdered Vlen and considers the case closed. The crew have not independently verified that conclusion and distrust Pallod's dismissal of the connected events at Dancado.

###### The courier theory
On 195-1105 [[Detective Odoy]] told the crew that **Vlen was a courier for [[Ven Yasha]]**, and that **Yasha killed him** — not Jak. Odoy also says the rumour of a murdered [[Zhodani]] spy refers to Vlen, that Vlen was **not** a spy, and that the story is a cover for the [[Imperium|Imperial]] takeover of the inquiry.

All unverified. It would, though, account for a man with no visible job, constant travel, and an address book running from scientists to high nobility — and it sits alongside his last recovered call going to [[Winter Shipping]], where Odoy places the fixer [[Harlen]].

Unresolved: whether courier work and the funding of [[Ujinka's Research]] are the same thread or two separate lives.

###### Family and money on Alell — Tel's pre-session handout
What [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]'s parents told him, second-hand:
- His closest living kin on [[Alell]] are the [[Backett Family]] of [[Vaca Goya]]. [[Tomas Backett]] **never liked him** and is probably coming to see "the old charlatan" properly buried
- [[Hans Backett]] often talked about Vlen having **a fortune hidden in his old lodge**, and Kostas's parents are pretty sure Hans is looking for an angle. See [[Vlen's Hidden Fortune]]
- About ten years ago Vlen **borrowed money from [[Yusul Parad]]**, head of the [[Parad Family]], a local criminal gang. Kostas's parents are "pretty sure", not certain

The family expect a burial, and **the crew have the body**: it was transferred to the [[Event Horizon]] on [[Regina]] and is with them at Alell.
