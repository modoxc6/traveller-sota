[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A species found outside the [[Imperium]], in territory referred to as the **Vargr extents**. [[Tusal]] is believed to have been imported from there roughly a century ago.

**Uplifted from Terran canines by [[The Ancients]]** — which makes them one of the few living things with a direct claim on the Ancients' legacy, and the [[Church of the Chosen Ones]] builds a whole theology on exactly that.

###### Vargr in the file so far
- [[40th Squadron]] — organised Vargr power bloc in [[Gvurrdon]], previously bankrolled by the [[Zhodani]]
- [[Church of the Chosen Ones]] — fringe sect holding Vargr to be the Ancients' rightful heirs
- [[Menorb]] — agricultural world feeding the Vargr market, raided for it
- [[Rynkatropa]] — a TL1 sophont species the Vargr enslaved
- [[Tusal]] — the narcotic that killed [[Vlen Backett]] came out of the Vargr extents
- Vargr once crossed hundreds of parsecs to consult Vlen himself

On at least one occasion, Vargr travelled hundreds of parsecs to consult [[Vlen Backett]] — the single strangest thing in his address book, and the clearest sign his interests reach well beyond Imperial space.
