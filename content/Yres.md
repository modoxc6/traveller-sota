[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

The only surface structure is the entrance to the starport — an extremely impressive system of airlocks and tubes reaching from the surface to the seabed. The cities of this **balkanised** world are all underground and underwater.

- Visitors face an additional docking fee of **Cr250**, an obligatory donation to the Yres Reconstruction and War Widows Fund
- The atmosphere is **highly radioactive**; iodine tablets are available from public dispensers throughout the cities

A reconstruction fund and a war widows fund implies a war worth reconstructing from.
