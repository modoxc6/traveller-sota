#traveller #sota #ship
[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A ship that reportedly arrived at [[Regina Highport]] several weeks before the party's investigation.

It is **registered to the Scout Service but is not a standard scout design**. The ship and all other information about it came through [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]]'s network and have not been independently verified.

###### Connections
- Its unnamed captain reportedly held numerous meetings with [[Pallod Norrim]]
- Offworlders have reportedly been seen operating in [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] uniforms since the Alahir arrived
- The local POC are rumoured to be angry that an Imperial police force has taken over a murder inquiry
- A further rumour claims the murdered person was a [[Zhodani]] spy and the inquiry is now a state-security matter; the victim's identity and the claim itself remain unconfirmed

###### Odoy's account — 195-1105
[[Detective Odoy]] told the crew that the [[Imperium|Imperial]] force which arrived aboard the Alahir is **hunting [[Ven Yasha]]**, and that the [[Zhodani]]-spy rumour is a **fabrication** — the murder it refers to is [[Vlen Backett]]'s, and the spy framing exists to justify the Imperial takeover of the inquiry. He also says that force **took both [[Dancado]] attackers**, removing the surviving prisoner from POC custody.

Unverified, but it is consistent with the reported offworlders in [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] uniforms and with [[Pallod Norrim]] having gone unreachable.

###### Open questions
- What kind of ship it actually is
- Who commands and crews it
- Why its captain has been meeting Pallod
- Whether its personnel were involved at [[Dancado]] or used the grav car seen leaving the scene
