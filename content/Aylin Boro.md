**Contact.** Retired admiral. Hates [[Tyrus Cassius]].

[[Kostas Backett]] served under her right before he left the navy, so she remembers his face — but she has him confused with another idiot who was the son of her superior, so she has to be nice to him. She has no idea he's a loser dropout.

The mistaken identity is the whole basis of the relationship. If she works out who he actually is, the contact goes away.
