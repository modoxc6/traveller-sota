A **growth formula** held by [[Black Horizon]] aboard the [[Event Horizon]]. According to [[Kay]] (to Ozy, Advocate Effect +7, in bed), Black Horizon were carrying **cryogenically frozen [[Carthus|carthus]] embryos** and this serum separately. "Somehow the two of them met": the embryos **grew instantly**, broke containment and escaped into the maintenance shafts. That's why the carthuses were juveniles and weaker than adults.

- **All the embryo samples have been destroyed**, per Kay. **The serum has been kept**
- **Kay's offer:** keep the incident quiet and the serum might be made available to the crew, if it would be useful to them. Not answered yet

What else it does, what it was for, and how the two "met" by accident are all unexplained. It's Kay's account only.
