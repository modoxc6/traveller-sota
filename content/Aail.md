Representative of the **[[Brotherhood of Humaniti]]**, who arrived at Vlen's homecoming in [[Vaca Goya]] in [[Session 2026-10-18]]. *Name heard as "Ail"/"Ayl"; spelled Aail in the filename of the GM's image.*

![[aail.png|250]]

Came in **dancing**, in green robes (hers very revealing), shaved head, **three monks** behind her chanting a mantra while she spoke.

###### Her claim
> The initiate Zu, known to the secular world as Vlen Backett, was a member of our order some years ago. He fled our monastery in the dead of night and never returned. As part of his initiation he signed over all worldly goods to the Brotherhood of Humaniti. It has taken us some time to find Zu's homeworld, and now we are here. We claim what is rightfully ours.

*(Transcribed as "Zoo"; Rich spells it **Zu**.)*

The monks have **paperwork** for the signing-over. According to [[Porter Sajan]], they also want **their own ceremony for their initiate, "some DNA thing that will take hours"**. See [[Claims on Vlen's Estate]].

###### Manner
Hugs everyone, warmly and at length ("Blessed be the child", "we must protect you, sweet child"). [[Hans Backett]] was mortified. [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] hugged her and was left euphoric. He then realised **he may have passed her contact contamination from the plants he'd picked**, and said nothing.
