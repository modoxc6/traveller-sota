Receptionist at the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] — where [[Vlen Backett]] was staying when he was killed, and where the party are now staying themselves.

She and [[Kostas Backett]] have been lightly flirting.

After Kostas was shot at [[Dancado]], he tried to use the injury to win her sympathy. It did not work.

She was **not** on duty the night Vlen died — that was a different receptionist, since traced and interviewed, who had nothing useful to offer.

The desk sees everyone who comes and goes. She is the closest thing the party have to a source inside the building the murder happened in, and Kostas is the one talking to her.
