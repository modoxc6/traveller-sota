A **fixer**, red-haired, **connected to [[Winter Shipping]]**.

Named by [[Detective Odoy]] at the [[Meeting at the Flotsam]] on 195-1105 as the red-haired man in the case. Odoy's account is unverified.

###### Possible identity with Darrian Jak
[[Pallod Norrim]] arrested short, red-haired [[Darrian Jak]] for [[Vlen Backett]]'s murder and treats the case as closed. Odoy names the red-haired man as Harlen and says [[Ven Yasha]] is the killer.

**Whether Harlen and Darrian Jak are the same person, an alias, or two different red-haired men is not established.** Recorded separately until the table settles it. Both descriptions also broadly match the unseen red-haired third figure in [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]].

**The crew's working theory:** Harlen and Jak are *not* the same man. Jak is a **fall guy**, arrested purely because he is also red-haired and was conveniently to hand — which would make Harlen the real third man and Pallod's case a deliberate misdirection. This is the crew's inference, not established.
