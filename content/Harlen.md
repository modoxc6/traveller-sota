[[Secrets of the Ancients]]

A **fixer**, red-haired, who **ran [[Winter Shipping]]** for [[Ven Yasha]]. **Seen in person for the first time on 196-1105**, aboard the [[Winter Shipping Station|company's hidden base]] — and last seen there as it counted down to destruction.

Originally named by [[Detective Odoy]] at the [[Meeting at the Flotsam]] on 195-1105 as the red-haired man in the case, then confirmed from inside the company by [[Exceedingly Discreet]].

###### 196-1105 — aboard the base
He arrived late into the firefight on the upper level, **shot at [[Gunner Gunnerson]] and missed**, and began shouting to rally his remaining people.

Then he saw [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]. **His expression changed and he muttered "oh"** — he had only registered the others until that point. He looked across at **[[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]]' uniform**, said **"This is suboptimal"**, and **closed his eyes**.

**Every screen in the base went black.** An alarm sounded and a synthesised voice announced *destruct sequence activated, detonation imminent, all hands to re-entry capsules*, with floor lights marking a path to the capsule airlock. Thirty seconds. He stood there with his eyes shut through it.

He legged it before [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] could order him taken alive. [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]], opening doors on the way to the capsules, found him **sat at his desk, eyes closed, fingers steepled, muttering "this is not the end" over and over**. Ozy took the [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals|statuette and data disks]] off the desk and left him there.

**Nobody saw him board a capsule and nobody saw him die.** The base was destroyed roughly twenty seconds after the crew launched.

###### He controls things without touching them
The station obeyed him with **no console, no keypad and no spoken command** — he shut his eyes and the screens died and the destruct ran. This is not explained. Candidates: **psionics** (see [[Zhodani]], and the [[Imperium]]'s attitude to psionic talent), a neural implant tied into the base's systems, or something to do with [[The Ancients]].

Worth setting against **[[The Device]]** carried by the third man at [[Dancado]], which caused electrical interference nobody could account for, and against the **localised systems failures** [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] asked [[Pallod Norrim]] about and was told were coincidence.

###### He knew the crew on sight
He recognised **Kostas** without being told who he was, and recognised **Cassius' uniform**. Either the company was briefed on who was coming, or he had seen Vlen's family before — Kostas is Vlen's nephew and next of kin.

###### From inside the company — 196-1105
Per [[Exceedingly Discreet]], the [[Llellewyoly]] running [[Winter Shipping]]'s records, all unverified:
- **Harlen ran the company**, and **[[Ven Yasha]] hired them all**
- **Short-sighted and a poor tactician**, but **loyal to Ven Yasha** and not for turning
- No particular weakness — "he is just a man, though"
- **He held [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals]]** — volunteered unprompted, because Kostas is Vlen's nephew

Exceedingly Discreet wanted Harlen dead and the company for itself, and made that the price of flying the crew up. **The crew agreed to kill him.** In the event the base blew up with him apparently still in it — which satisfies the deal only by assumption, and Exceedingly Discreet does not yet know any of it.

###### Possible identity with Darrian Jak
[[Pallod Norrim]] arrested short, red-haired [[Darrian Jak]] for [[Vlen Backett]]'s murder and treats the case as closed. Odoy names the red-haired man as Harlen and says [[Ven Yasha]] is the killer.

**The crew's working theory:** Harlen and Jak are *not* the same man. Jak is a **fall guy**, arrested purely because he is also red-haired and was conveniently to hand — which would make Harlen the real third man in [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]] and Pallod's case a deliberate misdirection. Nothing seen aboard the base settles this either way; Jak is still in POC custody and Harlen was never questioned.
