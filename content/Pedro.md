**[[Black Horizon]] security guard** aboard the [[Event Horizon]]. A stat-block NPC with a stunner, sent into the maintenance shafts with the crew to recover Black Horizon's escaped [[Carthus|carthuses]]. Named at the table ("Pedro to Ops"). He's probably the same unnamed security officer who went in with them last session, and the GM's token calls him "Armed Security Guard, Black Horizon".

Missed with his stunner more often than he hit, but **stunned two of the carthuses**, which makes him the reason any were taken alive. Kept telling the crew "we need to keep them alive… I'm going to get it in the neck for this". When Ozy tried to get more out of him: "above my pay grade, mate". Asked how many there were: "a handful". Married.

Went ahead down a corridor alone and had **a massive chunk bitten out of his shoulder (13 damage)**. His own first aid failed because his hands were slipping in the blood. Attacked again, he dived prone, "crying for his mummy". Survived and called it in.
