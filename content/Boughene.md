[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

A scout base is established here as part of continued surveying operations coreward and spinward of the [[Imperium]].

There is little else in the system of interest to Travellers, and **the scout base is not open to visitors**.

###### The g-band footprint — from [[Ujinka's Research]]
Officially nothing here. Ujinka's notes say otherwise:
- The **1102 survey** logged a g-band drop-off around the Boughene precursor ruins, the same footprint found in the [[Regina]] sub-sector
- Her own log (Entry 884-A) orbiting **Boughene IV**, a gas giant with a high metal-rich asteroid density: sensor packet degradation at 4.2 GHz and a 0.03-second clock-drift on the nav computers
- A **trader pulled a statue from the asteroid belt** — Ujinka judged it a fragment of a larger anchor array. [[Vlen Backett]] borrowed it and it is now [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals|aboard the Event Horizon]]
- "Could something else be hidden away in this system?" — and **Vlen intended to scan it** with Ujinka's g-band sensor system, per his own disks

A closed scout base, a system described as uninteresting, and an Ancient anchor array in its asteroid belt.
