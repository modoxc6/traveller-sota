[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A forested region north of [[Credo City]], on [[Regina]] — **private property of the Duke of Regina**. Home of [[Iaros Ujinka]].

> [!important] Whose land this is
> The [[Regina Public Order Commission]] acts "in the name of his grace the Duke". A professor was shot dead on the Duke's own private ground, with a Commission grav car parked outside; the party killed one intruder and subdued another there. Ujinka living on ducal land at all says something about her standing — or her patronage.

**[[Iaros Ujinka]] was shot dead here, through a window, while meeting the party.** They had been tailed to the estate by a [[Regina Public Order Commission]] grav car — see [[Being Followed]].

**The attack:** power cut, security drone destroyed, [[Iaros Ujinka]] shot dead through the upstairs lounge window, front door broken down. Two men came upstairs; **one was killed and the other knocked unconscious** — the same two from the [[Three Men at Vlen's Room|hostel footage]]. A third shooter stayed outside throughout and never entered.

The authorities took control of the scene and the surviving intruder entered POC custody. Before they arrived, he mentioned [[Ven Yasha]] during a brief questioning by the crew.

###### Aftermath
- [[Kostas Backett]] and [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] got Ujinka to the autodoc, but it was too late to revive her
- StClair treated Kostas, restoring 1 END and bringing him to END 1
- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] recovered data concerning [[Ujinka's Research]], Vlen and the g-band work; its specific contents are not yet known
- [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] reported the situation to [[Pallod Norrim]]
- Arriving officers said they were the only POC personnel in the area and claimed the crew must have misidentified the grav car seen leaving
- The crew returned to the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] after the authorities arrived

###### The autodoc (room K, upper floor)
Same floor as the lounge, where [[Iaros Ujinka]] was shot. An autodoc is a self-contained diagnostic, pharmaceutical and surgical system the size of a large coffin — **stationary and 500kg**, so patients come to it, not the other way around. The crew got Ujinka into it, but **the attempt came too late to revive her**.

| TL | Autodoc | Skills | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | Basic | Medic 3 | Diagnosis, drug delivery, surgery |
| 14 | Improved | Medic 3, Science (biology) 3 | **Reanimation**, integral low berth, bioreactor that regrows limbs and organs |
| 15 | Advanced | Medic 4, Investigate/Science 4 | Fabrication chamber, tissue regeneration, simple augments, advanced sensors |

Even the basic model has **Medic 3**, better than anyone in the party — it treats "as efficiently as a qualified doctor".

**If this one is TL14 or better, it can reanimate**, provided no more than 2D minutes have passed since death. Doubled in extremely cold conditions, or extended to 1D days if the body is put in a low berth or cryoberth. Revival survival checks still apply.

**Ujinka died minutes ago, one room away from it.**

###### The house
![[dancado.png]]

**Ground floor** (right on the map) — entrance hallway, dining room, kitchen, utility room, bathroom, storerooms, **holoroom**, a very large **library**, carport, **boathouse**.

**Upper floor** (left) — lounge, **study**, **workroom**, **autodoc**, four bedrooms, two bathrooms, storerooms.

Worth noting for play:
- A boathouse means water at the property line — a second way in, and a second way out
- The carport is open to the outside; the grav car tailing the party has somewhere obvious to sit and wait
- Two staircases connect the floors
- The library dominates the ground floor and the study and workroom sit directly above it — this is a working scholar's house, and whatever Ujinka wanted to discuss with [[Vlen Backett]] is likely in one of those three rooms
- The autodoc is a private one, upstairs
