Person of interest on the investigation board, alongside [[Pallod Norrim]].

**He's the mysterious man who wants to meet the party at [[The Flotsam]] at 10pm on 195-1105** — due tonight; see [[Meeting at the Flotsam]].

Dark unkempt hair, stubble, worn dark jacket — nothing like Norrim's uniform. A detective who arranges meetings in a bar at night rather than summoning people to an office is either working around his own organisation or isn't with one.

Not yet recorded: which agency he belongs to, whether he's on [[Vlen Backett's Murder]], and how he got word to the party.

###### What he told the crew — 195-1105
The meeting happened. Odoy is **investigating [[Ven Yasha]]**, and his account contradicts [[Pallod Norrim]]'s official case at every point. **All of the following is Odoy's claim and none of it is independently verified:**

- **[[Ven Yasha]] killed [[Vlen Backett]]** — not [[Darrian Jak]], whom the POC have arrested for it
- **Vlen was a courier for Ven Yasha**
- **No [[Zhodani]] spy was killed.** The murder behind that rumour *is* Vlen's, and he was not a spy — the spy story is a **cover** used to justify the [[Imperium|Imperial]] police takeover of the inquiry
- **Imperial security have been everywhere** on [[Regina]]
- The Imperial force that arrived aboard the **[[Alahir]] is hunting Ven Yasha**
- **Both [[Dancado]] attackers are in Imperial hands** — the prisoner is no longer in POC custody, and the Imperials also collected the dead man
- The red-haired man is a fixer called **[[Harlen]]**, **connected to [[Winter Shipping]]**
- He knows little about [[Iaros Ujinka]] or why she would be killed

###### Open questions

- Whether [[Harlen]] and [[Darrian Jak]] are the same man
- Why he approached this crew rather than working through his own organisation
