[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A station [[Winter Shipping]] holds **in orbit**, revealed by [[Exceedingly Discreet]] in the small hours of 196-1105. Referred to as "the station we have in orbit" and "the base" — whether it is a separate structure or a leased section of [[Regina Highport]] is not established.

**[[Harlen]] is there.**

Almost certainly the destination of the **launch runs that take hours longer than a surface-to-Highport trip should** — the anomaly [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] found in the hacked flight manifests.

[[Exceedingly Discreet]] has agreed to put the crew on the company launch and set its flight path there. It warns that the crew will have to **deal with the staff on board as well as [[Harlen]]**, and that they will need weapons — which is why the launch is to rendezvous with the [[Event Horizon]] in orbit first.

###### Open questions
- What it is, how big, and how many people are aboard
- What the overlong runs are carrying
- Whether it is on any official register
