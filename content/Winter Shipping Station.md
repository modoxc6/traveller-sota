[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

**Destroyed on 196-1105.** [[Harlen]] triggered its self-destruct while the crew were aboard; the crew escaped by capsule and watched it go.

Called "the station we have in orbit" and "the base" by [[Exceedingly Discreet]], which flew the crew up to it. **It was never a station.** It was **three shipping containers bolted together**, hidden inside a container yard at a **semi-stable Lagrangian point** between [[Regina]] and [[Assiniboia]] — the same sort of point [[Assiniboia]] is noted for. Anything parked there stays put for years.

The approach ran through a **constellation of cargo containers, thousands of them**, docked into long lines and cubes with some drifting free and untethered, scattered with nav buoys and warning beacons. The launch weaved into the heart of the yard, dipped beneath a **trio of old containers**, and a **concealed hatch** opened to dock it inside. Nothing about it was visible or registered as a structure.

This was the destination of the **launch runs that took hours longer than a surface-to-Highport trip should** — the anomaly [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] found in the hacked flight manifests.

###### What was inside
- **No lights, anywhere.** The staff knew their way around in the dark
- Doors **barely airtight** — on a structure in vacuum — and everything cobbled together from salvage
- A dock bay of roughly 20 by 50 feet holding the launch and a few sealed crates
- An upper level reached by an airlock and ladder, with cover scattered through it, an office, and what looked like a **gun emplacement**
- **[[Harlen]]'s office**, with a **statuette and data disks** on the desk — see [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals]]
- A capsule deck with **several escape capsules**, each big enough for five. Two were locked and jammed when the smugglers reached them
- A crew of **smugglers** — the GM's word for them — the number never established. At least seven died

###### The destruct
[[Harlen]] saw [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]], recognised him, looked at [[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]]' uniform, said "This is suboptimal" and **closed his eyes**. Every screen in the base went black, an alarm sounded, and a synthesised voice announced *destruct sequence activated, detonation imminent, all hands to re-entry capsules*, with floor arrows lighting a route to the capsule airlock.

**Thirty seconds.** No console, no keypad, no spoken command — see [[Harlen]].

About twenty seconds after the capsule launched the base **imploded, scrunched up and then blew outward**. The capsule rode the edge of the wave down to [[Regina]] and landed in wild land outside the Twin Cities, at what looked like a designated recovery point with grav bikes waiting.

###### What went with it
- Whatever [[Winter Shipping]] was actually moving through the overlong runs — never established
- Any records held on the base rather than in [[Exceedingly Discreet]]'s head in [[Credo City|Credo]]
- Most of its staff
- Possibly [[Harlen]] himself, who was last seen at his desk as the count ran down
