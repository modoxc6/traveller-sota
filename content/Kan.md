[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

Site named in [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s Research Note 3 (see [[Ujinka's Research]]): the **Vilani records of thousands of years ago logged the site at Kan as "just another precursor society"** — shimmering glass harder than diamond, containers of unblemished un-corroded cobalt, classified as dead rubbish. Ujinka's conference notes take it as proof the Vilani missed what [[The Ancients]] were.

Where Kan is, and whether it's a world or a site on one, is not stated.
