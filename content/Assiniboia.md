The gas giant that [[Regina]] orbits — Regina being technically a moon, not a planet. Assiniboia in turn orbits the locus of the primary star Lusor and its companion Speck; a third star, a red dwarf, runs a long eccentric orbit.

**Lagrangian points:** one of five positions in a three-body orbital configuration where a small object affected only by gravity stays stationary relative to the two larger bodies. With Assiniboia's multiple moons, the system has **several semi-stable points** — the natural places to park something you don't want found.
