**Imperial officer, captain of the [[Alahir]]**, said to hold a special portfolio in Imperial Intelligence. **[[Tyrus Cassius|The Admiral]]'s rival.**

*Spelling per Rich: **Gand Holsiss**. Heard as "Gand Hulses" in [[Session 2026-08-23]] and written up as "Gand Holsiss" after [[Session 2026-10-25]]; the Admiral's sheet has "Gan Halsis".*

###### The Admiral's rival
On the Admiral's character sheet: **a tutor he shamed at the academy** by proving his theory wrong. Holsiss left under a cloud and changed career. The Admiral: "that bloody tutor who turned up early in my naval career." He's heard Holsiss holds a big grudge. The connection to the Alahir was confirmed by the GM in [[Session 2026-10-25]]; the Admiral had missed the name sessions earlier.

###### Vlen, ten years ago
Per [[Marisa Jocell]] ([[Session 2026-10-25]]): a few hours after [[Vlen Backett]] hid the [[Star Hunter]] at the [[Alell Starport]], saying he was being followed, **an Imperial Scout ship arrived, piloted by "this very strange officer", Gand Holsiss.** He questioned Vlen about where he'd been and where his ship was. Vlen denied everything. [[Bard Jocell]] remembered the name.

So the Imperial interest in Vlen goes back at least ten years, and the same man was on [[Regina]] when Vlen died. *Inference: whether he was the one following Vlen ten years ago isn't stated.*
