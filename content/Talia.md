**Steward of the [[Event Horizon]]**, Steward 3 — "one of the better stewards you'll ever get". Known aboard as "Talia the underpaid": on Cr2,000 a month until 196-1105, when five high passengers landed on her and a Perception check made it obvious she was pissed off. The crew **doubled her pay to Cr4,000**; she went "from not trusting you to mildly suspicious".

Runs the ship's day-to-day while the crew are away — reported a quiet few days over the [[Regina]] stop. Two or three days into the jump to [[Forboldn]] she came onto the bridge to ask for help with the **[[Helix]] / [[Black Horizon]] ownership row** — settled by Ozy that week, to her relief. In the second jump her Steward check (8) ran the passenger headcount that found the empty skin suit — see [[Five High Passengers]]. Makes coffee, but not as well as Kostas.

Name heard as Talia / Talley; spelling unconfirmed.
