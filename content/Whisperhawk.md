*An animal.* One of the most common sights in the [[Alell]] jungle — beautiful avians with prodigious wingspans.

Their wings are both locomotion and feeding organs: whisperhawks feed by digesting the insects caught on the sticky undersides. A flock gliding across the canopy at twilight catches hundreds of thousands of insects; the buzzing of the trapped insects and the susurrus of the wind gives the whisperhawk its name.

They are **notoriously stupid** and have been known to attack humans and vehicles, as well as flagposts, fences, houses and small trees. If threatened, a whisperhawk attacks by wrapping its wings around a larger foe and **trying to smother it**.
