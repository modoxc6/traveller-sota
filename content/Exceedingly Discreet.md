A **[[Llellewyoly]]** — a "Dandelion" — found working alone in a sealed environmental chamber in [[Winter Shipping]]'s closed [[Credo City|Credo]]-side building, in the small hours of 196-1105.

**"Exceedingly Discreet" is the title it gave when asked**, not necessarily a name: *"I am exceedingly discreet. What is your title at this time, strangers?"* Llellewyoly hold dozens of formal titles at once, so this may be one of many.

It calls itself **the brains of the operation**. It works an enormous volume of data across pads and monitors on a **closed system with no external route in** — which is why [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]]'s remote hack of Winter Shipping found an almost empty server. It also controls the company launch's flight path from that console.

It addresses people by titles it invents for them: "overly dressed pompous man" ([[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]]), "pale-skinned smartly dressed man" ([[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]]), "downtrodden depressed man" ([[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]), "unruly-haired rude scientist man" ([[Graxius StClair|StClair]], after greeting it as a "ballsack with chicken legs").

###### The deal — 196-1105
It saw through two cover stories and responded to the truth. Purely transactional, and openly ambitious.

**It gives:** passage on the [[Winter Shipping]] launch and up to the [[Winter Shipping Station|company station in orbit]], with the flight path set from its own console and [[Marid]] kept unaware. It suggested the launch **meet the [[Event Horizon]] in orbit** so the crew can arm themselves, since Credo's Law Level 9 makes that impossible on the ground.

**It gets:** a guarantee that **[[Harlen]] does not survive**, and silence about the conversation. It intends to take over [[Winter Shipping]] itself, and offers its own ambition as the crew's guarantee that it won't turn them in.

###### What it says — all unverified
- **[[Harlen]] runs the company** — "short-sighted and a poor tactician", but loyal to [[Ven Yasha]] and not for turning. "He is just a man, though"
- **[[Ven Yasha]] hired all of them**
- **Ven Yasha ordered [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s death.** It does not understand why and says so — better understanding of Ancient technology would only raise the value of Ancient artefacts. It doesn't question Yasha's requests
- **[[Vlen Backett]] was a messenger for Ven Yasha** — "he must have brought bad news, hence his demise"
- It was aware of both murders
- **[[Harlen]] holds [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals]]** — volunteered unprompted, because [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] is Vlen's nephew
- **Imperial Intelligence** were on [[Regina]] in numbers, arrived aboard the [[Alahir]], and **have left**; their business is concluded. The captain — name heard as "Gand Hulses", unconfirmed — is said to hold a special portfolio in the intelligence services. It does not know whether they came for Yasha
- On what Ancient technology is worth: nobody understands the secrets of [[The Ancients]], but the potential runs to immortality, taking systems, taking the [[Imperium]], destroying armies. It knows of no specific plan of Yasha's
- **It will not discuss [[Ven Yasha]] at all** — "for my own survival" — while freely offering [[Harlen]], the hired thugs, and Imperial Intelligence

###### Open questions
- Whether it means to honour the deal, or is arranging for the crew and Harlen to remove each other
- What is on the closed system it guards
- What it actually knows about [[Ven Yasha]] and is holding back
- Whether it is a willing employee or, as [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] wondered, effectively a prisoner
