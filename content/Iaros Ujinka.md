**Professor Iaros Ujinka**, Department of History, [[University of Regina]]. Lives at [[Dancado]], an estate just outside [[Credo City]].

**[[Graxius StClair]]'s mentor** — she was very supportive of him. At some point she went off to work with StClair's ex-wife, which appears to be a sore subject.

The party met her at Dancado after being followed there by a grav car apparently in [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] colours — see [[Being Followed]]. She was murdered during that meeting.

Her work, and what she wanted from Vlen, is in [[Ujinka's Research]]: links between [[G-Band Transmissions]] and sites of [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]]. Before her death, she asked whether the party would put the [[Event Horizon]] behind it. **No answer given yet.**

On [[Ven Yasha]] — whose name kept cropping up in connection with Vlen — she knows him only by reputation, and says he is very unpleasant.

###### The letter
Found among [[Vlen Backett]]'s effects, addressed to him care of the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]]:

> I have returned from [[Beck's World]], and am now available for that meeting we discussed at your convenience. If you would care to visit me at my home on Dancado, I shall be delighted to receive you and discuss topics of mutual interest.

This is the lead that sent the party to Dancado.

###### What it establishes
- Vlen and Ujinka **already knew each other** and had a meeting arranged before Vlen ever reached [[Regina]] — "that meeting we discussed"
- The "topics of mutual interest" turn out to be [[Ujinka's Research]] — Vlen was to provide funding and a ship
- Ujinka had been away on Beck's World and had only just got back
- A drifter with no visible profession was corresponding with a university historian — which fits Vlen's dabbling in archaeology and his unnamed contacts among scientists
- Vlen may well have been on Regina *for this meeting*. He never made it.

###### Open questions
- Whether anyone else knew about the meeting — the letter was sitting in a hostel room that three men walked into
- Whether she knew Vlen and StClair were connected, or whether two of her contacts turning up in the same story is coincidence
- How much she knew about where Vlen's money came from
- Why [[Ven Yasha]]'s name attaches to Vlen at all
