Player character. Crew of the [[Event Horizon]].

###### Appearance
![[ozymandias.png|250]]

Lean, sharp-featured, short fair hair, tinted glasses. Long dark coat over a three-piece suit — waistcoat, tie, worn leather belt — and a small satchel at the hip. Carries a tablet in one hand, the other in a pocket. Reads as the one who handles the paperwork and the talking.

- After the failed attempt to revive Ujinka, Ozy began searching her library for notes relevant to [[Ujinka's Research]].
- He recovered unspecified data concerning Ujinka's Ancients research, [[Vlen Backett]] and the [[G-Band Transmissions|g-band work]].
- After several attempts to repair Vlen's [[Smashed Comms|smashed comms]], he extracted the last-dialled number immediately before the device became unusable. It belongs to [[Winter Shipping]].
