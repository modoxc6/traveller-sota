#traveller #sota #session
[[Secrets of the Ancients]]

Session date: 2026-09-06
In-fiction date: 196-1105, from the small hours through to the morning

###### Starting position
The crew are inside [[Winter Shipping]]'s closed [[Credo City|Credo]]-side office in the small hours of 196-1105, deal struck with [[Exceedingly Discreet]]: passage up to the company's orbital base in exchange for [[Harlen]]'s death.

- The route up is arranged — the company launch, flight path set from Exceedingly Discreet's console, [[Marid]] unaware
- The crew have no weapons; [[Regina]] is Law Level 9
- [[Kostas Backett]] is badly injured at STR 3, DEX 9, END 1
- [[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals]] are said to be in [[Harlen]]'s hands, aboard the base

###### Live notes
- **[[Graxius StClair|StClair]] sat the raid out.** He went back to the [[Event Horizon]] in a mood to do research, leaving the two least effective combat characters to board a hostile installation. He handed [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] a **medikit** on the way out
- The [[Implacable]] had already returned to the [[Event Horizon]] — its mooring fees at [[Regina Highport]] were only paid so far. It met the launch in orbit as planned and the crew armed there
- **Backup: [[Gunner Gunnerson]]**, the [[Event Horizon]]'s gunner — a grizzled ex-Marine in a filthy boiler suit, chewing a cigar, rolled up as *elite*. Announced himself with "did someone call for backup?" Run by [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]' player as a second character. Stealth −4; he does not sneak
- **The base is not a station.** The launch flew out to a semi-stable **Lagrangian point** between [[Regina]] and [[Assiniboia]], into a **container yard** — thousands of cargo containers docked into long lines and cubes, some drifting free and untethered, nav buoys and warning beacons among them. The launch dipped beneath a trio of old containers, a **concealed hatch** opened, and it docked inside
- Interior: **three shipping containers bolted together.** No lights anywhere — the staff know their way around in the dark. Doors barely airtight, everything cobbled together. The dock bay held only the launch and a few sealed crates
- **First firefight — the dock level.** [[Gunner Gunnerson|Gunnerson]] took point, opened the door and got a **surprise round** on a man standing with his back turned, mid-conversation with someone in the toilet
	- Gunnerson's opening shot tore into his back for 19 and left him at END 0, still standing
	- Kostas fired [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]]'s gun for 9 once the laser sight and P-HUD bonuses were found — the table spent a while working out that the sheet wasn't including them
	- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] leaned through the doorway at −1 and hit the ceiling
	- **[[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]] rolled Leadership for Effect 5** — five DM+1 tokens to hand out over the fight, and they carried most of the party's hits
	- Gunnerson aimed and finished him for 35 damage, at which point the GM **retconned his laser rifle down to a carbine** for being wildly overpowered. Reduced to 27; the man's top half was obliterated
	- The second man burst out of the toilet still pulling his trousers up. **Kostas shot him for 15** — "this one's for the Admiral" — the Effect wiping out his protection entirely. He was flung back into the toilet, dropped his gun and slipped in the mess. Cassius finished him with the machine gun
	- The bodies yielded a stub pistol and some cigarettes. Nothing else
- The machine gun was loud. Movement was heard on the **floor above**, reached by an airlock and ladder
- **Second firefight — the upper level.** A long cover fight against the base's **smugglers**, most shots hitting cover rather than people. Gunnerson went up first, called down that he could see two or three, and fought from cover the whole way
	- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] missed repeatedly, then discovered his **damage dice were configured wrong on Roll20** — rolling a flat +3 instead of 3D6. Corrected mid-fight
	- One smuggler charged out, **slipped on his mate's corpse** and went prone at −6. Kostas gut-shot him dead
	- Cassius came up, dropped one with the Gauss rifle for 9 through no cover, and another later with a body shot
	- **Kostas was shot for 3 through his flak jacket (Protection 5)** — enough to take **END to 0** and put him on STR. He was hit again shortly after
- **Kostas' self-treatment failed.** With no doctor in reach, he used StClair's medikit (TL10, DM+1) on himself and rolled **Effect −4**. The GM checked the rules, found nothing that defines the consequence of a failed first aid, and **homeruled it**: no characteristics regained, no further damage — "killing yourself with a third medic roll is insane" — and the **medikit is spent**. StClair owes himself a new one
- [[Gunner Gunnerson|Gunnerson]] later broke off to bandage Kostas for **1 point**, taken on END
- **[[Harlen]] appeared.** A **red-haired** man, arriving late into the fight. He shot at Gunnerson and missed, then began shouting to rally his people
	- Then **he saw Kostas** — and his expression changed. He muttered "oh". He looked across at **[[Tyrus Cassius|the Admiral]]'s uniform**, said **"This is suboptimal"**, and **closed his eyes**
	- **Every screen in the base went black.** An alarm sounded and a synthesised voice announced: *destruct sequence activated, detonation imminent, all hands to re-entry capsules.* Floor lights lit a path of white arrows to an airlock, which popped open
	- **Thirty seconds to detonation.** Harlen simply stood there with his eyes closed
- The crew debated staying to grab something versus running. **Cassius made the executive decision to go.** Kostas wanted Harlen taken alive — "shoot him in the legs, Gunner" — reasoning he wouldn't blow a station he was still standing on. Harlen legged it before anyone could act on it
- **The launch was never an escape route.** It was on an automated flight path controlled remotely by [[Exceedingly Discreet]], and had already detached. The only way off was the **escape capsules**
- Down at the capsule deck the surviving smugglers were trying the hatches; the first two were **locked and jammed**. Kostas ran past them and a capsule **lit green and opened** — big enough for five. He held it and waved his gun at anyone approaching
- **[[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] went door-to-door on the way out.** One door opened onto what looked like the base's **gun emplacement**. The next opened onto **[[Harlen]] at his desk** — eyes closed, fingers steepled, muttering **"this is not the end"** over and over
	- On the desk: a **statuette** Ozy recognised as the same sort of thing that was in [[Iaros Ujinka|Professor Ujinka]]'s house, and a set of **data disks**
	- **He grabbed both and ran.** He did not touch Harlen
- Gunnerson killed a smuggler at the capsules and took a parting shot at the last one as the capsule launched. The crew left the final smuggler alive, still trying to force a jammed hatch
- **The base died about twenty seconds out** — imploded, scrunched up, then blew outward. The capsule rode the edge of the wave, guided itself down, and landed in wild land on the outskirts of the **Twin Cities** on [[Regina]]. Grav bikes were waiting at what looked like a designated recovery point
- GM: **"You've completed Chapter 1."**

###### Decisions and discoveries
- **[[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals]] are recovered** — the party's stated objective for the raid, achieved in the last thirty seconds of it. Presumed Vlen's on strong circumstantial grounds; nobody in the fiction confirmed it
- **The statuette resembles [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s Ancient material.** The first physical object linking [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]], [[Ujinka's Research]] and [[Ven Yasha]]'s organisation
- **[[Winter Shipping Station|The "station" was a hidden base]]** — three containers bolted together, buried in a Lagrangian container yard, unlit and unregistered. **It is destroyed.**
- **[[Harlen]] triggered the self-destruct by closing his eyes.** Screens blacked out and the station obeyed him with no console, no keypad and no spoken command. Psionics, an implant, or something else is not established — but the GM flagged his red hair repeatedly and gave him "this is not the end" as an exit line
- **Harlen recognised Kostas on sight**, and recognised Cassius' uniform. He knew who they were before they said a word
- **Harlen's fate is unknown.** He was last seen at his desk as the countdown ran, and the base was destroyed. Nobody saw him board a capsule and nobody saw him die. The promise to [[Exceedingly Discreet]] is fulfilled only by assumption
- **[[Gunner Gunnerson]] carried the fight.** Both firefights turned on his shooting, and he twice broke off to drag or patch [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]
- The **failed medic check** is now settled as a house rule: no gain, no extra harm, equipment consumed

###### End position
- Morning of 196-1105, standing in wild land outside the Twin Cities on [[Regina]] with grav bikes available and a ride back into the city
- **[[Kostas Backett]] at roughly STR 1, DEX 2, END 1** — three damaged characteristics, needs **surgery**, and cannot heal on rest. The [[Event Horizon]]'s sickbay is the fix
- The party hold a **statuette and data disks** neither of them has examined
- [[Winter Shipping]]'s orbital base, its records of whatever moved through it, and most of its staff are gone. [[Exceedingly Discreet]] is still at its console in [[Credo City|Credo]], and does not yet know
- [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s request that the [[Event Horizon]] back [[Ujinka's Research]] is **still unanswered**
