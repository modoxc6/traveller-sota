###### Starting position
The crew are back at the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] on 195-1105, the day of their 10pm meeting with [[Detective Odoy]] at [[The Flotsam]].

- [[Iaros Ujinka]] is confirmed dead after the attack at [[Dancado]]. One attacker died; the survivor is in POC custody after mentioning [[Ven Yasha]] under questioning. The third man is still loose
- A grav car apparently in [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] colours left Dancado immediately after the attack; local officers deny any other POC unit was present
- [[Pallod Norrim]] claims [[Darrian Jak]], a short redhead, murdered [[Vlen Backett]] and treats the case as closed. The crew do not consider that proven and found his manner suspicious
- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] holds research data recovered from Ujinka's house (contents unspecified) and pulled the last-dialled number from [[Smashed Comms|Vlen's comms]] before the device failed — it belongs to [[Winter Shipping]]
- Admiral [[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]] learned of the [[Alahir]], its captain's repeated meetings with Pallod, offworlders reportedly wearing POC uniforms, and rumours of an Imperial state-security takeover of the murder inquiry
- [[Kostas Backett]] remains badly injured: STR 3, DEX 9, END 1
- Ujinka's request that the [[Event Horizon]] back [[Ujinka's Research]] is still unanswered

###### Live notes
- The crew split to work the [[Winter Shipping]] lead: [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] to research the company and attempt a remote hack of its systems, while [[Kostas Backett]] goes to the premises in person to recon the building. Kostas is still badly injured (END 1)
- [[Winter Shipping]]'s premises are two buildings, one either side of the fence between [[Credo City]] and [[Startown|Star Town]]. The crew are approaching from the Credo side
- Kostas rolled well and located the offices. The Credo-side building is closed, with a notice directing callers to the Star Town offices, which are visible across the fence
- A huge brute of a man entered the Credo-side building and emerged on the Star Town side — a crossing that is not supposed to be possible. This implies a connection or passage through the fence between the two buildings
- Kostas and [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] scoped out the security system. It looks unsophisticated; the crew believe they could shut down the alarms if they decide to break in
- Ozy successfully hacked [[Winter Shipping]]'s systems remotely, but found almost nothing of interest — suspiciously little. The crew suspect the real records sit on a separate, unconnected system
- The Star Town side operates a launch that runs up to [[Regina Highport]]
- Ozy found flight records showing that some of those launch runs take hours longer than the trip should
- The crew decided against breaking in for now. Plan: cross into [[Startown|Star Town]], walk into the [[Winter Shipping]] offices with a legitimate piece of business as cover, and see what they can learn face to face
- [[Kostas Backett]] bought a dagger in [[Startown|Star Town]] for protection. [[Regina]] is Law Level 9, where *all* weapons are banned — carrying it is illegal anywhere on the moon. The crew judge Star Town lawless enough to carry it there, and Kostas intends to drop it before returning to [[Credo City]]
- Inside, the Star Town [[Winter Shipping]] office is very fancy — far smarter than the surroundings suggest. Behind the desk is **Marid**, a very attractive receptionist
- The legitimate business used as cover was the crew's cargo from [[Yori]]: 3 tons of crystals and gems, bought for Cr54,000 and sold for Cr72,000 — a Cr18,000 profit. That is the [[Event Horizon]]'s entire 3-ton hold
- The sale is to [[Winter Shipping]] itself. **Olso**, the crew's Winter Shipping contact, will be picking the cargo up — giving the crew a legitimate reason to observe Winter Shipping personnel and their collection process
- [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] called [[Pallod Norrim]] to try to find out what happened to the surviving [[Dancado]] intruder taken into POC custody
- [[Pallod Norrim]] is no longer taking the crew's calls. Nothing was learned about the prisoner
- The crew set off for the 10pm [[Meeting at the Flotsam|meeting with Detective Odoy]] at [[The Flotsam]]
- [[Detective Odoy]] tells the crew he is investigating [[Ven Yasha]], and that he believes **Ven Yasha killed [[Vlen Backett]]** — directly contradicting [[Pallod Norrim]]'s official line that [[Darrian Jak]] is the murderer. Odoy's claim is unverified
- Odoy's theory is that [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]] was working as a **courier for [[Ven Yasha]]**. Unverified, but it would fit Vlen's constant travel and improbable contacts
- Further from Odoy (all his account, unverified):
	- **No [[Zhodani]] spy was killed.** The murder behind the rumour *is* [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]], and he was not a spy — the spy story is a cover used to justify the Imperial police takeover of the inquiry
	- **[[Imperium|Imperial]] security have been everywhere** on [[Regina]]
	- The Imperial force that arrived aboard the [[Alahir]] are **after [[Ven Yasha]]**
	- Odoy knows little about [[Iaros Ujinka]] or why she would be killed
	- **The [[Dancado]] prisoner is no longer in POC custody** — and the Imperial force also collected the other man from that attack
	- The red-haired man appears to be a fixer called **Harlen**, who is **connected to [[Winter Shipping]]**
- Open question: [[Pallod Norrim]] named the short redhead as [[Darrian Jak]] and arrested him; Odoy names the red-haired man as Harlen. Whether Jak and Harlen are the same person, two different redheads, or an alias is **not established**

###### Decisions and discoveries
- [[Winter Shipping]] occupies two buildings straddling the [[Credo City]]/[[Startown|Star Town]] fence, with an apparent internal passage between them — a man was seen entering one side and emerging on the other
- The Credo-side office is closed and redirects callers to Star Town. Security on the premises is unsophisticated and the crew believe they could defeat the alarms
- Winter Shipping's networked systems hold almost nothing. The crew infer a second, unconnected system holding the real records — an inference, not confirmed
- Winter Shipping runs a launch from the Star Town side to [[Regina Highport]], and some of those runs take hours longer than the journey should. Unexplained
- The crew sold their [[Yori]] cargo — 3 tons of crystals and gems — to Winter Shipping for Cr72,000 against a Cr54,000 outlay, using the sale as cover and a way to observe the company. **Olso** is the contact collecting it
- [[Pallod Norrim]] has stopped taking the crew's calls entirely. Nothing was learned about the [[Dancado]] prisoner through him
- [[Detective Odoy]] gave a wholly different account of the case from Pallod's. **Everything below is Odoy's claim and none of it is independently verified:**
	- [[Ven Yasha]], not [[Darrian Jak]], killed [[Vlen Backett]]
	- Vlen was a **courier for Ven Yasha**
	- The [[Zhodani]] spy story is false. The murder it refers to is Vlen's, and the spy framing exists to justify the [[Imperium|Imperial]] police takeover of the inquiry
	- The Imperial force aboard the [[Alahir]] is hunting Ven Yasha, and Imperial security are all over [[Regina]]
	- Both Dancado attackers — the dead man and the prisoner — have been taken by that Imperial force. The prisoner is no longer in POC custody
	- The red-haired man is a fixer named **Harlen**, connected to Winter Shipping
	- Odoy has no explanation for [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s murder
- Two independent sources now attach [[Ven Yasha]] to the case: the Dancado prisoner named him under questioning, and Odoy's whole investigation is built on him
- **Unresolved identity:** Pallod's arrested suspect [[Darrian Jak]] and Odoy's fixer Harlen are both short red-haired men tied to this murder. Whether they are the same man, an alias, or two people is not established
- Harlen's link to Winter Shipping ties the crew's own recon to Odoy's account: the same company took Vlen's last call, employs a fixer matching the third man, and runs unexplained flights to the Highport
- Odoy's own agency, motives and reason for approaching the crew remain unrecorded

###### End position
- The crew are at [[The Flotsam]] on the night of 195-1105, having just heard out [[Detective Odoy]]
- The POC's official case (Jak did it, case closed) and Odoy's account (Yasha did it, the Imperials are hunting Yasha, the spy story is a cover) are in direct conflict. The crew have no way yet to test either
- [[Pallod Norrim]] is unreachable; both Dancado attackers are beyond the crew's reach in Imperial hands
- [[Winter Shipping]] is now the strongest live lead: Vlen's last call, Harlen's employer, an internal fence crossing, a soft alarm system and unexplained Highport launches
- The crew are up Cr18,000 on the [[Yori]] cargo, with the [[Event Horizon]]'s 3-ton hold empty pending collection by Olso
- [[Kostas Backett]] remains injured at STR 3, DEX 9, END 1
- [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s request that the Event Horizon back [[Ujinka's Research]] is still unanswered

- **Find out more about the [[Alahir]]** — its captain, its crew, and the Imperial force Odoy says it carries
- **Try to meet [[Darrian Jak]] in prison** — test Pallod's case and settle whether Jak is Harlen
