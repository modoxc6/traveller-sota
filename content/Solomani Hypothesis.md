[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

The theory that **Humaniti originally evolved on the planet of Terra**, home of the Solomani. According to it, [[The Ancients]] transplanted primitive humans to other worlds across the galaxy — most notably the homeworlds of the Vilani and the [[Zhodani]].

Genetic evidence has largely confirmed it, though some scientists and religious groups advocate other origins for Humaniti, such as parallel evolution or divine intervention.

###### Why it's in the file
Its confirmation is what gave rise to the [[Brotherhood of Humaniti]] — and the Brotherhood's doctrine goes further, holding that the Ancients destroyed an ur-race of 'true Humaniti' when they split the species into tribes.

So the Ancients didn't merely leave ruins in the [[Spinward Marches]]. They made the people currently arguing over those ruins.
