[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A [[Vargr]] religious sect who believe **Vargr are the chosen heirs of [[The Ancients]]**, and therefore the rightful rulers of the galaxy.

At its height some 200 years ago; today more of a fringe cult.

###### Why it's in the file
A movement whose entire theology rests on Ancients inheritance is, on the face of it, exactly the kind of group with a stake in who gets to Ancients sites first — and [[Ujinka's Research]] was about locating them. Nothing connects the Church to the killings. It's noted because the pieces sit next to each other, not because anyone has drawn a line.
