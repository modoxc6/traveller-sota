The party's ship. A **Lab Ship**. Owned by [[Graxius StClair]] — the other characters' Ship Shares, [[Kostas Backett]]'s among them, contributed a little towards her.

Carries a Pinnace, the [[Implacable]], which belongs to [[Tyrus Cassius]].

She belongs to StClair. [[Tyrus Cassius]] owns the Implacable and nothing more of her.

This is the ship [[Kostas Backett]] talked his way aboard to find Cassius and demand a job.

**Open decision:** [[Iaros Ujinka]] has asked whether the party will put the Event Horizon behind [[Ujinka's Research]], in the role [[Vlen Backett]] was going to fill. No answer given yet. She's a Lab Ship, which makes her the right hull for it.
