#traveller #sota #location
[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

High Tech Level, high population, **almost as influential as [[Regina]]**. A major crossroads for trade and Imperial naval operations — the [[213th Fleet]] is headquartered here.

Visitors are cautioned that local customs and cultures change over short periods, and that the pace of life is uncomfortable to many. Per the Efate tourist board: live fast, die young, get resurrected by our advanced medical technology.

[[Uakye]] is closely associated with Efate, and resents it.
