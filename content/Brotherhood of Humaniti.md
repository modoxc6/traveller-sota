[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A quasi-religious group that arose following confirmation of the **Solomani Hypothesis** — that all Humaniti stems from a single source.

- Believes all humans should come together as one species, united under a common government
- **Any government or institution that opposes such radical unity is an enemy of the species and must be destroyed**
- Since its inception it has moved towards mysticism, attributing increasingly unlikely supernatural powers to **'true Humaniti'** — the ur-race destroyed when [[The Ancients]] divided Humaniti into different 'tribes'

###### Why this is in the file
This is the second movement on record whose theology turns on Ancients inheritance. The [[Church of the Chosen Ones]] holds that the [[Vargr]] are the Ancients' heirs; the Brotherhood holds that the Ancients *broke* humanity and that a lost true version of it had powers worth recovering.

Both would have an interest in [[Ujinka's Research]] — a method of locating Ancients sites. The Brotherhood is the one of the two that has doctrine explicitly licensing the destruction of institutions that stand in its way, and a university department is an institution.

Nothing connects the Brotherhood to either killing. Noted as a candidate, not a conclusion.
