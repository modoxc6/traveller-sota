[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

**Class A starport**, one of the largest in the entire [[Spinward Marches]]. It sees more traffic in a day than some lesser systems see in a decade.

Over 80% of the cargo passing through [[Regina]] is destined for other worlds in the sector, so most of the port is given over to cargo handling and warehousing.

Located in geostationary orbit over the Regina Downport and [[Startown]], which sits halfway between the cities of [[Credo City|Credo]] and Atora.

Presumably where the [[Event Horizon]] is berthed.

The [[Alahir]] reportedly arrived here several weeks before the party's investigation. It is registered to the Scout Service but is not a standard scout design.
