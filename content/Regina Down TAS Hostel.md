[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

The [[Travellers' Aid Society]] hostel in [[Credo City]] where [[Vlen Backett]] was staying, and where he was murdered.

**The party are staying here now.** [[Zena Antonia]] works the reception desk.

- Mail reached him here, care of the hostel — including [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s letter
- Its security camera caught the [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]]
- The [[Smashed Comms]] came from his room here
- Ozy worked on the smashed comms here and recovered the last-dialled number — [[Winter Shipping]] — immediately before the device became unusable
- The receptionist on duty the night of the murder has been traced and interviewed — no use. [[Zena Antonia]] works the desk on other shifts
