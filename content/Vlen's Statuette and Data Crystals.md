[[Secrets of the Ancients]]

**Recovered on 196-1105.** A **statuette** and a set of **data disks**, taken off [[Harlen]]'s desk aboard the [[Winter Shipping Station|hidden base]] by [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] in the last thirty seconds before it self-destructed. Harlen was sitting right there with his eyes closed and was not interfered with.

Originally volunteered by [[Exceedingly Discreet]] in the small hours of 196-1105, unprompted — "it would be amiss if I didn't mention that, seeing as you are his nephew" — as possessions of [[Vlen Backett]]'s that [[Harlen]] was holding.

###### Are they Vlen's?
**Presumed so, on strong circumstantial grounds, but never confirmed in the fiction.** What lines up:
- Exceedingly Discreet named a **statuette and data crystals** in Harlen's hands, and a statuette and data disks are exactly what was on his desk
- They were not part of the estate the [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] are holding
- Nobody at the table heard Harlen or anyone else say whose they were

The wording also drifted — "crystals" from Exceedingly Discreet, "disks" as described on the desk. Probably the same objects.

###### The statuette
[[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] recognised it as **the same sort of thing that was in [[Iaros Ujinka|Professor Ujinka]]'s house** — his Recon roll was poor and he has no relevant expertise, so this is a layman's resemblance and no more. If it holds, it makes this the **first physical object tying [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]], [[Ujinka's Research]] and [[Ven Yasha]]'s organisation together**, and very likely an [[The Ancients|Ancient]] artefact.

Vlen was funding Ujinka's work. Ven Yasha had Ujinka killed for reasons his own records-keeper could not explain. This object sat on the desk of the man who ran Yasha's company.

###### The data disks
Contents unknown. **Nobody has examined either object.**

###### Open questions
- What the statuette actually is, and whether it is [[The Ancients|Ancient]]
- What is on the disks — and whether it overlaps with the [[Ujinka's Research|material Ozy already recovered from Ujinka]]
- Why they were on [[Harlen]]'s desk rather than with [[Ven Yasha]]
- Whether reading the disks is safe, given what happened to everyone else who knew what Ujinka was working on
