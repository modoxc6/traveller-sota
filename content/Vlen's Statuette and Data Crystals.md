[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

Possessions of [[Vlen Backett]]'s said to be **in [[Harlen]]'s hands**: a **statuette** and **data crystals**.

Volunteered by [[Exceedingly Discreet]] in the small hours of 196-1105, unprompted — "it would be amiss if I didn't mention that, seeing as you are his nephew". Unverified, but it is the first concrete property of Vlen's traced to anyone connected with his murder, and it is not part of the estate the [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] are holding.

Nothing is known about what the statuette is or what the crystals hold. Given [[Ujinka's Research]] and [[Vlen Backett|Vlen]]'s funding of it, the crew are likely to read the statuette as an [[The Ancients|Ancient]] artefact — that is a guess, and nobody has said so.

Recovering them is a stated objective for the raid on the [[Winter Shipping Station]].
