[[Vlen Backett]] was murdered in [[Credo City]]. From the coroner's report:

- **Cause of death:** low-velocity gunshot wound to the heart, delivered at close range
- **Defensive wounds:** none
- **Blood test:** 200 ng/mL of the narcotic [[Tusal]] — at that level he would barely have been able to stand, let alone fight back. That's in the "very strong dose" band: pain receptors dulled, heading toward unconsciousness
- **Augmentations:** none

Drugged first, then shot through the heart from close range, with no attempt to defend himself. That reads as an execution rather than a fight or a robbery gone wrong — *inference, not stated in the report*.

Low-velocity and close range also suggests a quiet weapon and someone who got near enough to use it — worth knowing whether Vlen let them get that close.

###### What the investigation has since established
- Vlen arrived on [[Regina]] from [[Rhylanor]] three days before he died
- He was staying at the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]], and their camera caught the [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]] — two faces, and a third who kept off camera but had red hair
- He was on Regina to meet [[Iaros Ujinka]], a historian he already knew — a meeting arranged before he arrived, and one he never made
- **[[Iaros Ujinka]] has since been murdered too**, at [[Dancado]], minutes after explaining the same research to the party — and the party killed one of the two intruders and captured the other, both men from the hostel footage, tying the attacks to one crew
- The meeting was to agree **funding and a ship** for [[Ujinka's Research]] into [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]] sites. The first credible motive: he was about to put money and a hull behind an Ancients expedition
- [[Ven Yasha]]'s name kept cropping up in connection with Vlen
- The third carried [[The Device]], which seems to have caused electrical interference
- [[Darrian Jak]], a local dealer, sold a lot of [[Tusal]] the night Vlen died. [[Pallod Norrim]] now claims the short, red-haired Jak killed Vlen, but the crew have not independently verified that conclusion
- His [[Smashed Comms]] yielded one last number before becoming unusable: [[Winter Shipping]]
- Admiral Cassius's network reports that an Imperial police force has taken over a murder inquiry as a state-security matter, with the victim rumoured to be a [[Zhodani]] spy. Whether that inquiry and victim are Vlen's is unconfirmed

Running state in [[Investigation Status]].
