An **illegal narcotic**, usually sold as tablets, though some prefer it in its less processed, undried form as a wad to be chewed. Derived from a seaweed.

Origin unknown, but believed to have been imported from the **[[Vargr]] extents** about a century ago. Now grown on various worlds along the **[[Corridor]]** sector.

###### Effects
- A dose produces mild euphoria and intoxication
- **A very strong dose dulls pain receptors and causes unconsciousness**
- Users stay functional on a low dose, so many addicts chew six or seven tablets a day to hold a constant drugged haze

###### Who uses it
Especially associated with menial jobs and the unemployed — an otherwise dull life of drudgery made pleasant. Long-term addiction brings respiratory and digestive problems and a high incidence of stomach cancer.

###### Bearing on the murder
[[Vlen Backett]] had **200 ng/mL** in his blood when he died — per the coroner, enough that he would barely have been able to stand, let alone fight back. That is squarely the "very strong dose" band: pain receptors dulled, heading for unconsciousness.

Two things that don't fit a recreational user:
- The drug's user base is menial labourers and the unemployed holding a *low* functional dose. Vlen was neither, and his dose was nowhere near low.
- A man that sedated doesn't wander into a very strong dose by accident and then happen to be shot through the heart at close range with no defensive wounds.

It reads as a dose given to him to make the killing easy — *inference, not established*. See [[Vlen Backett's Murder]].

[[Darrian Jak]] sold a lot of Tusal in [[Credo City]] the night Vlen died — the party's best lead on where this batch came from.

###### Loose thread
Tusal is believed to have come out of the Vargr extents. Vlen was a man that [[Vargr]] crossed hundreds of parsecs to consult. Probably nothing — the drug's been in Imperial space a century and is grown locally — but it's noted.
