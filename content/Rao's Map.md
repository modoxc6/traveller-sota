A **moth-eaten map** from [[Rao]]'s paperwork, on which she marked **roughly where she dropped [[Vlen Backett]] off** in the [[Alell]] jungle, years ago. She collected him a week later. Bought by [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] for Cr100 in [[Session 2026-10-18]]. "It's been a while, though."

**Kostas has shown it to no one** at the homecoming. He doesn't trust anyone there.

Kostas thinks the mark is **Vlen's lodge in the woods**. [[Nairal Backett]] says the lodge exists but the family don't know where it is. See [[Vlen's Hidden Fortune]]. *The link between the drop-off point and the lodge is Kostas's guess.*
