**The night before Vlen's funeral, someone fired a rifle at [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]** as the crew walked from the village hall to [[Vlen's House]] in [[Vaca Goya]] ([[Session 2026-10-25]]). The shot whizzed past his head. Kostas took [[Tyrus Cassius|the Admiral]] to the ground.

- **A shadowy figure ran along the tree line**, seen by the Admiral and [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]]. Too dark to identify
- **They ran like someone panicking**, not someone at home in the dark. **And they missed**
- **A shell casing** at the firing point, found by Kostas's light: fancy, good quality, **from a hunting rifle**
- **Guns are illegal on [[Alell]]**

###### Suspect
- **[[Hans Backett]]**, shown the casing: a high-quality hunting rifle, illegal. Anyone round here with a gun like that? **"That prick mummy's boy." [[Bard Jocell]]**, idiot enough to bring a gun down here
- The Admiral reckoned "the lady's son" too
- **[[Marisa Jocell]] and Bard had left the hall before everyone else**
- *Unproven. Hans is himself a hunter, had just been turned down by Ozy over the will, and warned him "the jungles around Alell are quite dangerous". Nobody at the table suggested Hans; noted here only because he's the one naming the suspect.*

Reported to [[Porter Sajan]]. The crew chose to let Bard sweat and deal with it in the morning.
