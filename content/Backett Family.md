The family of [[Vlen Backett]] and [[Kostas Backett]], based in [[Vaca Goya]] on [[Alell]]. Vlen's closest living kin still on the planet.

- **Heads of the family:** [[Tomas Backett]], a local merchant, and his ~~cousin~~ **brother** (corrected at the table) [[Hans Backett]], a hunter and trapper
- [[Gunter Backett]], 15, Tomas's son
- [[Nairal Backett]], 92, Kostas's great-uncle and the oldest member of the family
- The [[Jocell Family]] are a branch of the Backetts, descended from a cousin who left Vaca Goya for the starport. They call the Backetts their "country bumpkin cousins"

Expected to gather for Vlen's burial.

*Source: Tel's pre-session handout on [[Alell]], mostly what [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]'s parents have told him.*

See the [Alell Family Tree](https://modoxc6.github.io/personal/alell-family-tree/) for the whole family and everyone else at the homecoming, on one page.
