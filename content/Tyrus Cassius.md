**Admiral Tyrus Cassius** — player character. Owns the Pinnace [[Implacable]], carried by the [[Event Horizon]].

*Originally named Cmdr Valarian; renamed to Cassius. Anything recorded elsewhere under the old name refers to him.*

Naval officer [[Kostas Backett]] served under. Led a mutiny that failed; Kostas backed it and was punished for it, Cassius apparently was not — he's since retired as an Admiral. Kostas talked his way aboard the [[Event Horizon]] to find him and demand a job.

[[Aylin Boro]], a retired admiral and one of Kostas' contacts, hates him.

###### Appearance
![[cassius.png|250]]

Older man, greying dark hair, waxed moustache. Dark naval dress uniform — heavy gold braid at the cuffs, epaulettes, aiguillette, medal ribbons and orders at the breast — with a long greatcoat worn over the shoulders and his cap under one arm. Dressed for ceremony, not for work.

###### Notes
- After the attack at [[Dancado]], Cassius began tying up the unconscious intruder while the rest of the crew tried to restore the autodoc and retrieve [[Iaros Ujinka]].
- Cassius reported the Dancado attack to Chief Inspector [[Pallod Norrim]].
- He used his network to build influence on Regina beyond Pallod and learned about the [[Alahir]], its captain's repeated meetings with Pallod, and offworlders reportedly operating in POC uniforms.
- His network also reported that an Imperial police force had taken over a murder inquiry as state security, with the dead man rumoured to have been a Zhodani spy. The claims remain unverified.
