[[Detective Odoy]] met the party at [[The Flotsam]] at **10pm on 195-1105**. **The meeting happened** — see [[Detective Odoy]] for the full account he gave.

He is investigating [[Ven Yasha]], and told the crew that Yasha killed [[Vlen Backett]], that Vlen was Yasha's courier, that the [[Zhodani]] spy story is a cover for an [[Imperium|Imperial]] takeover of the inquiry, that the [[Alahir]]'s Imperial force is hunting Yasha and has taken both [[Dancado]] attackers, and that the red-haired man is a fixer called [[Harlen]] linked to [[Winter Shipping]]. All of it unverified.

Still unknown how he made contact, which agency he belongs to, and **what he wants from the crew** — he arranged this, they didn't.

Worth weighing against [[Being Followed]]: the party are already under [[Regina Public Order Commission]] observation, and a night meeting in a bar is not how the Commission has done business with them so far.

- The crew plan to investigate [[Winter Shipping]] first, then attend this meeting as arranged.
