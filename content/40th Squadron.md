[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

One of the best-organised [[Vargr]] bands in [[Gvurrdon]].

- Descended from a squadron of Vargr warships that survived the [[First Frontier War]] and established their own petty kingdom in the chaotic aftermath
- Controls a loose alliance of about half a dozen systems
- Individual planets have their own governments, but the 40th's **naval officers are the real power** in the empire
- Lacks the resources to maintain its full fleet without external help — in the past the **[[Zhodani]]** have propped them up to threaten the [[Imperium]]'s flank

A Vargr power bloc that answers to naval officers and has taken foreign money to menace the Imperium before.
