The subject of [[Ujinka's Research]]: [[Iaros Ujinka]] is investigating links between g-band transmissions and sites of [[The Ancients|Ancient technology]].

###### What they are — from [[Ujinka's Research|Ujinka's notes]], 196-1105
It's not a transmission so much as an **absence of one**. Standard subspace telemetry in the G-band (Ujinka's log puts it at **4.2 GHz**) suffers a sharp, recursive drop-off near Ancient sites — not solar radiation, not iron ore, but something that mimics a **low-frequency multi-phasic dampening field**. Ujinka reads it as the passive footprint of active "Monadium structures" buried deep in a world's mantle.

**The method**: map hidden sites by the communication **dead zones** they cast across a system, rather than by looking for ruins.

Where the footprint has been found:
- The [[Regina]] sub-sector — the 1102 survey, around the Boughene and Regina precursor ruins
- **[[Boughene]]**, at the metal-rich asteroid belt around Boughene IV — sensor packet degradation and a 0.03-second clock-drift on the navigation computers. A statue pulled from that belt is a **fragment of a larger anchor array**, and is now aboard the [[Event Horizon]] ([[Vlen's Statuette and Data Crystals]])

Ujinka had a **g-band sensor system** of her own. [[Vlen Backett]]'s disks contain his reminder to acquire it and scan Boughene with it. Whether that sensor system still exists, and where, is unknown.

[[Juni]] is pursuing the signals at the Ancients site on [[Beck's World]] now.
