**The local police force of [[Regina]].** [[Pallod Norrim]] is a Chief Inspector. Its Regina Office sent the party the notice of [[Vlen Backett]]'s death on 190-1105.

> [!warning] Law Level 9 — most personal weapons are banned
> POC officers are also empowered to **detain individuals or confiscate items** judged "disruptive or damaging to public order and safety". This has been used to control technologies and to restrict the movement of visitors to the moon.
>
> In practice the POC is much more lenient in the most cosmopolitan cities; visitors to less trafficked regions are advised to exercise caution. [[Dancado]] is a forested region north of [[Credo City]] — not a cosmopolitan city.
>
> The party fought a gun battle, killed one man and captured another on the Duke's private property, on a world that bans most personal weapons, under a legal system with [[Assumption of Custody]]. The POC took custody of the surviving attacker.

Holds instructions left on file by [[Vlen Backett]] naming [[Kostas Backett]] his next of kin, and handles the estates and remains of the dead. Acts in the name of the Duke, under the Public Order Act of 527 (Amended) — which it used to *require and enjoin* Kostas to travel to Regina, with a reply demanded if he can't.

Wants to speak to Kostas in person on arrival about irregularities in Vlen's affairs — see [[Irregularities in Vlen Backett's Affairs]].

The party were tailed to [[Dancado]] by a POC grav car. As [[Kostas Backett]] and [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] entered the lounge after the attack, they saw a grav car flying away and thought it bore POC colours. **The identification is unconfirmed.** See [[Being Followed]].

The officers who arrived afterward said they were the **only POC personnel in the area** and dismissed the crew's identification of the departing vehicle. Admiral Cassius's network later reported that offworlders have operated in POC uniforms since the [[Alahir]] arrived, potentially explaining the discrepancy; this remains unconfirmed.

The POC arrested [[Darrian Jak]], and [[Pallod Norrim]] told Kostas that Jak murdered Vlen. Pallod dismissed the apparent POC vehicle, the link to Ujinka's murder and the localised system failures as coincidence before ending the call quickly. The crew regard his response and the sudden closure of the case as suspicious.

Rumour says the local POC are angry that an Imperial police force has taken over a murder inquiry as a state-security matter. A further rumour identifies the victim as a [[Zhodani]] spy. Neither the takeover nor the victim's identity is confirmed.

###### Powers
[[Assumption of Custody]] — the Commission can detain suspects for up to a month without trial, and Travellers are one of the groups it sweeps as a matter of routine. The single biggest legal threat hanging over the party.
