###### Starting position
The session resumed immediately after the attack at [[Dancado]]. [[Iaros Ujinka]] had been shot through the upstairs lounge window, the power and security drone had been taken out, and the third shooter remained outside and unidentified. Of the two intruders who entered the house, one was dead and the other unconscious. [[Kostas Backett]] was badly wounded at END 0 and STR 3. A grav car believed to belong to the [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] was outside.

###### Live notes
- The power was still out, so the upstairs autodoc would not work
- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] ran to the ground-floor utility room to try to restore power
- [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] began tying up the unconscious intruder
- [[Kostas Backett]] and [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] entered the exposed upstairs lounge to retrieve Ujinka, moving carefully in case the sniper still had a line of fire
- As Kostas and StClair entered the lounge, they saw a grav car flying away. They thought it bore POC colours, but the identification was unconfirmed
- Kostas and StClair got Ujinka to the autodoc, but it was too late to revive her
- StClair treated Kostas's injuries and restored 1 END, bringing him to END 1
- Ozy began searching Ujinka's library for notes relevant to her research
- Admiral Cassius began calling Chief Inspector [[Pallod Norrim]] to report the situation
- The crew planned to search the other rooms before the authorities arrived
- With the authorities almost at Dancado, the crew began bringing the unconscious intruder around so they could question him first
- The questioning produced little, but the intruder mentioned [[Ven Yasha]]. What connection Yasha has to the attack was not established
- The arriving officers said they were the only [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] personnel in the area and believed the crew had mistaken the identity of the grav car seen leaving
- After dealing with the authorities at Dancado, the crew headed back to the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]]
- Ozy recovered data from Ujinka's house concerning [[Ujinka's Research|her Ancients research]], [[Vlen Backett|Uncle Vlen]], and the [[G-Band Transmissions|G-band work]]. The GM gave no specific contents yet, but the data may become useful later
- The crew decided to remain at the hostel and try to repair [[Smashed Comms|Vlen's smashed comms device]]
- Kostas tried to use being shot to win sympathy from [[Zena Antonia]], but it did not work
- Ozy made some progress repairing Vlen's smashed comms, but completing the repair will take several attempts
- The crew returned to the commercial-district alleys to find [[Darrian Jak]], but learned that the police had apparently picked him up earlier that day
- [[Pallod Norrim]] called Kostas and said the POC had arrested [[Darrian Jak]] for [[Vlen Backett|Vlen's]] murder. Pallod confirmed that Jak is a short redhead
- Kostas asked Pallod about the suspected POC grav car, the connection to Ujinka's shooting, and the attackers' apparent ability to disable systems in a localised area. Pallod dismissed everything as coincidence and nothing to worry about, then quickly ended the call. Kostas found this very suspicious
- Kostas left a message with Pallod's secretary thanking him for closing the investigation and asking for an opportunity to face his uncle's alleged killer
- The crew went to bed. The following day, they were due to meet [[Detective Odoy]] that night
- During the day, Ozy continued working on the smashed comms, Kostas researched Vlen's unexplained age, and [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] used his network to build influence on Regina beyond Pallod
- Before the smashed comms became fully damaged, Ozy recovered the last number called from the device. It belongs to [[Winter Shipping]], a logistics company operating in both [[Credo City]] and [[Startown|Star Town]]
- Kostas found nothing new about Vlen's true age
- Admiral Cassius's network reported that a ship called the [[Alahir]] arrived at [[Regina Highport]] several weeks ago. It is registered to the Scout Service but is not a standard scout design
- The Alahir's captain reportedly held numerous meetings with [[Pallod Norrim]], and offworlders have reportedly been seen operating in POC uniforms since the ship arrived
- The local POC are reportedly angry that an Imperial police force has taken over a murder inquiry
- Rumour says the murdered man was a [[Zhodani]] spy and that the inquiry has become a matter of state security. The source did not establish the victim's identity or confirm the rumour
- [[Startown|Star Town]] is a thin strip between [[Credo City]] and [[Atora]]
- An existing Winter Shipping advert offers to buy cargo from crews struggling with mortgage payments, salaries, or repairs, promising "Prompt service. No questions asked."

###### Decisions and discoveries
- One of the two intruders survived, mentioned [[Ven Yasha]] during questioning, and then entered POC custody
- The suspected POC grav car left immediately after the attack rather than intervening; its identity and role remain unconfirmed
- Ujinka is confirmed dead; the autodoc revival attempt was too late
- The crew chose to notify the POC through Pallod before the authorities arrived
- The surviving intruder connected the name Ven Yasha to the situation, but gave no clear explanation of his role
- The POC deny having another vehicle in the area. This leaves the departing grav car unidentified; either the crew misread its colours, it was impersonating the POC, or the officers' account is incomplete. These possibilities are unconfirmed
- Ozy now holds potentially useful research data from Ujinka's house, but its specific contents and significance remain undefined
- The crew prioritised repairing Vlen's comms while waiting for the meeting with Odoy
- Pallod now officially identifies Darrian Jak as Vlen's killer and confirms Jak is a short redhead. This is the POC's claim, not yet independently verified by the crew
- Pallod dismissed the grav car, Ujinka murder, and localised system failures as coincidences and ended the call quickly; the crew regard his response as suspicious
- The last-dialled number recovered from Vlen's comms belongs to Winter Shipping, providing a new lead to the logistics and loans company operating in both Credo City and Star Town
- Winter Shipping publicly advertises no-questions-asked cargo purchases aimed at financially distressed ship crews
- Research into Vlen's impossible age produced no new information
- The Alahir and its captain provide a new link to Pallod and to apparent offworlders operating in POC uniforms
- The offworlders in POC uniforms could explain the grav car seen leaving Dancado despite the local officers' denial that another POC unit was present; this is an inference, not confirmed
- Imperial police may have displaced the local POC from the murder investigation, while the Zhodani-spy and state-security claims remain unverified rumours

###### End position
- The crew are back at the [[Regina Down TAS Hostel]] on the day of their scheduled 10pm meeting with [[Detective Odoy]] at [[The Flotsam]]
- [[Iaros Ujinka]] is confirmed dead. The surviving intruder is in POC custody after mentioning [[Ven Yasha]] during the crew's brief questioning
- [[Pallod Norrim]] claims that short, red-haired [[Darrian Jak]] murdered Vlen and treats the case as solved, but the crew do not trust his explanation
- Ozy recovered Ujinka's research data, although its contents remain unspecified, and extracted the last-dialled number from Vlen's comms before the device became fully damaged
- The last-dialled number belongs to [[Winter Shipping]], a logistics and cargo-buying company operating in both [[Credo City]] and [[Startown|Star Town]]
- Admiral Cassius learned of the [[Alahir]], its captain's meetings with Pallod, offworlders in POC uniforms, and rumours of an Imperial police takeover involving state security and a supposed Zhodani spy
- Kostas remains injured at STR 3, DEX 9 and END 1
