###### Where the party are now — end of session, 195-1105
**At [[The Flotsam]], late on 195-1105, having just heard out [[Detective Odoy]].**

- Two irreconcilable accounts of [[Vlen Backett's Murder]] are now on the table. The POC's: [[Darrian Jak]] did it, case closed. Odoy's: [[Ven Yasha]] did it, Vlen was his courier, and the Imperials are here for Yasha. **Neither is verified**
- [[Pallod Norrim]] has stopped taking the crew's calls entirely
- Both [[Dancado]] attackers are beyond reach — Odoy says the Imperial force took them, the survivor out of POC custody
- [[Winter Shipping]] is the strongest live lead: Vlen's last call, [[Harlen]]'s employer, two buildings with an apparent passage through the Credo/Star Town fence, soft alarms, a near-empty network implying a hidden second system, and launch runs to [[Regina Highport]] that take hours too long
- The crew are Cr18,000 up on the [[Yori]] cargo, sold to Winter Shipping as cover; **[[Olso]]** collects it
- [[Iaros Ujinka]] is confirmed dead and Odoy has no explanation for her murder
- [[Kostas Backett]] remains badly injured at STR 3, DEX 9 and END 1

###### Current actions
- **Break into [[Winter Shipping]]** — the unnetworked system, the passage between the buildings, the launch records
- **Find out more about the [[Alahir]]** — its captain, its crew, the Imperial force aboard
- **Meet [[Darrian Jak]] in prison** — test Pallod's case, settle whether Jak is [[Harlen]]

Both people who knew what [[Ujinka's Research]] was about are now dead — [[Vlen Backett]] on or before 190, Ujinka today. The party are the only ones left who were told, and they were in the room when it happened.

###### Decision pending
Before her death, Ujinka asked the party to put the [[Event Horizon]] behind [[Ujinka's Research]] — the role [[Vlen Backett]] was going to fill. **No final answer was given.** The decision whether to continue her work now rests with the crew.

###### New, from the attack
- **One dead attacker and one surviving prisoner** — the survivor mentioned [[Ven Yasha]] but gave little else before entering POC custody
- **The third man is loose**, and was the one who stayed at range both times
- **Ujinka's data** — Ozy recovered material about her Ancients research, Vlen and the g-band work, but the GM has not yet specified its contents
- **Her department at the [[University of Regina]]** — the largest research facility in the Marches — still holds her colleagues and her papers
- **The autodoc, room K** — the party got Ujinka to it, but the revival attempt was too late
- **Kostas needs treatment** — StClair restored 1 END, leaving him at STR 3, DEX 9 and END 1; he remains badly injured
- Whoever sent the crew will notice they haven't reported back
- **Legal exposure — worse than it looked.** [[Regina]] is **Law Level 9**: most personal weapons are banned outright, and [[Regina Public Order Commission]] officers may detain people or confiscate items merely "disruptive or damaging to public order and safety". [[Assumption of Custody]] then allows a month's detention without trial, with Travellers swept as routine. The POC is lenient in cosmopolitan cities and *not* in less trafficked regions — and [[Dancado]] is a forested region that is **the Duke's private property**. A dead professor, a dead attacker, an unconscious captive, armed off-worlders, ducal land
