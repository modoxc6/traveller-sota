- They hold a **statuette and a set of data disks** off [[Harlen]]'s desk. **Nobody has examined either.** The statuette resembles the [[The Ancients|Ancient]] material in [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s house
- **[[Kostas Backett]] is at STR 1, DEX 2, END 1** — needs **surgery**, cannot heal on rest, and his own first aid failed. The [[Event Horizon]]'s sickbay is the fix

- [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s request that the [[Event Horizon]] back [[Ujinka's Research]] is **still unanswered**

###### The new headline question — how Harlen ran the station
He saw [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]], recognised him, looked at [[Tyrus Cassius|Cassius]]' uniform, said "This is suboptimal" and **shut his eyes**. Every screen went black and the destruct sequence ran. **No console, no keypad, no spoken command.**

Unexplained. Candidates: **psionics**, a neural implant wired into the base, or something touching [[The Ancients]]. Set it against:
- **[[The Device]]** carried by the third man at [[Dancado]], which caused electrical interference nobody could account for
- The **localised systems failures** [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] raised with [[Pallod Norrim]], who called them coincidence
- [[Smashed Comms]] — Vlen's comms failed in a way that made them unusable
- The **[[Zhodani]]**, for whom psionics are institutional, and the [[Imperium]]'s hostility to psionic talent

He also **knew the crew on sight** — Kostas by face, Cassius by uniform.

###### Superseded — where the party were in the small hours of 196-1105
**Inside [[Winter Shipping]]'s closed Credo-side office, having struck a deal with [[Exceedingly Discreet]].**

- The company's real records were a **[[Llellewyoly]] on a closed system** — unhackable, but talkative
- **[[Harlen]] ran Winter Shipping and [[Ven Yasha]] owns it.** Exceedingly Discreet wanted Harlen gone and the company for itself
- **The deal:** the company launch and passage to the base, flight path set from its console, [[Marid]] none the wiser; in return **Harlen dies** and the conversation never happened
- **[[Ven Yasha]] ordered [[Iaros Ujinka]]'s death** (per Exceedingly Discreet, who cannot explain why), and **[[Vlen Backett]] was Yasha's messenger** who "brought bad news"
- Everything above is Exceedingly Discreet's word. It refuses to discuss [[Ven Yasha]] himself at all

###### Legal exposure
[[Regina]] is **Law Level 9**: most personal weapons are banned outright, and [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] officers may detain people or confiscate items merely "disruptive or damaging to public order and safety". [[Assumption of Custody]] then allows a month's detention without trial, with Travellers swept as routine.

The tally now includes a dead professor on the Duke's private land at [[Dancado]], a break-in at a Credo business, **seven-plus dead aboard a destroyed installation**, and four armed off-worlders who have just come down in an escape capsule at a recovery point somebody presumably monitors. Whoever sent the crew will also notice they haven't reported back.
