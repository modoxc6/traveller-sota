Commonly used to refer to any of several prestigious academic institutions on [[Regina]]. Collectively, the University of Regina is the **largest and most successful higher-level education and research facility in the [[Spinward Marches]]**.

[[Iaros Ujinka]] was a professor in its Department of History — until she was murdered. Her department, her colleagues and her papers are all still there, and are the obvious next place to look for whatever she knew about [[Ujinka's Research]].
