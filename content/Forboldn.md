[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

World two parsecs from [[Regina]] in the [[Spinward Marches]]. **First leg of the [[Event Horizon]]'s route to [[Alell]]**, jump entered 196-1105, 7 days 5 hours. Has a **gas giant** for skimming fuel. Passengers and freight were available for it at Regina, but the crew chose to carry five high passengers straight through to Alell instead.

Reached without incident; the [[Event Horizon]] skimmed the gas giant and jumped on for [[Whanga]]. Nobody went down. Nothing else known.
