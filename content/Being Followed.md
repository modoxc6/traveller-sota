[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

[[Ozymandius Forthrast]] keeps getting the feeling the party is being followed — and he's right. A **[[Regina Public Order Commission]] grav car** tailed them on the way to meet [[Iaros Ujinka]] at [[Dancado]].

The same Commission that summoned [[Kostas Backett]] to [[Regina]], told him nothing about how his uncle actually died, and insisted he report to them on arrival.

During the attack, a grav car remained outside. As Kostas and [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] entered the upstairs lounge to retrieve [[Iaros Ujinka]], they saw it flying away. **They think it bore POC colours, but the identification is unconfirmed.** Its departure immediately after the attack makes its role more suspicious, but does not establish whether its occupants were involved, observing, or fleeing danger.

The officers who later arrived said they were the **only POC personnel in the area** and thought the crew had misidentified the vehicle. [[Tyrus Cassius|Admiral Cassius]] subsequently learned that offworlders have reportedly operated in POC uniforms since the [[Alahir]] arrived at [[Regina Highport]]. That could explain the apparent POC vehicle, but the connection is an inference rather than confirmed fact.
