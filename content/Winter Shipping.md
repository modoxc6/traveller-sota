[[index|Traveller Secrets of the Ancients]]

A logistics and cargo-buying company operating in both [[Credo City]] and [[Startown|Star Town]], the thin strip between Credo and [[Atora]].

The last number called from [[Vlen Backett]]'s [[Smashed Comms|smashed comms]] belongs to Winter Shipping. Ozy recovered the number immediately before the damaged device became unusable.

Its advertising targets ship crews struggling with mortgage payments, salaries or repair bills. It offers to buy their cargo with the promise: **"Prompt service. No questions asked."**

![[wintershipping.png]]

###### The premises — reconnoitred 195-1105
**Two buildings, one either side of the fence between [[Credo City]] and [[Startown|Star Town]].** The crew approached from the Credo side.

- The **Credo-side building is closed**, with a notice directing callers to the Star Town offices
- A huge brute of a man was seen entering the Credo side and emerging on the Star Town side — a crossing that is not supposed to be possible. The crew infer an **internal passage through the fence**; unconfirmed
- The **Star Town office interior is very fancy**, far smarter than its surroundings. **[[Marid]]** works reception
- [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] and [[Kostas Backett]] judged the **security system unsophisticated** — the crew believe they could shut down the alarms

###### Systems and the launch
- [[Ozymandius Forthrast|Ozy]] hacked the company's systems remotely and found **almost nothing of interest — suspiciously little**. The crew infer a second, unconnected system holding the real records; unconfirmed
- The Star Town side operates a **launch running up to [[Regina Highport]]**
- Flight records show **some of those runs take hours longer than the trip should**. Unexplained

###### Dealings with the crew
On 195-1105 the crew sold their [[Yori]] cargo — 3 tons of crystals and gems, bought for Cr54,000 — to Winter Shipping for **Cr72,000**, using the sale as cover and as a reason to watch the company work. **[[Olso]]** is the contact collecting it.

###### Connection to Harlen
[[Detective Odoy]] says the red-haired man in the case is a fixer called **[[Harlen]]**, **connected to Winter Shipping**. Odoy's account is unverified, but it puts the company at the centre of the murder as well as at the end of Vlen's last call.

###### Open questions
- Why Vlen called the company
- Whether the call concerned cargo, money, transport or something else
- Who at Winter Shipping spoke to him
- What the second system holds, and what the overlong launch runs are doing
- What [[Harlen]] does here, and whether the company is [[Ven Yasha]]'s

###### Notes
- The crew intend to investigate Winter Shipping before meeting [[Detective Odoy]] at [[The Flotsam]].
- After the Odoy meeting, the crew decided to **break in** — targets being the unnetworked system, the passage between the buildings, and the launch records.
