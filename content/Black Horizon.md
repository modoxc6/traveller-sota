[[index|Secrets of the Ancients]]

One of **two research companies chartering lab space aboard the [[Event Horizon]]**, the other being [[Helix]]. Their labs are off limits to passengers.

Won the dispute with Helix over a **joint discovery made with Helix equipment and Black Horizon research** in the first jump out of Regina — Ozy's log analysis put the discovery with them, and their contract does the rest. See [[Helix]].

**Their charter terms** (per Ozy's read): secretive, encrypt everything; **claim exclusive patent rights on any breakthrough, pay no royalties**; and **the crew may not inspect the cargo they bring aboard**. Their rep aboard is [[Kay]]. They also have their own **security detail**, with stunners.

**The specimens.** That cargo clause is how live **juvenile [[Carthus|carthuses]]** came aboard unseen. They broke containment in the third jump, [[Whanga]] → [[Alell]], and escaped into the maintenance shafts — "three, maybe four, possibly more"; Black Horizon's own count was vague. They want them back alive and will pay. A security officer of theirs went into the vents with the crew; one carthus stunned so far.

Nothing else known about what they research.
