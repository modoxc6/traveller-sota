##### Kostas Backett
38 years old. Ex-Navy (Flight), Petty Officer 3rd class — busted out after backing [[Tyrus Cassius]]'s failed mutiny. Burn scars across his upper body from a cryo accident, a cheap prosthetic arm from a boarding action gone wrong, and a glass eye bought with borrowed money. Low SOC, no connections, and just enough skills to be useful on someone else's ship.

###### Characteristics
STR 7 · DEX 10 · END 7 · INT 9 (+1 from mustering out) · EDU 8 · SOC 2

###### Current condition — [[Regina]], before the Odoy meeting
**Hit for 11 damage** (attack roll 12, Effect 2) during the attack at Dancado. Overflow taken on STR to protect DEX. He spent the rest of the fight hidden while the others stopped the two intruders — one killed, the other unconscious — with END 0 and STR 3 there was nothing else to do.

With the attack over but the sniper possibly still outside, Kostas and [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] entered the exposed lounge to retrieve [[Iaros Ujinka]]. As they entered, they saw a grav car flying away and thought it bore [[Regina Public Order Commission|POC]] colours, though they could not confirm it.

[[Graxius StClair|StClair]] treated Kostas after the fight and restored 1 END. His current values are STR 3, DEX 9 and END 1; STR remains the thin one, and the arm that came off in his piracy days took the first bite out of it.

After returning to the hostel, Kostas tried to use the gunshot wound to win sympathy from [[Zena Antonia]]. It did not work.

[[Pallod Norrim]] later told him that short, red-haired [[Darrian Jak]] had been arrested for Vlen's murder. Kostas questioned Pallod about the apparent POC grav car at Dancado, the connection between the murders and the localised systems failures; Pallod dismissed everything as coincidence and ended the call quickly. Kostas left Pallod's secretary a message asking for an opportunity to face his uncle's alleged killer.

Kostas researched [[Vlen Backett's True Age|Vlen's unexplained age]] the following day but found nothing new.

*Assumes no armour was subtracted from the 11.*

**First aid on himself:** Average (8+) Medic check, EDU, 1D rounds, restores characteristic points equal to the Effect (minimum 1), split as he likes between STR and END. Needs at least a medikit, must be started **within one minute of the injury**, and can only be successfully applied once. Kostas has no Medic skill — DM-3, EDU 8 gives +0, so he needs 11+ on 2D. A TL10 medikit makes it 10+, TL12 makes it 9+.

The autodoc upstairs at [[Dancado]] has **Medic 3** even in its basic form — vastly better odds than his own hands, if it has power and he gets to it inside the one-minute window.
