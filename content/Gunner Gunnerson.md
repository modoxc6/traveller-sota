The [[Event Horizon]]'s **gunner**. Brought along as hired muscle for the raid on the [[Winter Shipping Station]] on 196-1105, when [[Graxius StClair|StClair]] pulled out and the boarding party was down to its two worst combatants.

Arrived with the line **"did someone call for backup?"**

Rolled up as an **elite** NPC and it showed — both firefights aboard the base turned on his shooting, and the GM downgraded his **laser rifle to a laser carbine** mid-session after a single aimed shot did 35 damage and obliterated a man from the waist up.

Run at the table by [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]]' player as a second character.

###### Appearance
![[gunner gunnerson.png|250]]

Grizzled older man, close-cropped grey hair, heavy stubble, one eye ruined and scarred over. Filthy dark boiler suit patched with unit tags and stuffed with tools and pouches, sleeves rolled, work gloves at the belt. Chews a cigar throughout. Battered boots.

###### Manner
Former Marine and still fighting like one. Falls into hand signals none of the crew can read, takes point without being asked, and shoots rather than talks — no warnings, no hands up. Delivers one-liners over the bodies.

**Stealth −4.** He does not sneak.

He is also the only member of the boarding party who did anything medical: he broke off mid-firefight to bandage [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] for a point of END after Kostas' own first aid failed, and later dragged him bodily to the escape capsules.

###### Notes
- Killed both men on the base's dock level, and at least two more of the smugglers above
- Took a parting shot at the last surviving smuggler as the escape capsule launched, purely because he could
- 196-1105: took [[Kostas Backett|Kostas]] and the statuette and disks straight back to the [[Event Horizon]] from the landing site. Paid Cr4,000/month; a performance bonus was discussed, not decided. Regaling the ship with his deeds on the base — shin still sore from where the Admiral kicked him, which shook a spare clip out of his pocket. Looked the new passengers over and told the crew they were stupid
