Local drug dealer in [[Credo City]].

Word on the street is that Jak sold a lot of [[Tusal]] the night [[Vlen Backett]] died. The party's working assumption is that this is where the Tusal in Vlen's blood came from.

Frequents a couple of back alleys around the commercial district. When the crew went there to find him, they learned the police had picked him up earlier that day.

[[Pallod Norrim]] later told [[Kostas Backett]] that the POC had arrested Jak for Vlen's murder and confirmed that Jak is **short and red-haired**. That broadly matches the unseen red-haired third figure in [[Three Men at Vlen's Room]], but the crew have not independently established that Jak is the same person or that he killed Vlen.

Pallod treats the case as solved. The crew do not.

###### Jak or Harlen?
On 195-1105 [[Detective Odoy]] named the red-haired man in the case as a fixer called **[[Harlen]]**, connected to [[Winter Shipping]], and said [[Ven Yasha]] — not Jak — killed Vlen.

**Whether Jak and Harlen are the same person, an alias, or two different red-haired men is not established.** Kept as separate notes until the table settles it.

**The crew are fairly confident they are two different men, and that Jak is a fall guy** — arrested purely because he is also red-haired, giving the POC a redhead to charge and close the case with. This is the crew's inference, not proven, and it makes Pallod's arrest look like deliberate misdirection rather than incompetence.

###### Notes
- In POC custody; the crew asked for an opportunity to face him.
- The crew plan to **try to meet Jak in prison** to test Pallod's case and settle the Harlen question.
