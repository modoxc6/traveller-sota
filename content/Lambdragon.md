*An animal.* Large herbivore common to the [[Alell]] jungles.

Lambdragons graze slowly through the forest, eating from the treetops. Few natural predators, thanks to their size and the protection of the pack. The name comes from their perceived gentleness — but attacks are not impossible:

- Two sets of teeth: an exterior set of flat teeth for feeding on leaves, and a **retractable set of sharper teeth** used in defence and mating rituals
- **A stampede of lambdragons can flatten a tank**

They reproduce by depositing egg sacs in hollows in trees. The larval lambdragon feeds on the interior of the tree and 'hatches' when the weakened tree collapses. The passage of a large herd causes sizable **seismic tremors**, felling trees and so bringing new dragons into the herd.
