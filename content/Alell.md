World in the [[Regina]] subsector of the [[Spinward Marches]].

Homeworld of [[Vlen Backett]], who returned for a few weeks or a month or two at a time between voyages. The wider Backett family appears to be based here — and Vlen's remains are to be brought back here for burial.

See the [Alell Family Tree](https://modoxc6.github.io/personal/alell-family-tree/) for the whole family and everyone else at the homecoming, on one page.

###### The world
Originally settled by **the descendants of hotel staff**, Alell has become a rich and influential world despite its **extremely dangerous native wildlife**. The population clusters into a small number of fortified cities, though modern technology makes rural living possible.

- **Primary industry: tourism**
- Secondary exports: wood, furs, pharmaceuticals and other material harvested from the jungles covering 30% of the surface
- **Class B starport**, its original structure dating back to the First Imperium and considered an excellent example of fin-de-siècle Vilani design

A rich tourist world with lethal jungles and a pharmaceutical trade — and the man who grew up here died full of a plant-derived narcotic.

###### The system — as found, c. 219-1105
- **No gas giant and no highport.** No way to refuel by skimming
- A lot of **radiation from the system's sun**, enough to interfere with sensor scans
- In orbit, a **cargo handling station**, a **medical facility**, and a **rapid-response emergency service for rescuing people lost in the jungle**. None of them take visiting ships
- A **surface starport** ([[Alell Starport]]) that a pinnace like the [[Implacable]] can use and a lab ship can't
- **Doesn't normally have patrol ships assigned**, per Ozy's Computers check. [[Delta 2]] claims to be one anyway
- The GM's framing: "a bit of an arse-end", a toxic, poisonous jungle world used for hunting in the past, with very old First Imperium buildings. More to come when he reads out the world text
- From the chapter's arrival text ([[Session 2026-10-04]], paraphrased): Alell circles an **angry, energetic star**, so ships shelter in the planet's shadow to spare their hulls. From orbit the jungles show **gold and red**, with the lights of **walled resorts** scattered here and there. Getting here from Regina along the Spinward Main means a string of systems with no starport worth the name, refuelling from gas giants or automated hydrogen harvesters
- **No starport in space at all**, only on the surface. The Event Horizon can't dock anywhere, and Hull repairs can't be had here

###### History — from *Checking In Indefinitely: A History of Alell* (Tharsis Djar, Alell Free Press, 1101)
- On Alell, **ferocity and beauty go together**. The jungles shimmer red and gold, but touch the wrong frond with bare skin and a poison stinger lashes out. The [[Whisperhawk]] stoops on its prey from the green clouds. Even the sky is dangerous: the **auroras are bursts of radiation from the primary star**
- **Settled in the dying days of the [[First Imperium]].** Hunting the whisperhawk, the [[Maleficent|malificent]] and the "vicious glittering" [[Lambdragon]] became fashionable among nobles, who built **hunting lodges** in the most scenic spots: atop the glacial cliffs, in the heart of the [[Dranxi Jungle]], and on the savannah hunting grounds of the [[Cadgeree|cadegeree]]. Each lodge had a staff of technicians, groundskeepers and servants
- **Then the First Imperium collapsed.** When explorers from the resurgent Imperium re-contacted Alell several hundred years later, the descendants of those hotel porters and safari guides had built a civilisation around the lodges. **The lodges are now walled cities**, keeping out the jungle
- [[Vaca Goya]], the Backetts' home, has a **village porter** as one of its leading dignitaries — presumably a title that comes down from the lodge staff *(inference)*

The book calls the lambdragon "vicious"; the creature handout calls it a gentle herbivore that can still be dangerous. Kept both.

###### Culture
The hotel-staff origin still runs through the language:
- **Hos** — an inferior or servant, from the old Vilani dialect term *hos'ai*, servant or staff in the catering industry
- **Siu** — a term of respect, from the old Vilani *Siu'ch*, meaning guest or honoured visitor

A world that divides everyone into staff and guests, and got rich on it.

###### Wildlife
- [[Lambdragon]] — huge herbivore; a stampede can flatten a tank
- [[Maleficent]] — jet-black tentacled predator, possibly **not native to Alell**
- [[Whisperhawk]] — common canopy avian, stupid and prone to smothering things
- [[Cadgeree]] — winged snake that drops on prey from height; **confirmed as an Alell species** by Tel's "Monsters of Alell" handout, hunted on the savannah
