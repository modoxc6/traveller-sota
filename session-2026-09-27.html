Session date: 2026-09-27 In-fiction date: roughly 215–219-1105. The rest of the third jump, Whanga → Alell, then arrival at Alell (approximate; the table still counts jumps, not days)

Written from the session recording, which starts a few minutes in, partway through the second carthus fight. The table said nothing critical happened before that. Started late and paused at 11:30pm. StClair’s player was absent again, and StClair was used as an NPC twice.

Starting position

In the maintenance shafts of the Event Horizon hunting Black Horizon’s escaped carthuses with a Black Horizon security officer. One already stunned. Cassius down 3 END. Kostas fully recovered and carrying StClair’s stun stick.

Live notes
  • Second carthus (the recording picks up here). Kostas spotted it and aimed. Someone’s shot (12) “ripped into it”, and the GM said one more hit might kill it. The security officer has a stunner and the crew don’t. It lunged at Kostas’s “nicely new, healed face” for 16 damage, and Kostas dodged at −1 to his next round. The security officer missed with the stunner. Cassius then gave up on taking it alive: “this thing nearly took Kostas’s head off”. He aimed with his gyro-stabilised machine gun and laser sight and shredded it. The guard: “no, we need to keep them alive”
  • Ozy worked the guard for information with Persuade, which he’s training but can’t use yet. The GM turned down his pitch that it should count as on-the-job training. The guard said it was “above my pay grade”, and when asked how many more there were: “a handful”
  • Ship systems failing. A crew message reported spreading system failures near the control room. The guard’s access didn’t open the door. Kostas opened it (“in TCM we don’t just open doors, we have a conversation about it for 25 minutes”) and found two carthuses tearing into the ship’s systems, fires starting, electrical shorts
    • Ozy moved in first and hit hard with his laser pistol (3D6+3). One carthus went for someone, possibly Ozy, with a bite that would have done 10, and they dodged it. The security guard stunned one: “Damn. Good shot”. The table named him Pedro (“Pedro to Ops”). Kostas aimed and finished the other, not caring if it died: “Teamwork, Admiral”
    • Cascading failure. The GM wanted Mechanic, sensor or Computers checks. Ozy found the power cables about to overload and fixed them. Kostas had been hitting pipes with a wrench to no effect. The GM had them roll anyway: an 8 would have meant critical damage to the manoeuvre drive, −1 to all spacecraft control checks. Seven minutes to fix
  • Third fight, in the corridor. Pedro had gone on ahead, and the crew heard him scream: “found another one!” Kostas swapped his wrench for his gun. Pedro’s stun missed, and the carthus bit a huge chunk out of his shoulder for 13. He was bleeding badly. Cassius hit it and just missed Pedro. Then Kostas fired without aiming and blew it apart, which covered Pedro’s shoulder in bits of alien
    • Noises echoed around the vents. Pedro tried to patch himself up but his hands slipped in the blood. Another one attacked him and he dived prone, “crying for his mummy”. Kostas aimed and shot it (“splats against the wall”)
    • Another came round the corner. Cassius had held an action for exactly this and missed. It leapt on Kostas’s back with a boon die. Kostas dodged and Cassius used a leadership action to help, but it still did 19, 14 after armour 5. Kostas: END 0, STR 0, DEX 10, unconscious. The GM: “I just healed you up”. Ozy aimed and missed, and a stun attempt missed too. Pedro stunned it
    • Pedro called it in. StClair “appeared out of the shadows” and used the medikit Kostas was still carrying on him: rolled 8, “we’ll say it’s one”. Kostas was conscious but “not looking good”, and straight back to the medbay. The table’s running joke: “Kostas is unconscious every time we fight anyone”. “Memorial Medbay”, not yet
  • Clean-up. A Black Horizon xenobiologist arrived with officers to deal with “the fallen specimens” and put the stunned ones into secure containment. They were all tight-lipped about it (Per the GM in Session 2026-10-04, Black Horizon then incinerated the stunned ones as too dangerous.)
  • Ozy and Kay, a skill chain. First roll, Carousing: how good he was in bed. It succeeded, which gave DM+3 on the real roll, Advocate: Effect +7. What Kay told him:
    • Black Horizon were carrying cryogenically frozen embryos of these creatures and, separately, a growth serum. “Somehow the two of them met”: the embryos grew instantly, broke containment and got into the vents. That’s why the carthuses were weaker than adults: juveniles, freshly born
    • All the embryo samples have been destroyed. The growth formula has been kept
    • If the crew keep this quiet, the growth serum might be made available to them, if it would be useful. See Black Horizon’s Growth Serum
    • Ozy nudged, winked and looked under the sheets: “growth hormone, eh?” She kicked him out of the room
  • Kostas’s surgery. StClair operated with medbay DM+1, plus a boon die because he’s just done this operation on Kostas, “well acquainted with Kostas’s general biology”. Effect +3, END +3, then daily recovery. The GM: “you’ll be fine by the time you come out of jump, because I really don’t want you knackered”. Coming round, Kostas asked if the Admiral was all right. StClair: someone has got him a coffee, “but they can’t make it the same way you do”
  • Ozy went to pass on his condolences to Cassius for the substandard coffee, still naked. Cassius: “Kostas, get this pervert out of my sight”, then realised Kostas wasn’t there. Kostas, in the medbay, pulled the emergency cord because he felt the Admiral needed something
  • Arrival at Alell. Last of the three jumps. Alell has no gas giant and no highport, and the ship has one jump of fuel. The options raised: jump back to Whanga once business is done, or shuttle fuel up with the Implacable and pay for it. Left for later
  • “This is Alell patrol ship Delta 2. Prepare for inspection. Please confirm.” It showed at very long range and closed. The GM noted a lot of radiation from the system’s sun, which interfered with the first scan
    • Kostas ran a sensor check: good, but very long range makes it hard. Ozy assisted on Computers with a roll the GM called “cheating”: Alell doesn’t normally have patrol ships assigned
    • Ozy warned Black Horizon of “potential incoming authorities” (Kay gave him a pat on the bum), then Helix, who started preparing their documentation, “of which there is a lot”
    • Ozy asked Delta 2 for its security handshake, claiming sensor faults, and followed up by asking for the proper ship-to-ship protocol. A “wide boy” check (any skill) succeeded: they cut comms and armoured up. The table treated it as a false flag
  • Space combat, the first the table has fought. The GM explained it: move, attack and action phases each turn, plus reactions. Every crew member takes a role. (Sin joked that you’d get killed again; hits on the ship wounding crew is not a rule the GM stated)
    • The Event Horizon has three turrets: missile, pulse and beam, the pulse medium range and the beam long range. At very long range only missiles work, and they take four rounds to arrive. One rack of 12 missiles, all they have; restocking costs a quarter of a million. Each missile that gets through does 4D6 (heard as “46”), multiplied by the number still in flight. Sensors can knock incoming missiles down and give gunners locks. Improved sensors aboard
    • Thrust 2, which the GM called “shit thrust”. Delta 2 is faster. Nobody has shields out here, only armour. The Implacable has one fixed beam laser, usable only at dogfighting range. Flying it would split the crew, “a really good way of someone dying”, so everyone stayed aboard
    • Crew positions: Cassius captaining and piloting. Gunner Gunnerson on one laser turret and Kostas on the other. Atticus Brown, “the Greenhorn”, Gunner 2, on the missiles. Ozy on computers and sensors. No dedicated mechanic or engineer, so crew will swap roles if the ship takes damage. Kostas also built Cassius a coffee-loading station “like a Lancaster bomber”
    • Alell has no help to offer. There’s no highport and no station to call. There’s a cargo handling station in orbit, a medical facility, and a rapid-response emergency service for rescuing people lost in the jungle, but none of them take visiting ships. It’s a toxic, poisonous jungle world, used for hunting in the past, with very old First Imperium buildings. There is a starport on the surface that the Implacable can reach and the lab ship can’t. “Looks like you’re going to be on your own out here”
    • Initiative: 2D6 + Pilot (Cassius, 2) + Thrust (2), plus Cassius’s Tactics (Naval). The first roll was the wrong skill, at −4 (“you’re all flustered, Admiral”). It ended around 7, and the numbers in the recording don’t fully add up. Delta 2 came straight at them
    • Manoeuvre: run parallel/across its course and let it close. Six turns to short range, about four until it’s in pulse range
    • Decision to fire. It’s broadcasting under a false flag, it cut comms and armoured up: “I don’t think they’re coming to deliver some fresh coffee beans”. Turn 1: the Greenhorn launched three missiles, leaving 9
    • Ozy tried to identify the ship and its capabilities from sensor data. It’s too far to tell, but he picked up missile heat signatures, more than one, incoming (Rich asked for this to be scratched in Session 2026-10-04. Delta 2’s first salvo was the one Kostas detected in round 1 there.)
    • Paused here, turn 1, missiles in flight both ways. The GM will roll up Delta 2’s crew before next session
Decisions and discoveries
  • The carthus hunt is over. Four killed (one by Cassius’s machine gun, three by Kostas) and two stunned (both by Pedro). With last session’s that’s seven, three taken alive. Black Horizon wanted them all alive
  • What the carthuses were: frozen embryos that hit Black Horizon’s growth serum by accident and matured on the spot. The embryos are destroyed. Black Horizon kept the serum and is offering it to the crew for their silence. Nobody has answered yet
  • Ship damage from the carthuses was fixed by Ozy before it went critical
  • Kostas down again: END 0 / STR 0 at the time, then patched, operated on (Effect +3) and fit again by the end of the jump, per the GM
  • Pedro, Black Horizon’s security guard, lost a large piece of his shoulder
  • Delta 2 is claiming to be an Alell patrol ship. Alell doesn’t normally have patrol ships. It went silent when asked to authenticate, armoured up, closed at speed and has fired missiles. Who it really is is unknown
  • Alell system facts: no gas giant, no highport, heavy solar radiation, orbital cargo station, surface starport the lab ship can’t use. Fuel is now a problem: one jump left in the tanks
  • Rules settled: reactions (dodge) apply against the next round’s actions, and you can wait to see whether the attack hits before dodging. Armour comes off after the dodge. Damage takes END first, then STR or DEX, and two characteristics at 0 means unconscious. Space combat runs by range bands: missiles only at very long, four rounds of travel, 4D6 per missile. Initiative is 2D6 + Pilot + Thrust + Tactics (Naval). The GM’s Space Combat Cheat Sheet has the sensor-range table
End position
  • Alell system, turn 1 of a space fight with Delta 2: very long range, closing, missiles in flight both ways (three of theirs out, an unknown number of the enemy’s incoming)
  • Crew at stations: Cassius piloting, Gunnerson and Kostas on the laser turrets, Atticus on missiles, Ozy on sensors. Nobody on damage control
  • Missiles: 9 of 12 left. Restock Cr250,000
  • Fuel: one jump. No gas giant here
  • Kostas fit again, per the GM. Cassius was still down 3 END from last session, and nothing was said about healing it
  • Pedro badly injured. Carthus hunt finished, three specimens in containment. Black Horizon’s serum offer on the table
  • Cr300,000 in passage fees due on arrival and not yet collected: they haven’t made orbit
  • Harlen, Exceedingly Discreet, Detective Odoy, Vlen’s body, Boughene, Juni on Beck’s World: all untouched this session
Priorities for next session
  • Survive Delta 2. Missile defence on sensors, keep out of its better ranges, work out who it really is. Ask whether “Alell patrol” is a cover for pirates, someone after Black Horizon’s cargo, or someone after the crew
  • Put someone on damage control. There’s no mechanic aboard, so decide in advance who swaps off a station
  • Answer Kay about the growth serum: take it, refuse it, or ask what it actually does
  • Fuel: shuttle it up with the Implacable, or jump back to Whanga after
  • Once down: collect the Cr300,000, pay the month, and start on Vlen’s family and his burial
  • Black Horizon’s “appreciation” for the hunt is still unpaid. Buy stunners at the first port that sells them
  • Still open, further away now: Harlen, Exceedingly Discreet, Darrian Jak in prison, Detective Odoy, The Device, Vlen’s body, Boughene, Juni
