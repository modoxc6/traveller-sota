Secrets of the Ancients

Session 2026-10-18

18 Oct 202617 min read

traveller sota session Secrets of the Ancients

Session date: 2026-10-18 In-fiction date: the day the crew went down to Alell, shortly after c. 219-1105 (not stated). Midday at the homecoming

Written from the session recording (OBS, 21:14–23:02), transcribed with Whisper. Names are checked against the GM’s handouts and image filenames where possible; anything Whisper may have mangled is marked. All four players present, StClair’s player back after missing two sessions. Chapter 2 opens: almost no combat, a lot of NPCs, and a family funeral that turns into a fight over Vlen’s estate.

Starting position
  • Event Horizon in orbit over Alell, in the planet’s shadow. Hull 47 of 124, jump drive damaged (DM−2), missiles 0 of 12, one jump of fuel
  • Passengers delivered by the Implacable; Cr300,000 collected. The month’s mortgage and maintenance due
  • Vlen Backett’s body aboard in a cryotube, for burial on Alell
  • The friendly passenger may be joining the crew
Live notes

Aboard ship

  • The friendly passenger joins the crew. He wanted in, and while StClair was away “everyone said yes”. The GM rolled him up from the Quick Characters tables (Core Rulebook p. 92): Experienced Combatant, +2 on gun skills, +1 piloting small craft (not the lab ship). Quirk: the GM first read “in debt to criminals”, then corrected it to “has secret allies”. The GM said the quirk was out-of-game knowledge, and that he doesn’t have time to build the subplot in yet. Name rolled from tables: Zed Cole (heard on the recording as “Zed Cole”). See Zed Cole
  • Pay banter: the Admiral proposed a six-year “trial period” without pay, to keep the ship’s cash troubles quiet. The table ended on “he gets a salary”. Amount not set
  • StClair caught up on the ship damage. The crew all blame the Admiral; he says he saved them. StClair wants to dock his pay over the damage and the spent missiles
  • Recap for StClair: Khei (Black Horizon’s cat-woman rep, “well in there” with Ozy) and Rok (Helix’s hyena-man rep) fought over the joint discovery, Talia was overworked, the carthuses got loose, and Black Horizon’s Growth Serum might come in useful
  • Missiles: the table agreed to buy more eventually, and not let the Admiral fire them

Down to the starport

  • The GM read the chapter’s arrival text: the lonely run along the Spinward Main, Alell’s angry star, gold and red jungles, the walled resorts. It mentions “the corpse in the cargo hold”
  • The Implacable flew down to the Alell Starport and into the mouth of the pyramid: a gigantic artificial cavern, more temple than landing site, big enough to swallow a warship. The pinnace was sprayed with jets of chemical disinfectant under strong UV lights: biological control
  • There’s a cargo handling station in orbit, but nothing that can help the Event Horizon
  • Customs (GM, through Kostas as the local expert):
    • Blades allowed, because people need them to get through the jungle. Armour and vacc suits fine. Guns banned; firearms stay aboard the Implacable, which is safe to leave there
    • Absolutely no importing anything. Taking anything out is “somewhat frowned upon”. “Everything here is pretty much trying to kill you”
    • Protective suits for visitors not in vacc suits. The atmosphere is fine, but the plant life gives off a toxic gas that affects anyone not acclimatised. Kostas was asked whether he’s acclimatised; the player went without a suit (“raw dog”) and plans to buy a vacc suit while here
    • Misters: hand sprayers of fauna and flora repellent, issued free to visitors. The mist damages skin, hence the suits
    • Vlen’s cryotube had to go through customs to be scanned and disinfected
  • Kit: Kostas, a cutlass on his hip. StClair, a bright pink hazmat suit, a little dagger and his stealth dagger. The Admiral, a cutlass (“always”). Ozy, his fists (“Mr Lefty and Mr Righty”). Kostas produced a stun stick and lent it to Ozy. It’s the one borrowed from StClair, though Kostas claimed he “found it lying around”
  • The customs scanner glitched on Vlen’s cryotube. Screens glitched and there were confused faces. They ran it back through and it passed, put down to “some weird glitch in the system”

The flight to Vaca Goya

  • Air shuttles wait outside the starport with pilots, like taxis. One was big enough for the party and the cryotube. Shuttles carry misters too, which keep the fauna and flora off in flight
  • The pilot: Rao, name read off the window, pronounced “Rayo”. She knew the Backett resemblance, and has ferried Vlen around a few times over the years. She thought him secretive
  • Her spiel: before the First Imperium fell, Alell was where nobles came to hunt. Since its rediscovery it’s become a mix of science and conservation. Most settlements have high walls; some smaller ones use a sensor grid instead. Stick together, take a guide: “this place can eat you alive if you let it”
  • Kostas added “interesting facts” of his own. Judging by her face they were wrong, but she played along
  • Kostas told her he left to “secure his fortune, chase glory in the Navy”, and that it could have gone better
  • Rao’s story about Vlen: some while ago he chartered her to fly him out into the middle of nowhere, no questions asked. She picked him up a week later, unharmed. “You don’t go out into those jungles alone. He was mad for doing that”
  • Kostas paid her Cr100 to jog her memory. She dug out a moth-eaten map and marked roughly where she dropped him off, warning it was a while ago. See Rao’s Map
  • The Admiral told Kostas off for spending his own money with a “rich fucker” (StClair) aboard who won’t dip into his pockets. StClair: the ship goes nowhere unless he presses the button
  • The view: trees taller than skyscrapers, “very reminiscent of the Jurassic”, and flocks of whisperhawks with brilliant plumage around the shuttle. The aurora casts blood-red shadows
  • Kostas got Rao’s number for another ride

Vaca Goya

  • A small village on a hilltop, inside a ring of metal poles topped with motion-sensitive mist sprayers: Tel’s map’s green dots. Green is good. “If they ever go red, watch out.”
  • Founded on a nearby uranium deposit. The mines are largely exhausted; it’s now forestry and farming. Population about a thousand
  • The GM said the village map is AI-generated; there isn’t one in the book
  • The flyer landed on the outskirts beside four other shuttles. Kostas didn’t recognise them
  • Porter Sajan is the local magistrate and spiritual leader, waiting at the village hall
  • Kostas showed the others round: his old school, the old house. The village looks smaller than he remembers. He once jumped out of a tree there

The homecoming, at the village hall

  • Crowded, mostly villagers there for the free drinks, plus a few off-world visitors from the resorts in environmental suits. Nobody seems sad about Vlen: no tears, no wailing widows
  • Porter Sajan, tall, in ceremonial robes, with a doleful voice “as if apologising in advance”: “I presume you’ve brought the body.” Kostas introduced StClair (“very esteemed researcher”), Ozy (“high-level business executive”) and the Admiral. The Admiral announced another medal due for “blowing away pirates” and his 20% commission from the missile company
  • Sajan, privately to Kostas: tonight is Vlen’s homecoming. Tomorrow is the burial and the reading of the will. “You do have the will with you, don’t you?”
  • Kostas has no will. It was never among the effects collected on Regina. He said “Yes, definitely” first, then admitted it. Sajan had been told Vlen would have a copy with him. “There must be another copy somewhere.” He took charge of the body
  • Kostas’s parents are here. His mother, Henna Backett, went straight to the Admiral’s side (“so good to see you again”) and asked whether her boy’s coffee was up to standard. The Admiral said it had been poor lately; Kostas says only when he was unconscious and in surgery. “Shouldn’t have got shot then, should you, boy?” His father, Merrick Backett: “You look a mess, boy.” Sad news about Vlen, “although you won’t find any sad faces in there. We’re all waiting for that will reading.”
  • Kostas’s father ran down who’s here: Tomas, Hans and Nairal. Tomas’s son Gunter isn’t, “doing his own work”. The Jocells are here, and so are the Parads: “watch your step”
  • The Jocells. The GM showed images of Marisa and her “asshole of a son” Bard, who work in the starport area. Kostas introduced “my best friend, the Admiral”. Bard was impressed by the uniform and medals. Marisa wasn’t, though the Admiral said aloud that she’s hot. The Admiral’s SOC is 15; they were overawed and plainly trying to work out how to use it. Marisa still had a go at Kostas (“I remember when you were a snotty little boy”), then took Bard off for a drink. “But Mother, his medals!”
  • StClair went outside to study the flowers, sketching while snipping the ones worth stealing. Investigate, Profession and Survival rolls: he found some interesting plants. The GM will tell him privately what’s poisonous or useful. Nobody saw, because everyone was busy drinking
  • Ozy made a beeline for Tomas, “the head of the family”. Tomas is a local merchant; Alell’s main trade is exotic furs and unusual minerals and ores. Ozy pitched export business. Tomas: “Quite a few have arrived with claims of their own.” Someone is “over there wanting spaceships” (Marisa, it later turned out), and Yusul Parad wants a debt of a considerable amount repaid. Ozy offered his services as a neutral third party in bartering and legalese, and Tomas accepted (“my man”). Tomas then asked whether they’d brought the will; Ozy referred him to Kostas
  • The family tree, corrected at the table: Tomas and Hans are brothers, not cousins, and Nairal is their uncle, which makes him Kostas’s great-uncle. Kostas reckons Tomas is his uncle. Hans “looks a bit like a hunter”; Nairal looks close to death’s door
  • StClair came back in and told Marisa and Bard they looked very similar: were they husband and wife? Marisa blushed, said “this is my son”, and noted StClair was unattached. Then she asked his social standing. “Hermit.” SOC −4 (as said at the table). She raised a handkerchief to her mouth and called Bard away. StClair wiped a muddy hand on the bench
  • Yusul Parad and a bodyguard watched Kostas and the Admiral walk by, arm in arm. The table now calls the Admiral Kostas’s boyfriend
  • Nairal greeted Kostas: “Is that you, Kostas? Come here, boy.” He cried and hugged him. Kostas: “I’m just glad Uncle Tomas hasn’t done you in yet.” (Family joke. “He’s a dick”)
  • Nairal on Vlen: “I always remember being introduced to him. He was my great-uncle. Never understood it.” He didn’t look a day over 50. See Vlen Backett’s True Age
  • Nairal: Vlen was never around, always off-world. He had a lodge out in the woods (not a hunting lodge; those aren’t allowed now), and sometimes came back with off-world people who’d go to the lodge. The family don’t know where it is. Kostas thinks it’s where Rao marked her map, and showed the map to no one: he doesn’t trust anyone here
  • More shuttles landed

The claimants arrive

  • Aail of the Brotherhood of Humaniti came in dancing: green robes (hers very revealing), shaved heads, three monks behind her chanting a mantra while she spoke. Name heard as “Ail”/“Ayl”; the image file spells it Aail. “The initiate Zu, known to the secular world as Vlen Backett, was a member of our order some years ago. He fled our monastery in the dead of night and never returned. As part of his initiation he signed over all worldly goods to the Brotherhood of Humaniti. It has taken us some time to find Zu’s homeworld. We claim what is rightfully ours.” (Spelled Zu, per Rich.) The GM shared an AI video of the dance on Discord
  • The Brotherhood (GM reading): an esoteric group who believe all humanity must unite as one, and who love every human “in a distinctly uncomfortable way”: “treat every human like you want nothing more than to keep them safe forever, like a collection of butterflies”
  • The monks hugged everyone. Hans was mortified. StClair went in for a hug and was well received (“Blessed be the child”), then sat on a bench in a state of bliss. He realised he may have contaminated her with sap from the plants he’d been picking, and kept quiet. The Admiral was left queueing for his own hug
  • Then a second woman came in, shyly, after the monks had done their thing: fully grown, fully clothed, with hair, and a Southern accent. “My name is Orisa Backett. Vlen was my husband.” (Spelling per Rich.) To Kostas: “I’m your aunt. Come here, my little nephew,” with a big hug and a kiss on the cheek. She went straight to Tomas and Nairal. Tomas: “Her husband, Vlen? Rubbish.” “I tell you I am his wife. And this is my wedding certificate.” Tomas, flustered, kept glancing at Kostas
  • Kostas shouted to Tomas that he might want a word with the monks. Tomas: “You’re not coming back here and landing your problems on my lap. I’m not dealing with them”
  • Kostas paid his respects to Yusul Parad, with the Admiral. Yusul: “I hate the Backetts, but you, I know I can trust you to do the right thing. Your uncle owes me money.” How much? “Just a cool million credits.” He is restraining himself out of respect for the funeral, but if he doesn’t see his money soon there will be trouble
  • His muscle, Morgan, a “hunk of muscle” (no gun; they’re illegal). Yusul twice called the Admiral “lippy”: “He ought to watch himself, otherwise he might find himself in a bit of an accident”
  • The Admiral argued that Vlen’s debts pass with the family name to the head of the family, or to the wife nobody knew about. He doubts the wedding certificate is worth the paper, and assumes Yusul has receipts
  • Yusul, leaning in: “I know what your uncle was up to. I know who he was involved with. He was trading in ancient artefacts. Those things fetch quite a penny. If any of these artefacts might just fall into my possession, we’ll maybe see about getting this debt cleared.” He isn’t patient. Kostas promised to let him know first. “That’s why I like him, Morgan”
  • Orisa showed her wedding certificate to anyone who’d listen. The monks have paperwork showing Initiate Zu signed over all his worldly goods. Marisa Jocell: “We’re not last in line. He owes me his spaceship. I’m not losing out to these monks and this wife.” Yusul grew more riled up
  • Kostas’s parents and Porter Sajan came back out of the office. Sajan went round the room, but nothing he did calmed it down

Sajan hands it to Kostas

  • Sajan: as the person who brought the body, with friends, Kostas can act as an intermediary. He asks Kostas and the crew to resolve these claims for tomorrow, and the will. Sajan has too many duties: the Brotherhood want their own ceremony for their initiate, “some DNA thing that will take hours”. “Preposterous.” “I trust you and believe in you, Kostas.” He bowed and left without waiting for a yes
  • Kostas’s father: no vehicle today. “Things are getting a bit dangerous out there this time of day,” and there won’t be many shuttle pilots. “Best to do that tomorrow, boy.” His mother: don’t leave the Admiral alone, make sure he gets what he needs
  • The Backetts have no security: “we’re not well-to-do”. The village hall has terminals for checking the claims. Ozy wants network access to test whether they hold up
  • The GM’s reminder: the statuette from the redhead’s office on the base. The table wondered whether it hides a will, and the Admiral wanted to break it open. The GM confirmed it was scanned: nothing inside, not hollow
  • StClair, after the hug: “I have felt two things I’ve never felt before.” Euphoric, and not following any of it
  • Ended there, midday. Next week the GM promises the Southern drawl again
Decisions and discoveries
  • New crew member: Zed Cole, Experienced Combatant, small-craft pilot. Quirk: secret allies
  • Alell rules: blades yes, guns no, armour and vacc suits fine, nothing imported, nothing taken out. Plant gas means visitors wear suits. Misters are free
  • The scanner glitch on Vlen’s cryotube is noted, not explained. It may matter given The Device and the electrical interference that has followed this case (connection drawn here, not at the table)
  • Rao flew Vlen into the jungle and collected him a week later. Rao’s Map marks roughly where. Kostas has kept it to himself
  • Vlen had a lodge in the woods, location unknown to the family, visited by off-world guests. Probably the same thread as Vlen’s Hidden Fortune
  • Nairal, 92, was introduced to Vlen as a child as his great-uncle. Independent support for Vlen Backett’s True Age
  • No will. Sajan expected Kostas to bring one. The reading is tomorrow
  • Four claims on Vlen’s estate, plus the family’s. See Claims on Vlen’s Estate:
    • Brotherhood of Humaniti: he signed everything over as Initiate Zu, and has paperwork to show it
    • Orisa Backett: his wife, with a wedding certificate
    • Yusul Parad: Cr1,000,000 owed, or ancient artefacts in lieu
    • Marisa Jocell: “he owes me his spaceship”
  • Yusul says Vlen traded in ancient artefacts and that he knows who Vlen was involved with. That fits the statuette and Ujinka’s Research
  • Vlen was a Brotherhood initiate who fled. It’s the first direct link between Vlen and the Brotherhood, who already had an interest in Ancients sites
  • Family tree corrected: Tomas and Hans are brothers; Nairal is their uncle
  • Ozy is now Tomas’s “neutral third party” for the inheritance
End position
  • Midday, the village hall in Vaca Goya, Vlen’s homecoming in full swing. Burial and will reading tomorrow
  • Kostas has been made the intermediary for every claim on the estate, without being asked
  • Vlen’s body is with Porter Sajan. No will in hand
  • Rao’s Map in Kostas’s pocket, unshown. Rao’s number saved
  • StClair has a pocketful of picked plants and may have contaminated Aail. Ozy has the stun stick. Firearms are aboard the Implacable at the starport
  • No transport until tomorrow; it’s dangerous out at this time of day
  • Event Horizon still in orbit: damaged, no missiles, one jump of fuel. The month still to be paid
  • Zed Cole is crew
Priorities for next session
  • The claims, before the will reading: check each one at the village hall terminals
    • Orisa’s wedding certificate: real? when? where?
    • The Brotherhood’s signed-over-goods paperwork: is it genuine, and does it bind anything?
    • Yusul’s million: receipts, terms, and who Vlen’s artefact contacts were
    • Marisa’s “spaceship”: what ship? Vlen was going to supply one for Ujinka’s Research
  • Find the will, or establish there isn’t one. Ask Sajan who told him Vlen would carry a copy
  • The lodge: Rao’s map, Rao’s shuttle, a guide, and a week’s worth of jungle. Vacc suit for Kostas
  • The Brotherhood’s “DNA ceremony”: what are they testing for, and on whose body?
  • Is Aail all right? Find out what StClair picked
  • Pay Zed Cole: actual amount still unset
  • Still open: the month, repairs, fuel, missiles; Harlen, Exceedingly Discreet, Darrian Jak, Detective Odoy, The Device, Boughene, Juni
