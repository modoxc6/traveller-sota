traveller sota session Secrets of the Ancients

Session date: 2026-10-25 In-fiction date: the same day as Session 2026-10-18, shortly after c. 219-1105 (not stated). Midday at Vlen’s homecoming in Vaca Goya, through to nightfall. The GM said Arisa has been a widow “about a month”, five or six weeks

Written from the session recording (OBS, 21:12–23:19), transcribed with Whisper. Names are checked against the GM’s images and existing notes where possible; anything Whisper may have mangled is marked. All four players present. A talking session: the crew worked through every claim on Vlen’s estate, StClair poisoned a gangster, and someone took a shot at Kostas on the way home.

Starting position
  • Midday, the village hall in Vaca Goya, at Vlen Backett’s homecoming. Burial and will reading tomorrow
  • Porter Sajan has asked Kostas and the crew to sort out the Claims on Vlen’s Estate before then. No will in hand
  • Four claimants: the Brotherhood of Humaniti (Aail), Arisa Backett the widow, Yasul Parad’s million, and Marisa Jocell’s spaceship
  • Rao’s Map in Kostas’s pocket, unshown. Firearms aboard the Implacable at the starport
  • Event Horizon in orbit: damaged, no missiles, one jump of fuel
Live notes

Before play

  • The GM had sent an update text and an AI video of the homecoming, with Bard as a little kid with binoculars: “But Mummy, his medals!” The GM’s Discord now has a jobs list
  • The statue question again: someone asked whether the statuette could hide the will. The GM: it was scanned, not hollow, and the crew believe it’s a component of something Ancient, not a key
  • Nothing else of Vlen’s was overlooked on Regina: the broken comms, the confiscated drugs, some loose change, and his ticket from Rhylanor to Regina
  • The GM, on leaving early for the lodge: it would be very poor form to leave before the funeral rites are done. The will is discussed after the funeral. The lodge waits

StClair and the Brotherhood

  • StClair, “as ship owner”, invited the Brotherhood aboard the Event Horizon for an extended stay to tell him about their religion. They’re happy to, after the funeral, once they’ve got their stuff. He has a pamphlet, and wants to visit the Brotherhood’s headquarters
  • The table’s view of StClair: he started by joining a cult and went downhill from there

Arisa Backett: the wedding certificate

  • Kostas introduced himself. She spun round, hugged him, and kissed him on both cheeks: “Vlen told me so much about you.” She whispered that Vlen said Kostas was his favourite
  • Their story: a whirlwind romance. They met on Rhylanor and married the same day. The next day he said he had to go. She says he told her he was doing “something to do with those ancient types”. She teared up for the first time: “Such a charming gentleman.” The table got the Cat on a Hot Tin Roof reference
  • Timing: four days before the campaign’s first day. That ties to the ticket Vlen was carrying from Rhylanor to Regina
  • Kostas asked to see the certificate “to put any awkwardness to bed”. Issued from Regina, the subsector capital. It names Rhylanor, and its dates tally with Vlen’s ticket. It has an official-looking seal
  • Ozy checked it: Effect 6. “As genuine as genuine can be.” Nothing wrong with it
  • The GM on the law: if no will is found, the widow gets everything
  • Kostas handed it back (“there you go, Auntie”), told her to keep it safe and trust none of this lot except Nairal, and that the Admiral is single. She: it’s only been a month, “not long enough”
  • Ozy’s proposal: the widow has the valid claim, so everyone else’s grievances are her problem. If they’re big problems, the crew could offer to solve some of them for a fee. The GM pointed out there may still be a will

StClair and Yasul Parad

  • StClair went to “pay his respects” to Yasul, and asked why Vlen owed a million. “Because I lent it to him and he ain’t paid it back.” StClair pointed out that Vlen is dead. Yasul: from his estate. Then he lost his temper: “if people start pissing me about, I’m going to fucking start killing people.” He had Morgan see StClair out
  • On the way, StClair slipped something into Yasul’s drink (Streetwise, passed) while Yasul was looking at Morgan. Later he said it was “the Admiral’s special brew”, and that he’d used all of it
  • StClair whispered to Kostas that he’d “done a bad thing”, and hid in a staff room behind a box, eating an apple. The staff member he startled promised not to say a word
  • A drunk local stood up and shouted at Yasul: a criminal and a murderer, not welcome in “our village”. Yasul nodded to Morgan, who had a word, and the man lost his vigour quickly

Kostas and his father

  • Kostas asked Merrick Backett whether Yasul’s money is legit. Merrick: “Vlen kept to himself, boy. He was an old bastard. Never told any of us what he was up to.” Vlen was “up to stuff, and that stuff needed money”, so a loan wouldn’t surprise him, “but to go to Yasul… he’s not someone you mess around with, boy.” That’ll cause trouble

Ozy and Yasul

  • Yasul started coughing and shifting in his seat. “Morgan, what did you get me to drink?” “Your usual, sir”
  • Ozy asked whether his interest in the Ancients was about health, or some mystical cure. Yasul doesn’t care what it is: anything worth a million. “I want my fucking money.” Then he turned green and went to the toilets with Morgan
  • StClair locked them in. He hacked the door’s keypad (Electronics (computers), success) while a staff member watched. He promised to pay for any damage out of Kostas’s “big payout”. They could be heard puking. Morgan banged on the door

The Admiral and Aail

  • The crew sent the Admiral, “the one with his head screwed on most tightly”, with the promise of missiles if the inheritance comes through. Hans escaped Aail’s hugs as he arrived. Kostas announced him and fluttered his cape
  • He met her hug by kissing her knuckles with a deep bow, on his SOC. “A gentleman of great standing. You must be protected.” The three monks started their mantra
  • Her creed: all humanity must unite as one; every human loved, cherished, kept safe forever. The Admiral: “I also see that as my job”
  • The invitation: join by signing away all your earthly possessions. He declined, “poor as a church mouse”
  • They want all of Vlen’s earthly possessions loaded onto their shuttle
  • Initiate Zu signed everything over to them, then stole one of their prized artefacts, and they haven’t seen him since. The artefact is the Sceptre of Ur, their cherished relic. They worship the Ur-Human. The GM: this was months ago, and it’s not anything the crew have. Not the statuette
  • The date was “long before Rylanor”
  • The document: a copy of Vlen’s oath of membership, with his signature, which Kostas recognises, and an identity imprint. Everything looks in order
  • Asked whether she’ll contest the will, given the others Vlen owed: “Not really,” because their claim comes first
  • (Whisper heard “Ale” for Aail.)

Hans makes Ozy an offer

  • Before Ozy was called over, Hans took him aside: Ozy seems the most trustworthy of the group. Five tonnes of rare furs and other animal products, worth about Cr50,000 on the market, if Ozy slips him the will and nobody says anything more
  • Ozy wanted time to think. Hans: a one-and-done offer. Ozy turned it down. Hans: “You need to step carefully, friend. The jungles around Alell are quite dangerous”

Ozy on the Brotherhood’s paperwork

  • Advocate roll with a boon: just short. Something legal about it isn’t right, but he can’t put his finger on it. The GM: some hours with his legal books would get it
  • Later, a second Advocate roll, succeeded: under Regina law the claim wouldn’t hold up in court. A religious group’s claim to all of someone’s worldly goods has no limit: where does it start, where does it end, what’s the statute of limitations? They may have a bona fide claim, but not to everything

Ozy and Marisa Jocell

  • She was furious at being ignored all evening. (The GM found a hidden setting: everyone now has name tags.)
  • The ship: Vlen’s Far Trader, the Star Hunter. “I have the original documentation. The ship is mine.”
  • Her story, ten years ago: she works as a clerk at the starport (“one isn’t blessed to be born into money”). Vlen arrived and contacted her secretly: he was being followed and needed to hide his ship. She altered the starport records and hid it in a disused hangar. For that favour alone, he said he’d leave her the ship when he died. “And he’s dead. So it’s mine”
  • A few hours later an Imperial Scout ship came in, piloted by “this very strange officer”, Gand Holsiss (Bard supplied the name). He questioned Vlen about where he’d been and where his ship was. Vlen denied everything. They departed for the space station (unclear who “they” were)
  • The ship later went missing. She doesn’t know where it is, “otherwise I would have claimed it already”. She wouldn’t give Ozy any ID numbers or drive signatures to trace it: “I just want my ship”
  • Ozy pointed out that falsifying records is illegal. “I did keep a record, just the wrong one.” She started doubting herself. Ozy is holding that as leverage for later
  • The GM: she has the only copy of the original documentation

Gand Holsiss is the Admiral’s rival

  • The name rang a bell. It’s on the Admiral’s character sheet as his rival: a tutor he shamed at the academy by proving his theory wrong. The tutor left under a cloud and changed career. The Admiral: “that bloody tutor who turned up early in my naval career… apparently he’s got a really big grudge against me”
  • The GM: he’s the captain of the Alahir, the Imperial Intelligence ship from Regina. The Admiral missed the name four or five sessions ago
  • Spelling per Rich: Gand Holsiss. The earlier note had “Gand Hulses”, the sheet “Gan Halsis”.

The crew compare notes

  • In the kitchen. Ozy: the wife’s certificate is genuine; the Brotherhood won’t hold up in court; Marisa broke the law to hide the ship. “Zero for zero” for the other claimants
  • On Yasul: no proof he even knew Vlen, a suspiciously round number, no interest mentioned. Kostas may “kick the can down the road”: tell Yasul they’re steering the inheritance to someone friendly, who’ll pay what’s owed
  • Could Arisa be an impostor using the real wife’s name? The GM: only by finding her homeworld and checking its records
  • Morgan burst out of the toilet: “Is there a doctor?” Nobody admitted to one. He ducked back in
  • Kostas warned Arisa that if she inherits, Yasul will come for her. She’s “just so glad to meet all my new family”, and the inheritance “never crossed her mind”. Kostas has zero confidence she grasped it
  • Kostas told Morgan, through the toilet door, that they’re making sure Yasul gets his money once the dust settles
  • The table’s line if no will turns up: it goes to the wife, and the crew offer her their services against whoever comes after her, maybe passage off-world, “for at least 40 percent”. Forging a will was floated as a joke

Reporting to Porter Sajan

  • On the way into his office, Bard: “Mummy, it’s the man with all the medals!” The Admiral told him to join the Navy; Kostas agreed. Marisa: “Don’t touch my boy”
  • Told Sajan: Marisa’s got nothing, nor has the Brotherhood, the wedding seems legit, and Yasul is a shakedown of whoever comes out on top. Sajan was shocked by the wedding
  • Sajan: Vlen’s assets are whatever the will says. Without one they’re back at square one
  • Sajan had already searched Vlen’s house in Vaca Goya and found nothing. He gave them the key, to look again and stay overnight. The lodge can be searched after the funeral
  • Kostas’s parents no longer live in the village; they’re staying with the other Backetts. They’ve offered the Admiral Kostas’s old room, which they now call the spare room

Shot at

  • As they left: Marisa and Bard had already gone. Yasul and Morgan had gone too. The Brotherhood danced off chanting
  • Night, walking to Vlen’s house: a gunshot. Something whizzed past Kostas’s head. Kostas threw himself on the Admiral and took him to the ground
  • A shadowy figure ran along the tree line, seen by the Admiral and Ozy. Too dark to identify. No guns of their own to answer with
  • Ozy went to where the shot came from; too dark (Recon failed). Kostas brought a light and found a shell casing: fancy, good quality, from a hunting rifle
  • The shooter ran like someone panicking, not someone confident in the dark. And missed. The Admiral reckons “the lady’s son”
  • Reported to Porter Sajan, “another problem for his list”. He suggested showing the casing to Hans, the hunter. Ozy didn’t want to be the one, after Hans’s offer
  • Kostas took it to Hans at the Backett household. Hans: from a fancy hunting rifle, which is illegal, definitely high quality. Anyone round here with a gun like that? “That prick mummy’s boy.” Definitely him; he’d be idiot enough to bring a gun down here. (Hans’s identification of Bard, not proven.)
  • Hans asked whether they want to go after him now. They’ll let him sweat and deal with it in the morning

Vlen’s House

  • On the outskirts of Vaca Goya. Dusty, unused for a long time. A local brought provisions: bread, cheese, coffee, milk
  • Searched (Kostas dusted every surface the Admiral might sit on first). No will. Ozy found Vlen’s porn stash and flicked through it to be sure. It went to the Admiral
  • Ended there, the night before the funeral
Decisions and discoveries
  • Arisa Backett’s wedding certificate is genuine (Ozy, Effect 6). They met and married on Rhylanor the same day, four days before the campaign began, and he left the next day. If there’s no will, she gets everything. Whether she’s the woman named on it is unverified
  • Vlen told Arisa he was on Rhylanor for “something to do with those ancient types”
  • The Brotherhood of Humaniti’s claim wouldn’t hold up under Regina law, not to everything. Their paperwork is Vlen’s oath of membership with his signature and identity imprint, dated long before Rylanor
  • Vlen stole the Sceptre of Ur from the Brotherhood when he fled. They worship the Ur-Human. The crew don’t have it
  • Vlen owned a Far Trader, the Star Hunter. Ten years ago Marisa hid it in a disused hangar at the Alell Starport by falsifying records, because Vlen said he was being followed. The ship has since gone missing. She holds the only original documentation, and broke the law to earn it
  • The Imperial officer who came after Vlen ten years ago was Gand Holsiss, now captain of the Alahir, and the Admiral’s rival
  • Ten years ago is also when Vlen borrowed from Yasul Parad (connection drawn here, not at the table)
  • Yasul will take anything worth a million. No proof of the loan shown. He’s threatened to start killing people
  • Hans Backett tried to buy the will from Ozy for five tonnes of furs (about Cr50,000), and warned him about the jungle when he refused
  • Someone shot at Kostas with a high-quality, illegal hunting rifle. Hans says it was Bard Jocell; the Admiral thinks so too. Unproven. See Shot at in Vaca Goya
  • No will in Vlen’s house in Vaca Goya, searched by Sajan and then by the crew
End position
  • Night, in Vlen’s House in Vaca Goya. Funeral and will reading tomorrow
  • Still no will. By default the estate goes to Arisa
  • Yasul is poisoned and furious, and doesn’t know it was StClair (as far as anyone knows). Kostas has promised him his money once the dust settles
  • The shell casing is with the crew (where exactly not said). Dealing with Bard is set for the morning
  • The Brotherhood are coming aboard the Event Horizon after the funeral, by StClair’s invitation
  • Ozy holds Marisa’s record-falsifying as leverage
  • Rao’s Map still unshown. Event Horizon still damaged, unarmed, one jump of fuel. The month still to be paid
Priorities for next session
  • The funeral and the will reading. If no will appears, the estate goes to Arisa
  • Bard and the shot: confront him in the morning. Where’s the rifle?
  • Yasul: how to keep him off Arisa, and the crew. Does he know who poisoned him?
  • The Star Hunter: where it is now, who moved it, and whether Gand Holsiss found it
  • The Sceptre of Ur: what it is, and where Vlen put it. The lodge?
  • The lodge, after the funeral: Rao’s Map, a shuttle, a guide. Hans has warned the crew about the jungle
  • Is Arisa who she says she is? Her homeworld’s records would tell
  • The Brotherhood aboard ship: StClair’s idea. And their “DNA ceremony”
  • Still open: the month, repairs, fuel, missiles; pay for Zed Cole; Harlen, Exceedingly Discreet, Darrian Jak, Detective Odoy, The Device, Boughene, Juni
